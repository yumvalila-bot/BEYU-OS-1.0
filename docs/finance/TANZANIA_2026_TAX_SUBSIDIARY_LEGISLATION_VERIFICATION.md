## GN 158F — forest-produce single-instalment regulations

### Statutory parent law cross-check

The official OAG MIS PDF for Finance Act No. 2 of 2026 is:
https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/337/download

The PDF is a 57-page official Act Supplement to the Special Gazette of the United Republic of Tanzania No. 8, Vol. 107, dated 30 June 2026. Section 25, printed pages 29–31 (PDF pages 28–30), repeals and replaces Income Tax Act section 116A. The text provides:

- A single instalment equal to 2% of gross payment.
- Payment at the earliest applicable statutory event: receipt of all or part of payment; produce about to be transported; parting with possession, use or control; or payment of forest cess under the Local Government Finance Act.
- Forest produce includes timber, logs, mirunda, poles and natural varnish including latex, resin, sap or gums.
- An instalment payer is a person other than a corporation who sells forest resources before harvest or after harvest as forest produce.
- Gross payment is the greatest of farm-gate price, purchase price or the value determined by Tanzania Forest Service Agency.
- Farm-gate price is fair market value in the relevant local government authority's open market between independent parties.

This statutory parent text has been directly reviewed from the official OAG PDF. It is not the same thing as authenticating GN 158F.

### Provisional clause map from the reproduced GN 158F text

| Regulation | Reproduced subject | Verification / implementation issue |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Definitions, including gross payment, certificate, instalment payer and forest produce | Reconcile every defined term verbatim against the official Gazette and amended section 116A |
| 3 | Assessment on gross payment and electronic payment | The parent Act sets four alternative payment-trigger events. Regulation 3's short wording “on the sale” must not be interpreted to narrow the Act |
| 4 | Buyer may pay on behalf of payer if payer identity, address, mobile and TIN are held; buyer receives no tax credit | Verify identity/data handling, payer linkage, allocation, proof of payment and no-credit treatment |
| 5 | Certificate valid ten days; extension may be granted on an application before expiry | Determine certificate-to-consignment/batch scope and the extension workflow |
| 6 | Certificate holder need not issue fiscal receipt unless Commissioner specifies otherwise in writing | Search for subsequent Commissioner notices and reconcile with EFD/e-receipt rules |
| 7 | Certificate can prove buyer expenditure if it contains buyer name and TIN | This does not by itself establish all normal deductibility requirements |
| 8 | Transport requires certificate showing tax paid or no tax payable | Confirm acceptable digital/original formats, consignment linkage and no-tax-payable process |
| 9 | Tax officer may stop and inspect a vehicle without notice and restrain produce if tax unpaid | Review officer authority, custody, evidence, release, complaints and interaction with forest/transport enforcement |
| 10 | 100 currency points for failure to pay by due date under regulation 3 | Do not convert currency points to TZS until the current statutory point value and applicable penalty law are verified |

The reproduced GN 158F text states that it was made under section 155 of the Income Tax Act, is dated 30 June 2026 and commences on 1 July 2026. All of these details remain subject to official Gazette matching.

### Temporal law and no-double-count safeguards

- Finance Act No. 11 of 2025, section 58 inserted the earlier section 116A, which was scheduled to commence on 1 January 2026.
- Finance Act No. 2 of 2026, section 25 repealed and replaced that section with effect from 1 July 2026.
- The 2026 Act retains the 2% amount but changes the trigger events and definitions. The earlier and replacement provisions must be separate effective-dated versions.
- The 2026 regulations commence on 1 July 2026 according to the reproduced GN 158F. Transactions spanning the 1 July boundary require transaction-date and transitional-law review.
- Forest cess under the Local Government Finance Act is one of the statutory trigger events in section 116A(2)(d). Do not treat it as a second income-tax charge or automatically add it to gross payment. Its separate legal basis, payment evidence and interaction must be modelled only after specialist review.

## GN 158G — Tax Administration (General) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026

The OAG MIS official metadata record confirms GN 158G of 2026, published 30 June 2026 and commencing 1 July 2026:
https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/legislation/4648

A third-party transcription reports an amendment to regulation 58 requiring a person conducting business on social media to display a Certificate, TIN or Tax Clearance Certificate visibly on the social-media profile/account. This detail is not treated as verified operative text until the complete official GN 158G is acquired and checked.

Before any compliance workflow is designed, confirm:
- The exact amended regulation and whether the display requirement is confined to a defined class of social-media business.
- Which of Certificate, TIN or Tax Clearance Certificate satisfies the obligation and whether display of one is sufficient.
- The required placement, legibility, duration and treatment of multiple accounts.
- Privacy/security risks from publicly displaying tax identifiers or certificates.
- Penalty/enforcement provisions, any exceptions, and later amendments or notices.
