# Finance Act No. 2 of 2026 — selected tax amendment index

## Scope and source

This is a selected navigation index of tax-relevant provisions in the enacted
**Finance Act No. 2 of 2026**, published 30 June 2026 and commencing 1 July 2026.
It is not a complete legal digest or a consolidated version of the principal Acts.

Official source:
- OAG MIS record: https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/337
- Official Act PDF: https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/337/download

The index records the principal Act section, amending Finance Act section and printed
page locator. Each item is marked INDEX_ONLY_NOT_A_TAX_RULE and requires consolidation
and specialist review.

## Interpretation safeguards

1. **Do not equate a Finance Act amendment with a consolidated principal Act.** The index must be read alongside the current Income Tax Act, Tax Administration Act, VAT Act, Excise Act and Imports Control Act, including all subsequent amendments.
2. **Do not assume a blanket effective date for every operational consequence.** The Act's general commencement date is 1 July 2026; provision-specific wording, transitional clauses, later Gazette notices and regulations may affect application.
3. **Do not broaden narrow provisions.** For example, withholding VAT percentages are not the general VAT rate; the digital intermediary rule is not a blanket rule for every electronic service; and framework-agreement exemptions require the statutory/Gazette conditions.
4. **Do not automate the forest-produce instalment provision from the Act alone.** The relevant 2026 regulations, Local Government Finance Act forest cess interface, payer definition, valuation source and certificate process require review.
5. **Do not implement penalty calculations from a summary.** Transfer-pricing and framework-agreement offence provisions require the complete amended text, procedural context and specialist review.
6. **Keep Bills separate from enacted instruments.** The June 2026 Bill is retained as legislative history only; the enacted Act No. 2 of 2026 is the primary source for the amendments indexed here.

### Forest-produce instalment tax — temporal version history

The original Income Tax Act section 116A was inserted by Finance Act No. 11 of 2025,
section 58, at two percent and was expressly scheduled to commence on 1 January 2026.
Finance Act No. 2 of 2026, section 25, repealed and replaced that section. The 2026
replacement retains two percent but expands/changes the payment-trigger events and
definitions. The 2025 and 2026 texts must therefore be treated as separate effective
versions, not merged into one timeless rule.

Official 2025 source:
- OAG MIS record: https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/329
- Official Act PDF: https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/329/download

The 2026 replacement must be reconciled with the 2026 Income Tax (Single Instalment Tax on
Forest Produce) Regulations, the Local Government Finance Act forest cess interface and
any transitional facts before implementation.
