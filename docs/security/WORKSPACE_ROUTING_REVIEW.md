# Destination and role-workspace review

**Status: PARTIAL — not release-ready.** The single launcher resolves live persisted principal grants and destination scope on the server; navigation is not authority and destinations recheck their own guards. No hostname, title, menu label or query parameter grants permission. The real zero/one/multiple login evidence in `CANONICAL_ENTRY_REVIEW.md` is limited to the listed disposable identities.

A general governed role-to-workspace resolver for the requested eleven responsibilities has not been implemented. The current Trustee Command (`/os/family/trustee-command`) is a real read-only surface guarded by `familyoffice:trust.read`; appointment or job title alone does not grant access. Health contains role-labelled screens and example data, but a mounted screen and historical sector identity link do not establish a verified Health backend session or live role-specific workspace. Do not route a doctor, nurse, patient, board member or any other human to a privileged screen by job title alone.

**Owner decision required:** enterprise identity, domain and policy owners must approve persisted assignment semantics, entity/country/classification constraints, workspace catalogue and availability, conflict and multi-assignment handling, and whether an explicit chooser is required. The implementation must use existing canonical identity and authorization, not new grants; recheck the target's permission and scope; fail safely on absent/ambiguous assignments; and verify revocation and separation of duties. The entire requested role matrix requires real isolated end-to-end tests after Health federation and the appropriate workspaces are available.
