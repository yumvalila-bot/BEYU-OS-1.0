> **⚠⚠ WHY THIS IS NOT A PUBLISHABLE PRIVACY POLICY YET.**
> **1. There is no verified entity.** A privacy notice must name **who is responsible** — the legal entity (or
> a real person, if no entity exists). **`INC-01` is `BLOCKED`; no entity is verified.** Naming a company that
> does not exist, or naming one that exists but is not the controller, is a misstatement.
> **2. There is no verified product.** There is **no `/terms` or `/privacy` route in the application**, and the
> features described in the repository's fixtures (`sectors/health/src/data/*`) are **fictional**. **A privacy
> notice may not describe features, data collection or purposes that do not exist.**
> **3. The data inventory is not done.** `PRIV-02` is empty. **This document is therefore a fully-structured
> shell with every substantive field marked `[USER TO CONFIRM]`** — because writing plausible text here would
> create a false public statement about personal data.
> **4. Nothing may be claimed about consent, registration, certification, localisation, adequacy or compliance.**
> **No registration, no permit, no certification and no consent has been obtained or verified.** The notice must
> never say otherwise.
> **5. This is not the Foundation's notice, and not a family document.** Where the group processes family,
> beneficiary or trust data, that processing is governed by the trust and family framework **and** by data
> protection law — **and the family's internal documents are not a substitute for a lawful basis.**

**3.1 The language rule.** `EMP-04` §5 governs: **reconcile clause by clause; state the governing language;

## 4. Prohibited content (checklist before publication)

| # | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Naming a **company that does not exist**, or failing to identify the controller |
| 4.2 | Describing **features, data, purposes or recipients that do not exist** |
| 4.3 | A **seeded integration row** named as a recipient (`secretRef` is a reference, never a secret, and a seeded row is not a live relationship) |
| 4.4 | Any claim of **registration, notification, permit, certification, adequacy, localisation or audit** without the instrument |
| 4.5 | Any claim that **consent has been obtained** where the mechanism is unverified |
| 4.6 | **"We never share your data"** where any recipient exists; **"bank-level" / "military-grade" security**; **"100% secure"** |
| 4.7 | **"We comply with [law]"** as a bare claim — say what is done, and what the group will do |
| 4.8 | A **retention promise the system cannot perform** (`§6.2` of `PRIV-02`) |
| 4.9 | A **rights route that does not work**, or a timescale the group cannot meet |
| 4.10 | A **regulator, address, form or fee invented** for the complaints section |
| 4.11 | **Anything about the family, the trust, beneficiaries or the family office** — the family's internal arrangements are not a public privacy notice, and publishing them would breach privacy |
| 4.12 | **Health or clinical claims** without the sector analysis (`CMP-01` §5) |

## 5. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Which entity publishes the notice** — and if none exists, the interim position (counsel's advice).
2. **What data the group actually collects** (`PRIV-02`) — including whether it should stop collecting some.
3. The **lawful bases**, per purpose.
4. The **retention periods**.
5. The **DPO's identity and public contact route**.
6. The **complaint route** (internal, then the authority).
7. Whether the **AI features** touch personal data, and how they are described.
8. Whether the group is **prepared to operate the notice** — because an unoperated notice is a liability.
