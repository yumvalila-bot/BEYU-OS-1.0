> **⚠ THREE RULES.**
> **1. The competent body must exist.** A resolution of a board that has not been constituted, or of members
> of a company that has not been incorporated, is **nothing**. No template cures that.
> **2. The resolution must be of the correct type and passed by the correct majority.** Whether a matter
> requires an ordinary resolution, a special resolution, a unanimous consent, a class consent or a court step
> is a **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** question for every entry in `D27`. **The pack asserts no resolution type
> as verified.**
> **3. A recorded resolution is not the underlying act.** The resolution **authorises**; the executed
> instrument, the filing, the payment or the transfer **effects**. A workflow "APPROVED" state is neither.

## 1. Universal drafting rules for every resolution

1. **Identify the body and the company** precisely; state the date and how the meeting/resolution was held.
2. **Cite the power** for each decision — a clause of the Articles, a statutory provision, a delegation
   (with its reference), or `D27`.
3. **State the decision fully**, including amounts, parties and dates. Never "as discussed".
4. **State the reasons** for a material decision.
5. **Declare interests**: who disclosed what, who recused, and confirm the **quorum of non-conflicted
   persons**.
6. **Record the approvals relied on**: any member approval, any class consent, any Reserved Matter, any
   Protector consent (Trust matters), and any regulator approval.
7. **Record the votes**: for/against/abstained, and any dissent on request.
8. **Name the persons authorised to execute**, and **what** they are authorised to execute — with limits.
9. **List the registers and filings** the decision requires and **who is responsible**.
10. **No blank left in an executed resolution.** A resolution with a `[PLACEHOLDER]` in its operative part
    is defective and must not be relied on.

```

1. TRANSFER: from [transferor] to [transferee], [N] Class [A/B] shares, for [consideration].
2. CHECKS: ☐ transferor is the registered holder ☐ transfer complies with the Articles' transfer restriction
   ☐ pre-emption completed/waived (ref [ ]) ☐ transferee has executed the adherence deed (ref [ ])
   ☐ transferee's beneficial-ownership information obtained (ref [ ]) ☐ no prohibited transferee
   ☐ board consent requirements satisfied.
3. CONVERSION: because the shares are Class B and the transfer is not to a Permitted Transferee, the shares

```

## 8. Template TR-01 — Trustees' decision on a company matter (**a Trust document, not a company document**)

```
[TRUST NAME] — TRUSTEE DECISION ON A COMPANY-GOVERNANCE MATTER
(NB: this is a decision of the TRUSTEES under the trust deed. It is recorded in the Trust's decision
register, NOT in the company's. It is not a company resolution.)
Matter: [e.g. how to exercise the Trust's votes at a general meeting of [COMPANY] on [DATE] /
whether to consent to [variation] as a class holder / whether to subscribe for shares]
1. POWER: trust deed [clause]; D04 §7 ref [RM-xx] if a Reserved Matter.
2. INFORMATION CONSIDERED: [the company's papers, advice, valuations, the effect on the Trust Fund and on
   the Beneficiaries].
3. CONFLICT: [declared; recusal; non-conflicted quorum].
4. CONSENTS REQUIRED: ☐ none ☐ Protector (ref [ ]) ☐ Beneficiary approval (ref [ ]).
5. TRUSTEE REASONS: [why this exercise of the right serves the Beneficiaries / the purposes of the trust —
   this must be the Trustees' own reasoning, not the company's recommendation].
6. DECISION: [how the Trust will vote / consent / subscribe].
7. AUTHORITY TO ACT: [who will sign the company's consent or vote on the Trust's behalf, and under what
   authority].
8. REGISTERS: Trust decision register ☐  Trust asset register (D67) if the transaction affects Trust assets ☐
D73 interface (as a record only) ☐
SIGNED: [TRUSTEES] ______
```

**8.1 The wall.** A `TR-01` decision is **not** a company resolution, and a company resolution **cannot**
substitute for it. Where the Trust holds a right, **the Trustees must decide** — and a company document that
purports to decide for them is **void as to that matter** (`D22` §5.4).
