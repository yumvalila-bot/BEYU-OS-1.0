Title: Reserved Matters and Delegation Matrix
Version: 1.0 (draft)
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — the matrix is drafted. **It is not adopted, it is not embedded in any

Document owner: Company Secretary function (proposed)
Approving authority (proposed): the board, then the shareholders (and, where the Trust holds shares, the

Next review date: Annually, and on any capital or structural change
Classification: `CONFIDENTIAL`
Related documents: `D04` §7 (**the Trust-side list — deliberately distinct**), `D22`, `D23`, `D24`, `D25`,
`D26`, `D28`, `D29`, `D30`, `D51`, `D61`, `D72`, `D73`, `D74`
Depends on: `DAA-ARCH-005`; the adoption of the Articles and the shareholders' agreement; **`FIR` decisions
that this matrix must not pre-empt**

> **⚠ THREE RULES THAT PREVENT THE MOST COMMON FAILURE.**
> **1. This matrix is the COMPANY-side list. `D04` §7 is the TRUST-side list. They are different, they
> govern different bodies, and they are NOT interchangeable.** A matter may appear on both — and then **both
> decisions are required** (`§8`). A matrix that silently merges them is the defect this pack exists to
> prevent.
> **2. Fail-closed.** Where a threshold, cap or quorum is blank, **the more restrictive approval applies** —
> and where it is unclear which is more restrictive, **the highest authority decides**. **No one may act on
> the basis that a blank means "no limit".**
> **3. A matrix is not authority by itself.** Authority arises from the *instrument* (the Articles, the
> shareholders' agreement, a delegation, a resolution). **This document is the drafting plan for those
> instruments — not the instruments.**

## 1. How to read the matrix

### 1.1 The levels (who decides)

| Code | Decision-maker | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Members / shareholders** (in general meeting or by written resolution) | Includes a class meeting where a class right is varied (**CL**) |
| **CL** | **Class meeting** of the affected class | Required where a class right is varied (`D22` §9.3; `Q-H-01(f)`) |
| **BD** | **Board of directors** | The default corporate decision-maker |
| **BD-CC** | **Board, with a specified committee's prior recommendation/input** | The committee **recommends**; the board decides (`D26` §6.2) |
| **COM** | **Committee**, within a delegated authority | A committee **never** exceeds its delegation |
| **CEO-CFO** | **CEO/CFO jointly** (or CEO alone where stated) | Executive authority, within budget and limits |
| **MGMT** | **Management**, within policy | Operational |
| **TR** | **Trustees** (Trust-side; shown for the cross-walk only) | `D04` §7 |
| **FG** | **Foundation governing body** (independent quorum) | `D49`, `D51` |
| **—** | **Not delegable at all** (see N-1…N-9) | — |

### 1.2 The columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ref** | Matter reference |
| **Matter** | What is being decided |
| **Proposer** | Who may bring it |
| **Decision** | The level that decides |
| **Committee input** | The committee whose recommendation is required first |
| **Threshold / limit** | The value at which the level applies — **blank = fail-closed** |
| **Trust-side equivalent** | The `D04` §7 `RM-*` item, if any — **the cross-walk (§8)** |
| **Record** | The instrument that must be produced (`D28`, `D73`, `D74`) |

### 1.3 The fail-closed rule in practice

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| The threshold is blank | The **highest** level listed for that matter decides |
| Two levels could apply (e.g. an amount crosses a limit) | **Both** are required |
| A committee's delegation is unclear | The **board** decides |
| A matter is not listed at all | The **board** decides; and the matrix is **amended** at the next review — **the gap is recorded, not exploited** |
| The Trust and the company both list the matter | **Both** decisions are required (`§8`) |

## 3. Non-delegable matters (N-1…N-9)

**These may never be delegated, in any circumstances, to a committee, an executive, an agent, an adviser, the
Family Office, software or AI:**

| Ref | Non-delegable matter |
|---|---|
| **N-1** | Amendments to the Articles, the shareholders' agreement or the trust instrument |
| **N-2** | Approval of the annual accounts and the declaration of a distribution |
| **N-3** | The **solvency** determination supporting a distribution or a buy-back |
| **N-4** | Any transaction with a **related party** (the disinterested decision itself) |
| **N-5** | Appointment, removal or remuneration of the directors and the auditors |
| **N-6** | Approval of a **material transaction** (acquisition, disposal, change of control, winding up) |
| **N-7** | The **conflict determinations** and recusals under `D25` §6 |
| **N-8** | Any decision affecting the **Foundation's independence** (which the Foundation's body must take itself — `D51` §4) |
| **N-9** | Any decision the **law** requires a specific organ or person to take |

**3.1 The AI rule.** AI may prepare, analyse, summarise and flag. **AI may never be a decision-maker,
a proposer of record, an approver, or a participant in a quorum** (`D02` cl. 7.4). **No resolution may
record an AI as having decided or approved anything.**

## 4. Proposal, decision and evidence

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every decision has a **proposer** and a **recorded decision** — an unwritten "we agreed" is not a decision |
| 2 | Board decisions use the `D28` templates; approvals are recorded in `D73` |
| 3 | A **committee recommendation is not a decision** (`D26` §6.2) |
| 4 | A decision **outside** the matrix, or taken by the wrong level, is **invalid** and must be **ratified or unwound** — never quietly accepted |
| 5 | Where the matter also sits on the Trust side, the **Trust decision must exist before or alongside** the company decision, and both are recorded |
| 6 | Thresholds are reviewed annually; a **blank** stays fail-closed until filled |
| 7 | Any **deviation** requires the next-higher level and a recorded reason |

## 8. Cross-walk to the Trust-side reserved matters (`D04` §7) — **the rule that prevents contradiction**

**8.1 The two lists are different and deliberately so.**

| Aspect | Trust side (`D04` §7) | Company side (this document) |
|---|---|---|
| The body | The **Trustees** | The **board** and the **shareholders** |
| Governs | The Trust Fund, the trust's administration, distributions | The company, its capital, its business |
| Source of authority | The **trust instrument** and the Trustee's fiduciary duty | The **Articles**, the **shareholders' agreement**, and company law |
| Can a company resolution authorise a Trustee act? | **No** | **No** |
| Can a Trustee's consent bind the company's board? | **No** — the board acts under its own duties | **No** |

**8.2 The cross-walk table.**

| `D04` §7 item | Company-side items that touch the same subject | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **RM-01** Amendment of the trust instrument | L-01 (Articles amendment) | Two separate instruments; each follows its own process |
| **RM-02** Change to beneficiary classes | C-03, C-04, L-08 | A class-rights change may require the class meeting **and** a Trustee decision where the Trust holds the affected class |
| **RM-03** Trust termination / final distribution | C-11, C-12 | A Trust termination may require company action (e.g. a transfer of the Trust's shares, or a distribution in specie) — **each step needs its own decision** |
| **RM-04** Trustee/protector appointment/removal | H-01 (directors) | **Entirely separate.** A Trustee is not a director and vice versa |
| **RM-05, RM-06, RM-07** Trust borrowing, security, guarantees | F-04, F-05, F-06, F-11 | Where the **company** borrows with a Trust guarantee, **both** decisions are required (and `D30` §3 may add the country-holding layer) |
| **RM-08** Sale/encumbrance of a controlling interest in the Holding Body | C-05, C-06, C-13, L-04 | The **same economic act seen from two sides**: the Trustees decide for the Trust; the company decides only for itself. **Both records exist** |
| **RM-09** Related-party transaction | F-07, G-07 | The Trust's threshold and the company's threshold are **different numbers**; **both apply where the Trust is a party** |
| **RM-10** Litigation above the threshold | L-07 | Where the Trust is the litigant, RM-10; where the company is, L-07; where both, **both** |
| **RM-11** Large distribution / capital reduction of the Trust Fund | C-11 (dividend), C-07 (buy-back) | A company distribution to the Trust is a **company** decision; what the Trustees then do with it is a **Trust** decision |
| **RM-12** Change of the Trust's governing law | L-09 (company re-domiciliation) | Separate, and often interdependent — plan together, decide separately |
| **RM-13** Amendment of the trust's non-delegable/AI/conflict clauses | L-01, L-11 | Same discipline on both sides: these are the clauses that should be hard to change |
| **RM-14** Creation of a lower-tier entity or a new trust using Trust assets | L-03, L-05 | **Both**, where the funding flows from the Trust |
| **RM-15** Investment mandate / risk appetite | F-09, F-10 | Where the company invests, the board; where the Trust invests, the Trustees. **`D34` must be adopted by each body for its own assets** |
| **RM-16** Distribution policy caps | (none) | Trust-only |
| **RM-17** Waiver/release of a Trust claim | L-07, F-16 | Trust-only for Trust claims |
| **RM-18** Foundation transaction not on written arm's-length terms | F-07, G-03, G-06 | The company also requires the `D51` process; **the Foundation's own independent decision is mandatory** |
| **RM-19** Benefit to a Trustee/Protector/connected person | H-07, H-10, G-07 | Both sides record; both apply |
| **RM-20** Delegation of investment management | F-09, G-04, G-08 | The Trust's delegation is a Trust decision; the company's is a board decision with the Family Office mandate (`D31`) |

**8.3 The double-recording rule.** Where a matter appears on both sides, **two decisions are required and two
records are created.** Neither may substitute for the other, and **a document that records only one of them
is incomplete** (`D73` must show both, cross-referenced).

**8.4 The conflict rule.** Where the company's interest and the Trust's interest diverge — the classic case
being a transaction between the two — the persons wearing both hats (a Trustee who is also a director) must
**manage the conflict under `D25` §6 and `D14`**, and where the conflict cannot be managed, **the interested
person must not decide on either side.**

**8.5 Where the two lists appear to conflict.** The rule is: **the instrument governing the body whose
decision it is prevails.** A company provision cannot override the trust instrument, and vice versa. **An
apparent conflict is a drafting defect to be fixed by counsel (`P-1` in the consistency report), not a matter
to be resolved by choosing the more convenient reading.**

**No threshold is set. No resolution has been passed under this matrix. It is not embedded in any instrument,
and it is not in force.**
