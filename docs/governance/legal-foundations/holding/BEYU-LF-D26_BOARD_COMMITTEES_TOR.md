> **⚠ THE ONE RULE THAT MAKES COMMITTEES SAFE.**
> **A committee recommends; the board decides — unless `D27` expressly delegates the decision, in which case
> the committee decides only within that delegation.** A committee that believes it has decided something the
> board has not delegated is the most common governance failure in growing groups.
> **Corollary: a committee is never a way to move a decision to a smaller, friendlier room.** Where a matter
> involves a **related party**, a **conflict**, a **family member** or the **Foundation**, the committee route
> does not sanitise it — **the disinterested decision is still required (`D27` F-07, G-03, G-07).**

## 1. Common provisions applying to every committee

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Source of authority:** the board resolution constituting the committee, within `D27`. No implied powers |
| 1.2 | **Membership:** at least `[N]` members, appointed by the board; the chair is appointed by the board. **A member's term is `[N]` years, renewable** |
| 1.3 | **Independence:** each committee must have at least `[N]` independent members (`D25` §2.2); the majority must be independent where the committee exercises a delegated decision |
| 1.4 | **Quorum:** `[QUORUM]` members, of whom `[INDEPENDENT QUORUM]` must be independent. **Interested members do not count** |
| 1.5 | **Attendance:** the CEO, CFO, and internal/external advisers attend **by invitation**, and **withdraw** when the committee discusses their own position, remuneration or a matter in which they are interested |
| 1.6 | **Meetings:** at least `[N]` per year and on request; papers circulated `[N]` days in advance |
| 1.7 | **Minutes:** recorded and reported to the next board meeting — attendance, conflicts and recusals, the papers, the recommendation or decision, the dissent, and the actions |
| 1.8 | **Recommendation versus decision (the typing rule)** — see §6. Every committee action must be typed: **`RECOMMEND` (goes to the board)**, **`DECIDE` (within a delegation)**, or **`INFORM`** |
| 1.9 | **Reporting:** the committee's annual report to the board covers what it did, what it found, what it recommends, and what it could not resolve |
| 1.10 | **Advisers:** the committee may instruct advisers **within a budget** `[USER TO SUPPLY]` and following `D36` |
| 1.11 | **Records:** the committee's records are the company's records; access follows `D73`/`D74` discipline |
| 1.12 | **No AI** may be a committee member, an attendee counted for quorum, or the author of a recommendation |
| 1.13 | **The control plane** may schedule, record and track committee matters; **it creates no authority** (`D27` §5.6) |

## 2. Audit and Risk Committee (`A-01`…`A-12`)

**Purpose:** independent oversight of financial reporting, internal control, risk, compliance, audit and the
integrity of the numbers — **including the numbers the family is shown.**

| Ref | Term |
|---|---|
| A-01 | **Composition:** at least `[N]` members, all non-executive, at least one with financial expertise; the chair may not be the board chair |
| A-02 | **Financial reporting:** review the annual and interim accounts before board approval; review the accounting policies and any change; **enquire specifically into the estimates and judgments** |
| A-03 | **The accounting authority:** confirm to the board that the figures presented trace to the accounting authority; **investigate any shadow ledger, parallel record or unsourced figure** (`D35` §6.3, `FIR-018`) |
| A-04 | **External audit:** recommend the auditors' appointment/removal; oversee the audit, its scope and its fees; **meet the auditors without management present** at least annually; review the management letter and the follow-up |
| A-05 | **Internal control:** review the control environment, the segregation of duties, the two-signature rule and the reconciliation discipline (`D35` §5) |
| A-06 | **Risk:** review the risk register (`D61`) at least quarterly; **satisfy itself that the critical risks have named owners and mitigations**; escalate a risk the board is not addressing |
| A-07 | **Compliance:** review the obligation and filing calendar (`D68`) for overdue items; review regulatory correspondence; **escalate a missed filing immediately** |
| A-08 | **Related parties:** review the related-party register (`D72`) quarterly for completeness and arm's-length evidence; **require the disinterested decision record before endorsing** |
| A-09 | **Solvency and liquidity:** review the solvency indicator and the liquidity floor (`D33` §5) before any distribution or buy-back recommendation |
| A-10 | **Whistleblowing:** operate the whistleblowing route and see every report; **the committee — not the person complained about — decides the response** |
| A-11 | **Fraud and irregularity:** receive and investigate reports of fraud, misappropriation, corruption or a breach of the authority limits; **preserve evidence and do not "quietly fix"** (`D35` §3.2 P-08) |
| A-12 | **Decisions it may take itself (delegated):** instruct the auditors within the approved budget; require a management response; **escalate directly to the board and, where Trust assets are exposed, to the Trustees** |

## 3. Remuneration Committee (`R-01`…`R-08`)

| Ref | Term |
|---|---|
| R-01 | **Composition:** at least `[N]` independent members; **a person whose own remuneration is being decided must not be present** |
| R-02 | **Scope:** director and executive remuneration, bonuses, LTIPs/ESOP grants under an approved plan, benefits, severance, and any payment to a person connected with a director |
| R-03 | **Policy:** recommend the remuneration policy (principles, benchmarks, the link to performance, and the ceiling) to the board **and, where `D27` requires, to the shareholders** |
| R-04 | **Market evidence:** any benchmarking must be **from a verifiable source**, with the comparator set recorded — **never "what a friend's company pays"** |
| R-05 | **Family and connected persons:** every remuneration decision for a family member or a connected person is **typed `RECOMMEND` to a disinterested decision**, and registered (`D72`, `H-07`) |
| R-06 | **Severance:** no severance or settlement above `[SEVERANCE LIMIT]` without board (and, where applicable, shareholder) approval; **no "consultancy" arrangement used to disguise a payment** |
| R-07 | **Disclosure:** the aggregate and the individual position for directors disclosed where the law or the Articles require |
| R-08 | **Records:** the committee's reasons are minuted, so that a remuneration decision can be explained to a shareholder, an auditor or a revenue authority |

## 4. Nomination Committee (`N-01`…`N-08`)

| Ref | Term |
|---|---|
| N-01 | **Composition:** at least `[N]` members, the majority independent; the chair of the board may be a member but not in a way that lets them alone select their successor |
| N-02 | **Scope:** board composition, the skills matrix, director recruitment, succession planning (`D58`), committee membership, and the induction of new directors |
| N-03 | **Independence determinations** are made here (with reasons recorded) before appointment (`D25` §2.2) |
| N-04 | **Eligibility and screening:** disqualification checks, references, conflicts of interest, regulatory checks (`Q-H-03`) |
| N-05 | **Succession:** at least annually, review the succession position for the chair, the CEO, the CFO and the Company Secretary; **escalate a key role with no successor** (`D58`) |
| N-06 | **Committee composition** recommendations go to the board |
| N-07 | **No self-selection:** a member may not be the sole decision-maker on their own re-appointment, or on the appointment of a person connected with them |
| N-08 | **Board evaluation** is owned here (with the chair) and reported (`D25` §8.1) |

## 5. Investment / Treasury Committee (`I-01`…`I-08`) — **optional**

**Constitution note:** the pack treats this committee as **optional**. Where it is not constituted, its
functions sit with the board (**and, for Trust assets, with the Trustees — the committee cannot substitute
for them**).

| Ref | Term |
|---|---|
| I-01 | **Composition:** at least `[N]` members; at least one with investment experience; **no member may be a beneficiary of the asset being invested** unless the conflict is managed and the interests disclosed |
| I-02 | **Scope:** the investment mandate and risk appetite (`D34`), the portfolio's composition against the mandate, the treasury position, banking relationships, and the cash-liquidity position inside a **written** policy |
| I-03 | **Mandate compliance:** review the portfolio against `D34` §3 limits at least quarterly; **report every breach immediately, with the cause** |
| I-04 | **No unlisted, illiquid or novel asset class** without the process in `D34` §4 and the board's (and where Trust assets are involved, the Trustees') approval |
| I-05 | **No discretionary authority to a manager** beyond the delegated authority and the `RM-20`/`D27` F-09 limits |
| I-06 | **Reporting:** the committee's reporting must satisfy `D33`'s three number rules — **never blend owners; never blend realised with contingent; reconcile or label `UNRECONCILED`** |
| I-07 | **Related-party and adviser conflicts** are handled under `D36` and `D72`; **a conflicted manager does not decide** |
| I-08 | **Decision typing:** where `D27` delegates an investment decision, the committee decides **within the limit**; anything above goes to the board (and to the Trustees where the Trust is the investor) |

## 6. Committee records and the reporting discipline

**6.1 Record contents (every meeting).** Date and place; members present and absent; **quorum and
independence confirmation**; conflicts declared and recusals; papers tabled; the substance of the discussion;
**the typed action (`RECOMMEND`/`DECIDE`/`INFORM`)** with the authority reference; the actions with owners and
dates; the date of the next meeting.

**6.2 The typing rule (mandatory).** Every committee output carries one of:

| Type | Meaning | What must follow |
|---|---|---|
| **`RECOMMEND`** | The committee proposes | The **board** must decide, using `D28`; the recommendation is not effective until then |
| **`DECIDE`** | The committee decides **within a `D27` delegation** | The delegation reference must be cited in the minute; the board is **informed** at the next meeting |
| **`INFORM`** | The committee informs the board of oversight activity | Nothing further, unless the board asks |

**6.3 The control plane convention.** The control plane may distinguish a committee recommendation from a
board decision **visually and in the workflow**; it must not present a recommendation as a decision, and it
must not permit a committee to record a `DECIDE` without citing a delegation. **Where the software cannot
enforce this, the control plane must record it as a limitation — not paper over it.**

**6.4 Escalation.** A committee must escalate to the board (and, where Trust assets or Trustee duties are
implicated, to the Trustees; where the Foundation is implicated, to its governing body):

| # | Trigger |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud, misappropriation, a breach of the authority limits, or a shadow ledger |
| 2 | A risk that is critical and unmitigated |
| 3 | A missed filing or a regulatory default |
| 4 | A related-party transaction without an arm's-length record |
| 5 | A solvency or liquidity concern |
| 6 | A conflict of interest the committee cannot manage |
| 7 | Any matter where the committee was refused information it reasonably required |

## 7. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Which committees** are constituted (Audit and Risk is recommended regardless of size; Nomination and
   Remuneration may be combined in a small group **provided no interested person decides**).
2. **Membership** of each committee, and the independence determinations.
3. The **delegations** (what, if anything, each committee decides rather than recommends).
4. The **adviser budget** for each committee.
5. Whether the **Investment/Treasury Committee** is constituted, or the board retains the function.
6. The **whistleblowing route** and who receives a report.

   company-secretary qualification (`Q-H-03`).

5. Whether **whistleblowing** requires a particular channel or protection in the applicable law.
6. Whether the **related-party** approval can validly be delegated at all (the pack's position: the decision
   itself cannot — `D27` N-4).
7. The **conflict-recusal** rules and their interaction with the Articles.
8. Whether the operation of a committee over a **Trust matter** creates any Trustee liability issue.
