> **⚠ THE THREE TRAPS IN EVERY HOME-MADE IP ASSIGNMENT.**
> **TRAP 1 — The assignor is wrong.** An assignment signed by an entity that did not own the rights
> ("the group", "BEYU OS", "the project", or a company that had not yet been incorporated **at the time the
> work was created**) transfers **nothing**. The assignor must be **the person or entity that actually held
> the rights**.
> **TRAP 2 — The rights are not identified.** "All intellectual property" is often too vague, and it may
> miss exactly what matters: the AI model weights, the prompt library, the dataset, the design files, the
> domain, the repository history.
> **TRAP 3 — The formalities are missing.** An assignment that is not in writing, not properly signed, not
> supported by consideration, or not **recorded against the registration**, may fail — exactly when it is
> relied upon (due diligence, a dispute, a financing, a tax enquiry).
> **A fictitious "signed" assignment exists in the repository's demonstration data** (`DOC-IP-001`). It is
> **not** a document to copy. Copy this template instead, and have counsel settle it.

## 1. When an assignment is needed

| Situation | Needed? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Employee creates work in the course of employment | **Depends on the law** (`Q-IP-01(a)`–`(b)`) — do not assume | Where the law does not vest it automatically, an assignment is mandatory |
| Contractor, freelancer, agency, offshore team | **Yes** | The most common failure |
| Founder, before incorporation | **Yes**, from the founder **to** the incorporated entity | And it must be dated, with the rights identified |
| Contributor to open source, or a volunteer | **Yes**, if the group is to own it | Otherwise the contribution may be permanently third-party-owned |
| Seller of a business or asset | Yes | As part of the sale |
| **Assigning into or out of the Trust** | **Yes — plus the Trustees' decision and a Reserved-Matters check** | `D04` §7 |
| Employee leaves and a prior assignment is missing | **Yes — a confirmatory assignment** | Take it before the relationship ends, where possible |

## 2. Required contents (`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` each)

| # | Clause | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parties | **Exact legal names**; for an individual, their identity details; capacity stated |
| 2 | Effective date | And the date the rights arose, where retrospective |
| 3 | **Assignment clause** | An **assigns to B** all right, title and interest in the **defined** rights, **with full title guarantee where available** |
| 4 | **The definition of "Rights"** | Enumerated: copyright and neighbouring rights; source and object code; **AI models, weights, prompts and training configurations**; datasets and databases; designs and drawings; documentation; trade marks and goodwill; domain names; inventions and patents; know-how and trade secrets; and **all rights of action for past infringements** |
| 5 | **Excluded pre-existing works** | **Schedule A — a mandatory schedule.** If it is empty, say expressly: "no pre-existing materials are excluded" (and confirm the assignor's warranty) |
| 6 | **Future rights** | An assignment of future rights **may be ineffective in some laws** — take a **present assignment plus a further-assurance covenant** |
| 7 | **Consideration** | State it. **An assignment without consideration may be unenforceable** — record what was paid or provided |
| 8 | **Moral rights** | A waiver **to the extent permitted by law**, plus a consent to modification/attribution practices — **and note that some moral rights may not be waivable** |
| 9 | Warranties | Ownership; no prior assignment; no encumbrance; no third-party rights; no infringement |
| 10 | **Further assurances** | A covenant to sign what is needed later — **including recordal documents against any registration** |
| 11 | **Recordal** | Who files the recordal, within what period, at whose cost, and the obligation to report the acknowledgement |
| 12 | Confidentiality | With the survival clause |
| 13 | Third-party materials | A statement that the assignor has identified any third-party/open-source material used, and the licence position (`D45`) |
| 14 | Data protection | Where personal data is involved in the assigned material, the parties' roles and lawful basis (`D70`) |
| 15 | Governing law and forum | `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` |
| 16 | **Execution formalities** | Witness, notarisation, stamping, seal — as the applicable law requires (`Q-R-02`) |
| 17 | Schedule A | Excluded materials |
| 18 | Schedule B | The specific works/deliverables/marks/domains being assigned |

## 4. Execution and remediation workflow

| Step | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm **the assignor is the real holder** of the rights | The chain check (`D40` §3) |
| 2 | Complete **Schedule B** (identify precisely) | The schedule |
| 3 | Complete **Schedule A** (exclusions) | The schedule |
| 4 | Complete **Schedule C** (third-party materials) | The schedule |
| 5 | Set **consideration** | Evidence of payment |
| 6 | Counsel settles the form and the formalities | Counsel's markup |
| 7 | Execute | The signed original → `D74` |
| 8 | **Record the change of ownership** in `D40`/`D44` and, if material, `D67` | The registers |
| 9 | **File the recordal** where a registration exists; diary the outcome in `D68` | Registry acknowledgement |
| 10 | If the assignor is a **past employee/contractor** and is unco-operative — escalate to counsel; do not rely on a template letter | Counsel |

**4.1 Group-wide remediation rule.** Where the `D40` inventory shows contributors who are **not** covered by
an assignment, remediation is prioritised by the asset's materiality (`D40` §2.1 field 7). **A remediation
list is not an assignment, and an assignment is not effective until it is executed and recorded.**
