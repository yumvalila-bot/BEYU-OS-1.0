## 3. Procedure rules (operational)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | **One tracker, one owner.** A mark, a domain or a right is entered once, in one place, with one owner named |
| 3.2 | **Counsel/agent instruction is a board-authorised step**; the authority limit is in `D27` |
| 3.3 | **Every filing produces evidence** (filing receipt, application number, correspondence) filed in `D74` and recorded here |
| 3.4 | **Office actions, oppositions, refusals and deadlines are recorded the day they are received**, with the response deadline derived from the document itself |
| 3.5 | **Renewal reminders** are entered at **two intervals** (typically 12 and 6 months before the window opens), and **the reminder is not the action** — a human must do the step |
| 3.6 | **Foreign filings** are tracked separately by territory, with the agent named |
| 3.7 | **No filing fee is paid** except through the `D35` payment chain (decision + verified beneficiary + two signatures + reconciliation) |
| 3.8 | **Expiries and abandonments are recorded as events** — not quietly deleted from the tracker. An abandoned right is a fact the family must know |
| 3.9 | **The tracker is reviewed quarterly** by the owner and the Audit and Risk Committee; the review is minuted |
| 3.10 | **Nothing in the tracker is a legal conclusion.** It is an operational index; the instruments, the certificates and counsel's advice are the authority |
