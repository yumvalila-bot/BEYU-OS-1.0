## 1. Purpose

To provide a **clause-by-clause checklist** for the Foundation's constitution or governing document, so that
when the legal form is decided (`D48`), the drafting is complete, purpose-locked, independent, and
compliant with the filing requirements of that form.

**1.1 The pack's position on the Foundation's purposes.** The Foundation's stated purposes must be
**charitable/public-purpose** purposes — capable of serving people beyond the family. The pack does **not**
write the family's purposes for them (§7 decision 1), but it does state the test: **a purpose that in
substance benefits only the family and the group will fail the public-interest test and expose the structure
to refusal, deregistration and reputational damage** (`D48` §5, `D52`).

## 2. Mandatory clauses (every form)

| # | Clause | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Name** | Exactly the name to be registered; for Cap. 318, the form may require **"Registered Trustees"**; no name implying a group subsidiary or a government connection | Name approval and the statutory form |
| 2 | **Legal form and jurisdiction** | Stated | — |
| 3 | **Purposes** | Clear, public-purpose, measurable statements — **each capable of being evidenced by activity** (§3) | The statutory purpose test and donor trust |
| 4 | **Powers** | What the Foundation may do (hold property, receive gifts, grant, contract, employ, borrow — **each deliberately included or excluded**) | Determines capacity |
| 5 | **Prohibition on private benefit** | An express clause: the Foundation's income and property are applied **solely** to its purposes; **no distribution or transfer to a member, founder, office bearer, family member or connected body** except for a documented arm's-length exchange or a purpose-serving grant properly approved | **The central protection** (`D52`) |
| 6 | **No distribution on winding up** | On dissolution, assets pass to another body with similar purposes (or as the law directs) — **never to the founders or their family** | The classic test of charitable character |
| 7 | **Governing body** | Composition, appointment, term, removal, quorum, decision rules; and **a mandatory independent quorum where a related-party matter is decided** | `D50`, `D51` |
| 8 | **Office bearers** | Chair, secretary, treasurer (as the form requires); duties; **no person may hold all offices** | Separation of duties |
| 9 | **Members** (if any) | Who they are, their rights, and the limit of their power over the governing body | Prevents a "members' veto" being used to direct the body |
| 10 | **Meetings and records** | Notice, quorum, minutes, resolutions, and the **record-keeping** duty | Evidence |
| 11 | **Conflicts of interest** | Declare, record, recuse, and **the interested person does not vote or count in the quorum** | `D50` |
| 12 | **Financial management** | Bank accounts (two-signature), budget, annual accounts, **audit**, and the accounting authority | `D35`, `D53` |
| 13 | **Annual report and public reporting** | That the annual report and audited accounts are prepared **and made public**, per the applicable form (`D55`) | Cap. 56 s.29 and equivalents |
| 14 | **Amendment** | The procedure, the majority, and — **critically — the regulator notification obligation** | Cap. 56 s.31 |
| 15 | **Dissolution** | The procedure, the regulator's role, and the destination of assets (§2 clause 6) | — |
| 16 | **Indemnity/insurance** for office bearers | **Not for dishonesty or wilful misconduct** | Balanced protection |
| 17 | **Governing law and the applicable regulator** | Stated | — |
| 18 | **Execution formalities** | Signature, witness, seal as required | `Q-R-02`, `Q-R-03` |

## 3. The purposes schedule — how to draft purposes that survive scrutiny

**3.1 The four tests for each stated purpose.**

| # | Test | Failing example | Compliant shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Public benefit** — does it serve people beyond the family? | "To support the education of the founder's descendants" | "To advance education for young people in [region], including by grants, scholarships and programmes selected on published criteria" |
| 2 | **Measurable** — can activity be evidenced? | "To do good" | "To award at least [n] grants annually on published criteria, with outcomes reported" |
| 3 | **Non-commercial** — not a business in disguise? | "To provide software services to the group at below cost" | Programmes with public benefit; any services to the group must be incidental, documented and arm's length |

**3.3 Prohibited purpose wording (never include).**

| # | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| 1 | A purpose whose beneficiaries are only the founder's family, without a genuine public element |
| 2 | A purpose that facilitates a **private benefit** to a family member or a group company |
| 3 | A purpose that is a **commercial activity** of the group |
| 4 | A purpose that gives the **family a veto** over the Foundation's decisions |
| 5 | Wording copied from another organisation's constitution without checking that it fits |
| 6 | Any wording that asserts a **tax exemption** (the pack asserts none — `D48` §3.1) |

## 4. The independence architecture (the clauses that matter most)

| # | Requirement | Clause design |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **No group instruction right** | The constitution must contain **no** provision allowing the Trust, a company, a family member or the Family Office to direct the governing body |
| 4.2 | **Independent quorum for related-party matters** | A related-party decision requires a quorum of which a stated majority are **non-interested**; a connected person's presence does not count |
| 4.3 | **Appointment of the governing body** | The appointment mechanism must not allow a single family person to appoint or remove a majority at will. **Where the family funds the Foundation and appoints the body, the independence comes from the *duties*, the *conflict rules* and the *audit* — and that is a weaker position that must be acknowledged rather than disguised** (`D50` §3) |
| 4.4 | **Removal** | Only for stated cause, by a defined majority, never by an external family instruction |
| 4.5 | **No reserved matters for the family** | The family has **no** reserved matter over the Foundation. Any matter the family cares about must be addressed through the purposes, not through control |
| 4.6 | **Related-party protocol embedded** | Either restated in the constitution or expressly incorporated by reference to `D51` |
| 4.7 | **Right to refuse** | The governing body must be empowered, and required, to **refuse** a transaction that fails the tests (`D51` §5) |
| 4.8 | **Legal rights preserved** | Nothing in the constitution removes any person's legal rights or access to a court or regulator |
