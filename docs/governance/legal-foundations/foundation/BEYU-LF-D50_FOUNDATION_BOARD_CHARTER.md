# BEYU-LF-D50 — Foundation Board / Governing Body Charter and Conflicts Policy

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D50` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — **no governing body exists, no member is appointed, no meeting has been held.**
Owner (proposed): Foundation Director. Approving authority: the Foundation's founders, then its own governing body.
Classification: `CONFIDENTIAL`. Related: `D25` (the company board — deliberately separate), `D48`, `D49`, `D51`, `D52`, `D53`, `D55`, `D66`, `D73`.
Depends on: the chosen legal form and the constitution (`D48`, `D49`); `Q-G-01`, `Q-G-03`.

> **⚠ THE INDEPENDENCE PROBLEM, STATED HONESTLY.**
> Where the family funds the Foundation and appoints its governing body, **independence cannot come from
> ownership — the family has none to give away.** It must come from: **(1) the DUTIES** (the members owe their
> duties to the purposes, not to the family); **(2) the CONFLICT RULES** (interested persons do not decide);
> **(3) the AUDIT and PUBLIC REPORTING**; and **(4) a governing document the family cannot unilaterally
> change** (`D49` §4). **Software controls are not legal independence (`DAA-FDN-005`)** — Foundation OS's
> boundary in the control plane is real and useful, and it is **not** a substitute for any of the four.

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | At least `[N]` meetings per year (recommended: quarterly), plus ad hoc meetings on `[N]` members' request |
| 3.2 | Papers circulated `[N]` days in advance, including financials, grants, and any related-party matter with its analysis |
| 3.3 | **Quorum:** `[QUORUM]`, with `[INDEPENDENT QUORUM]` independent members. **For a related-party matter, a majority of those present must be independent and non-interested, and the interested member is not counted** |
| 3.4 | **Decisions** by majority of those entitled to vote; minutes record attendance, conflicts, recusals, the substance, the decision and the dissent |

## 4. Conflicts of interest policy

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Declare** every interest (financial, family, fiduciary, social) in writing on appointment, annually, and immediately when one arises |
| 4.2 | **The register of interests** is maintained and available to the auditor and the regulator |
| 4.3 | **Recusal** from the discussion and the vote; **not counted** in the quorum; **not given the papers** for that item where the information is sensitive |
| 4.4 | **Interested-person test (wide):** a member is interested if the matter benefits them, a family member, an entity they control or are a director/officer of, the trust of which they are a trustee, or a group company — **including indirectly** |
| 4.5 | **A member who is also a director, trustee or Family Office principal** must declare the dual role; **for a transaction between the two bodies, they must not decide on either side** (`D14` §5.4, `D27` §8.4) |
| 4.6 | **No member may vote on their own remuneration, expenses, or a grant to an entity connected to them** |
| 4.7 | **Where the whole body is conflicted**, the matter is escalated per the constitution (an independent decision-maker, a regulator notification, or it does not proceed) |
| 4.8 | **A concealed conflict** is a disciplinary and governance matter, reported to the body and — depending on the form — to the regulator |
