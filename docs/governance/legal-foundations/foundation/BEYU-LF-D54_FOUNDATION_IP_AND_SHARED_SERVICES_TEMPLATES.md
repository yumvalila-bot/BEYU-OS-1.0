**2.1 Additional requirement for F-1:** the licence must be **defensible on value** — either at market rate,
or at nil with a **recorded reason** (e.g. the Foundation's charitable use of the mark for fundraising), the
comparison made, and the tax consequence considered.

## 3. F-2 — Foundation → group licence (highest scrutiny)

**Additional mandatory clauses:** the group pays **market value** (documented by comparison or valuation);
the licence **does not** permit the group to commercially exploit the Foundation's asset for the group's
profit; the Foundation may **terminate at will** on a governance determination; the licence **terminates
automatically** if the Foundation's governing body resolves that it no longer serves its purposes; the
group has **no right to the Foundation's goodwill**; and the arrangement is reported in the Foundation's
annual report. **Where the Foundation's asset was received with a donor restriction, the licence may not be
possible at all (`D52` §2.6).**

## 4. F-3 — Shared services agreement (clause set)

```
SHARED SERVICES AGREEMENT | Ref: [REF] | Date: [DATE]
PROVIDER: [ ]   RECIPIENT: [ ]
1. SERVICES: Schedule 1 (service, deliverable, standard, frequency, provider owner).
2. CHARGES: Schedule 2 (basis: direct cost / cost + margin / market rate; the allocation driver; the
   invoicing cycle; the payment terms). Every charge must be supported by the allocation basis in F-4.
3. ARM'S-LENGTH STATEMENT: the parties confirm the charges are intended to be at arm's length; the basis
   and the comparison are recorded in Schedule 2; the tax position was considered before signature
   [reference the tax advice].
4. DIRECTION AND IDENTITY: the Provider will not direct the Recipient's decisions; neither party is the
   agent of the other. [For the Foundation: its governing body retains independent decision-making.]
5. DATA PROTECTION: Schedule 3 (roles, data, security, breach route, cross-border position, AI restrictions).
6. RECORDS AND AUDIT: each party may audit the other's records relevant to the charges once per year.
7. TERM/TERMINATION/TRANSITION: [ ] with a wind-down and a final reconciliation.
8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: per the escalation in D17/D29 [ ].
9. RELATED-PARTY REGISTRATION: recorded in D72 (ref [ ]); reviewed annually against the D51 five tests.
10. GOVERNING LAW/FORMALITIES: [ ]
```
**4.1 The "no direction" clause is not decorative.** For the Foundation, it is the clause that evidences
independence; for the group, it is the clause that stops charitable funds being directed by the family.
**It must be capable of being proved in practice — the minutes must show that the Foundation decided.**
