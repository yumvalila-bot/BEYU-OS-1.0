# BEYU-LF-D51 — Foundation Independence and Related-Party Transaction Protocol

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D51` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — **no related-party transaction has been assessed, approved, registered or refused. No arrangement exists.**
Owner (proposed): Foundation Director with the Chief Governance Officer. Approving authority: the Foundation's governing body (independent quorum) and, on the group side, the board.
Classification: `CONFIDENTIAL`. Related: `D27` (F-07, G-03, G-06), `D30` §5.6, `D49`, `D50`, `D52`, `D53`, `D54`, `D72`, `D73`, `D74`.
Depends on: `Q-G-03(a)`–`(g)`, `Q-FO-02`; `DAA-FDN-004`.

> **⚠ THE EIGHT-WORD TEST.** **Would an independent regulator accept this?** If the answer is anything other
> than a confident yes — with the documents to prove it — the arrangement must be **restructured, documented
> until it can be, or abandoned.**
> **And the operating rule: the Foundation's decisions are the FOUNDATION'S.** A group resolution cannot
> authorise a Foundation act, and a Foundation resolution cannot authorise a group act.

## 1. What counts as a related-party transaction (the wide list)

| # | Transaction | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A **grant** to a body connected with a family member or a group company | Private benefit |
| 2 | The group **paying** the Foundation (donation, sponsorship) **with conditions** | Direction; benefit |
| 3 | The Foundation **paying** the group for services, rent, staff, software, IP | Overpayment; subsidy |
| 4 | The Foundation **receiving** services from the Family Office | Cost-shifting |
| 5 | An **IP licence** either way (`D54`) | Value transfer |
| 6 | **Secondment or employment** of a family member or a connected person by the Foundation | Benefit; independence |
| 7 | The Foundation **holding** an asset for the group's benefit, or vice versa | Beneficial ownership confusion |
| 8 | A **loan, guarantee or advance** in either direction | Exposure |
| 9 | **Use of the group's brand** by the Foundation, or vice versa | Value; confusion of identity |
| 10 | Any arrangement where a **governing-body member is interested** | Conflict |
| 11 | Any transaction with the **Trust** or a **beneficiary** | Private benefit |
| 12 | A transaction that is **only disclosed after it happened** | Process failure |

## 2. The five tests (mandatory, recorded for every transaction)

| # | Test | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| T-1 | **Purpose test** — does it serve the Foundation's stated purposes? | A written statement tying the transaction to a purpose (`D49` §3) |
| T-2 | **Necessity test** — is it necessary, and is there a cheaper or better alternative? | The comparison (including a free/non-group alternative, where one exists) |
| T-3 | **Value test** — is the consideration **no less favourable to the Foundation** than an arm's-length alternative? | A written comparison; an independent valuation where material; the basis and date recorded |
| T-4 | **Independence test** — was the decision taken by an **independent, non-interested** quorum, with the interested persons absent and not counted, and with the group not directing? | The minute showing the composition and the recusals |
| T-5 | **Evidence test** — is there a written agreement, a register entry, and an audit trail? | The instrument (`D74`), `D72`, the accounting record |

**2.1 Any test failed = the transaction does not proceed.** A partial pass is a fail. **No "commercially
sensible overall" override exists.**

## 3. The approval process (step by step)

| Step | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The transaction is **identified as related-party** at the earliest point (a register entry is created even at proposal stage) | Anyone → Foundation Director / CGO |
| 2 | The **analysis pack** is prepared: the purposes link, the necessity comparison, the value comparison, the conflict map, and the draft instrument | Foundation Director |
| 3 | The **conflict map** is checked against the register of interests; interested members are identified and excluded | Secretary |
| 4 | The **independent quorum** meets (with counsel present where material) and applies T-1…T-5 | Governing body |
| 5 | The **decision** is minuted as `RECOMMEND`/`DECIDE`/`REFUSE`, with reasons | Secretary |
| 6 | The **group side** takes its own decision (`D27` F-07/G-03/G-06), with the disinterested process | Board |
| 7 | The instrument is **executed** (`D54` templates), filed in `D74`, and **registered in `D72`** | Company Secretary |
| 8 | **Performance is monitored**: the deliverables are received, the invoice matches the agreement, the value stays defensible | Foundation Director |
| 9 | **Annual review** against T-1…T-5, and reporting in the annual report | Governing body |
| 10 | If the arrangement **no longer passes the tests**, it is **terminated or renegotiated — not continued for convenience** | Governing body |

## 4. The independence obligations of the group

| # | Obligation |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | The group must **not direct** the Foundation. A request is a request; a "requirement" is a governance event and is refused in writing |
| 4.2 | The group must **not** use the Foundation's funds, staff, assets, premises or brand as if they were the group's |
| 4.3 | The group must **not** condition a donation on a benefit to the group or the family |
| 4.4 | The group must **not** appoint or remove Foundation office bearers |
| 4.5 | The group must **not** access the Foundation's confidential information except under a documented agreement |
| 4.6 | The group must **pay** for what it receives from the Foundation, on documented arm's-length terms |
| 4.7 | The group's compliance calendar (`D68`) must include the Foundation's dependencies (review dates, renewals) |

**4.8 What to do when someone tries.** The Foundation's body must: record the attempt; decline in writing,
citing the constitution and `D51`; inform the independent members; and — where the attempt is a serious or
repeated breach — take advice on notification to the regulator. **A documented refusal is the strongest
evidence of independence the Foundation can hold.**

## 5. Prohibited transactions (absolute)

| # | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| 1 | A grant or payment that benefits a family member personally, unless it is a genuine purpose-serving grant to an independent beneficiary on published criteria |
| 2 | A transaction whose real purpose is to move money out of the Foundation to the group |
| 3 | A **loan** from the Foundation to the group or a family member |
| 4 | A **guarantee** by the Foundation for the group's obligations |
| 5 | The Foundation **holding** an asset the group uses without a documented arm's-length arrangement |
| 6 | Payment by the Foundation for the group's **marketing, brand or goodwill** benefit |
| 7 | Any arrangement where the Foundation **bears a cost** that properly belongs to the group |
| 8 | Any arrangement that **conditionally returns** a donation to the donor |
| 9 | Any transaction approved by an **interested majority** |
| 10 | Any transaction **not recorded** in `D72` within `[N]` days |

## 6. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. Whether the group will transact with the Foundation **at all** — and if so, the categories
   (donations in; services out; shared costs).
2. Who sits on the independent side of the decision.
3. The **value threshold** above which an independent valuation is required.
4. The **reporting** to the family: how much of the Foundation's activity will be visible to the family
   (noting that visibility must not become control).
5. Who **signs** for the Foundation, and the two-signature rule.
