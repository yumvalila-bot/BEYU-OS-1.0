> **⚠ THE VERIFIED OBLIGATION THAT DRIVES THIS CHECKLIST.**
> For an NGO under Cap. 56, the statute requires an **annual audited report submitted to the Council and the
> Board and made available to the public** (s.29); the **constitution governs** (s.30); a change of
> constitution, name or office bearers must be **notified to the Registrar in writing** with a certified
> resolution (s.31); and the certificate is subject to **renewal** (reported as a ten-year cycle with a
> six-month lead — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`). **This is a permanent compliance obligation, not a one-off
> registration.** Where a different form is chosen, the equivalent duties must be confirmed and this
> checklist updated.
