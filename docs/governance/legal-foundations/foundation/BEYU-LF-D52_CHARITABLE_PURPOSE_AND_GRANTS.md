> **⚠ THE PURPOSE-LOCK.** **Every shilling the Foundation holds is held for its stated purposes.** There is no
> "founder's discretion" over charitable funds; a founder's wish is not a purpose. **And the moment a grant,
> a programme, a job or a contract serves a private interest, the Foundation's legitimacy — and possibly its
> registration — is at risk (`D51`, `DAA-FDN-004`).**

## 1. The purposes

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | The Foundation acts **only** within its stated purposes (`D49` §3). A proposed activity outside them is **declined**, however attractive |
| 1.2 | Each activity must be traceable to a purpose, and the trace is recorded |
| 1.3 | The purposes are **filled in by the family** (`D49` §3.2 — `[USER TO SUPPLY]`); this document supplies the framework, not the content |
| 1.4 | Where a purpose is amended, the amendment follows the constitution's procedure **and the regulator notification** (Cap. 56 s.31) |

## 2. Funds: restricted, unrestricted and designated

| # | Fund type | Meaning | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Unrestricted** | Available for any purpose | Governed by the body's discretion, recorded |
| 2.2 | **Restricted** | Given for a **specified purpose or programme** | **May be used only for that purpose.** The restriction is honoured even if the purpose becomes unattractive |
| 2.3 | **Endowment/permanent** | Capital preserved; income applied | The capital policy is documented; the body may not quietly consume capital |
| 2.4 | **Designated** | Set aside by the body for an intention (not a donor restriction) | May be re-designated by the body with reasons |
| 2.5 | **Conditional gift** | A gift with a condition (matching, milestone, geography) | The condition is recorded; **an unlawful or improper condition is refused** |

**2.6 Restricted-fund discipline.** Each restricted fund has: its donor, its instrument/letter, its purpose,
its balance, its expenditure history, and the unspent balance reported in the accounts. **Where a restricted
fund cannot be applied as intended, the body seeks the donor's written variation or applies to the regulator
as the law allows — never repurposes it silently.**

## 3. Grantmaking

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Criteria published** (purpose, eligibility, geography, size, exclusions) before applications are invited |
| 3.2 | **Application and assessment** recorded; the assessment against criteria written down |
| 3.3 | **Due diligence** on the grantee: legal existence, governance, financial capability, purpose alignment, and — where applicable — its own regulatory standing |
| 3.4 | **No grant to a related party** without the full `D51` process, and **a grant to a family member personally is prohibited** except under a published programme with independent selection |
| 3.5 | **Grant agreement**: purpose, amount, timing, reporting, variation, termination, and the consequences of misuse |
| 3.6 | **Payment** per `D35` §4, against the agreement and the decision |
| 3.7 | **Monitoring**: reports received and reviewed; outcomes recorded; a non-performing grant addressed (support, variation, or recovery where agreement permits) |
| 3.8 | **Records**: the application, the assessment, the decision, the agreement, the payment, the reports and the outcome — for each grant |
| 3.9 | **Refusal is recorded with reasons** — refusals are evidence of a real selection process |

## 4. Private-benefit safeguards

| # | Prohibited | Permitted if properly handled |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | A grant or benefit to a family member, a connected person or a group company **except** on the arm's length/purpose-serving route with independent approval | A programme open on published criteria where a family member is one applicant among many — **with the relationship disclosed and the decision taken by non-interested persons** |
| 4.2 | Employment of a family member **because** they are a family member | A competitive appointment on documented merit, with the conflict managed |
| 4.3 | Provision of free services to the group | A documented arm's-length agreement (`D53`, `D54`) |
| 4.4 | The family using the Foundation's assets, premises, staff or funds for private purposes | — |
| 4.5 | A donation that buys the donor a benefit or influence | — |
| 4.6 | A "grant" that is in substance a payment for the group's work | Correct characterisation and full documentation |
| 4.7 | Funding a family member's business, education or living costs through the Foundation | — |

**4.8 The disclosure rule.** Any actual, potential or **perceived** private benefit must be **disclosed to the
governing body immediately**, recorded, and reported in the annual report where material. **Concealing a
benefit is worse than the benefit.**

## 5. Donations received

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | A donation is **accepted or refused** by the governing body against the purposes, the ethics, the reputational risk and the conditions |
| 5.2 | A donor's **conditions are recorded** and reviewed for propriety and legality |
| 5.3 | A donation from a **group company or a family member** is a related-party matter: `D51` applies (value, purpose, no direction, independence) |
| 5.4 | A donation creating a **conflict** for a member is managed (the member is interested) |
| 5.5 | **No donation may be accepted on terms that would allow the donor to recapture it** for their own benefit |
| 5.6 | Donations are **acknowledged and receipted**, and recorded in the accounts; **anonymous donations** are handled per the applicable law and any AML/permissibility rules `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` |
| 5.7 | A donation that is **itself the proceeds of unlawful activity** must be refused and escalated (`Q-G-03`) |

## 6. Reporting

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 6.1 | The **annual report** states: the purposes served; the activities; the grants made; the funds (restricted/unrestricted); the related-party transactions; the governance; and the audited accounts (Cap. 56 s.29 and equivalents) |
| 6.2 | The report is **made public** as the form requires — **and the family should read its own Foundation's public report as a stranger would** |
| 6.3 | **Impact reporting** is honest: what was spent, what was achieved, what failed — **no inflated claims, no unverifiable statistics** |
| 6.4 | **A failed programme is reported as failed.** A charity that never fails is a charity that never tries |

## 7. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. The **purposes** (`D49` §3.2).
2. The **programmes and criteria**.
3. The **budget** and the funding model for the year (`D53`).
4. Whether the family will make **grants to individuals**, and on what published criteria.
5. Whether the Foundation will **accept donations from the group**, and on what terms.
6. Who **selects and monitors grantees**, with what capacity.
7. The **endowment/reserves policy** (how many months of activity are held).
