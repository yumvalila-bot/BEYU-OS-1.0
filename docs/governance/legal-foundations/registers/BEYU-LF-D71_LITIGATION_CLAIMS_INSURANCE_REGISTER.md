# BEYU-LF-D71 — Litigation, Claims, Insurance and Regulatory Correspondence Register

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D71` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`TEMPLATE_NOT_EXECUTED`** — **BLANK TEMPLATE. Not populated. No row describes a real person, asset, obligation, transaction, decision or document.**
Owner (proposed): Company Secretary function with the CGO. Approving authority: the board.
Classification: `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` (privilege and personal data). Related: `D26` §2, `D29` §4, `D35` §4.3, `D36` §4, `D46` §7, `D60` §3.8, `D61`, `D67`, `D68`, `D73`, `D74`. Depends on: `Q-R-05`, `Q-R-06`; the insurance programme itself.

> **⚠ THE PRIVILEGE WARNING.**
> **A register that summarises legal advice can destroy the privilege it was created to protect.** This
> register records **facts and status**, not advice: the parties, the forum, the issue, the dates, the
> amounts, the insurer, the external adviser's **reference** — and **not** the adviser's reasoning.
> **Advice is held separately, restricted, and is never copied into a file that circulates to the family, the
> board papers, an auditor's working file or a third party.**
> **And: no register entry, and no AI summary of one, may be used to decide strategy.**

**1.1 The preservation duty.** From the moment a matter is **reasonably foreseeable**: suspend deletion,
preserve documents and messages, and **do not alter, backdate or "tidy" anything.** A register entry is
created **before** the first demand is answered.

**3.1 The two insurance questions that matter most.** **(a) If a director is sued, is that director actually
covered — and is the entity covered for indemnifying them?** **(b) Is there any material risk on `D61` that
no policy responds to?** Both are board questions, answered annually in writing.

## 4. Controls

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| C-01 | **No advice content** in this register; references only |
| C-02 | Access limited to the CGO, the Company Secretary, the CFO, the board and (on scope) the auditors |
| C-03 | **Legal holds are honoured**: no deletion, no "clean-up", no re-imaging of devices without advice |
| C-04 | **Notification to insurers is diarised and evidenced** |
| C-05 | **Settlement authority** is exercised only per `D27`; a settlement that affects the Trust goes through
the Trustee process |
| C-06 | **Provisioning and disclosure** are coordinated with the auditors and counsel |
| C-07 | No AI-generated strategy, assessment or prediction is recorded here as if it were advice |
| C-08 | Every matter has a **status review date**, and the board receives the summary at least quarterly |

## 5. Decisions required

1. **Settlement authority** levels (`D27` L-07).
2. The **insurance programme** and its limits (a cost/risk trade-off decision).
3. Whether **trustee** cover is arranged (the instrument may permit it — `D04`/`D10`; `DAA-TRUST-*`).
4. Who is the **single point of contact** for each regulator.
5. Whether matters are disclosed to the family, and at what level of detail.
