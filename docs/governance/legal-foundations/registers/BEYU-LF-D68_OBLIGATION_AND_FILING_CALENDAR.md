> **Nothing is entered from memory, a template, an old spread-sheet or a secondary web page.** Every
> obligation is entered **from the official source or from counsel's confirmed advice** — **with the source

## 1. Purpose

A single, source-verified schedule of: **statutory filings and returns; renewals; licence and permit
expiries; tax filings and payment dates; contractual obligations of a recurring nature; insurance renewals;
and internal governance obligations** — feeding the board and committee reporting.

## 4. Controls

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| C-01 | **Source citation mandatory**; a row without a source is deleted or marked `UNVERIFIED` and escalated |
| C-02 | **Two reminders minimum**, to a **role and a named person** |
| C-03 | **The calendar is tested** annually: does each reminder actually arrive, and is the owner still in post? |
| C-04 | **A missed deadline is an incident** (`D71`): cause, exposure, remedy, and a report to the board/committee |
| C-05 | **Entity-owned filing accounts** with two-person access (never one person's personal email/portal login) |
| C-06 | **Delegation to an agent does not transfer the obligation** — the entity remains liable; the agent's performance is monitored |
| C-07 | **Access, portal credentials and payment methods** are recorded and reviewable |
| C-08 | **Fees are paid through the `D35` chain**; **no fee payment without the obligation reference** |
