## 1. Purpose and the central question

**1.1** To identify, record and file the **beneficial owners** required to be disclosed in respect of each
entity — and to answer **the question the family must put to counsel first**:

> **If the BEYU Family Trust is the registered shareholder of the holding company and the family members are
> its beneficiaries, WHO must be disclosed as the beneficial owner — the trustee, the settlor, the
> beneficiaries, or a combination — and in what proportions?** (`Q-H-05(b)`.)

**1.2 What the pack can say without counsel.** Only this: the disclosure regime exists; the relevant forms
(including a "registered owner who does not hold the beneficial interest", a "ceasing to be a beneficial
owner" form, and a "beneficial owner not registered in their own name" form) were observed in the 2026
materials published by BRELA; and the **filing obligations fall on the company** (and, for foreign companies,
on the registered entity). **No threshold, no percentage and no period is asserted as verified.**
