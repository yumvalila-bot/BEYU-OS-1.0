> **⚠ THE PURPOSE OF AN ANNUAL REVIEW IS NOT REASSURANCE.**
> **It is to find the things that have quietly stopped working.** Every governance system decays: delegations
> expire unnoticed, conflicts stop being declared, registers drift from reality, renewals slip, and the
> "temporary" arrangement becomes permanent. **The review's output is a list of defects with owners and dates —
> and a report to the board and the Trustees that a reader could act on.**

## 2. The review process

| Step | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Set the **scope and the evidence list** before the review | CGO |
| 2.2 | Collect the evidence (registers, filings, confirmations) | Secretary |
| 2.3 | **Test by inspection** — do not accept a summary; look at the document | CGO |
| 2.4 | **Interview** at least one person per function (the person who actually does it) | CGO |
| 2.5 | Produce a **defect list** with severity, owner and date | CGO |
| 2.6 | **Report** to the board, the Trustees and the Foundation's body (as each applies) | CGO |
| 2.7 | Track remediation to closure; report the status quarterly | Board |
| 2.8 | **Update this pack**, the registers and the calendar that the review has changed | CGO |

**2.9 Independence.** The review should not be performed solely by the person responsible for the area being
reviewed. Where capacity is limited, **an external reviewer or the auditor's observations** should be used for
the highest-risk areas (money, data, filings).

## 3. Severity and consequence

| Severity | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | A missing registration; an overdue filing; an asset with no ownership evidence; a conflict undeclared; a control (two signatures) bypassed | Report immediately; remediate within `[N]` days; consider disclosure |
| **Major** | An expired delegation; an unreconciled account; a missing arm's-length record; a successor not named | Remediate within `[N]` days |
| **Minor** | A register field incomplete; a valuation without a basis; a document copy not filed | Remediate within `[N]` days |
| **Accepted** | A risk the family has consciously accepted | **Recorded with the reason and the accepting body** |
