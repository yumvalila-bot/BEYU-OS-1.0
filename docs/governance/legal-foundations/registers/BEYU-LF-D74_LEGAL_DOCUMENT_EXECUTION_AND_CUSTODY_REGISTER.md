> **⚠ THE TWO QUESTIONS THIS REGISTER ANSWERS.**
> **1. Where is the original, and who can produce it — today, and if that person is away?**
> **2. Is the document actually effective — signed by the right person, in the right form, and (where
> required) filed, stamped, notarised or recorded?**
> **An instrument that exists but was not validly executed is not an asset — it is a liability, because it
> will be relied upon.**

## 3. Custody rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Originals are held at a defined place** with a defined custodian, and the location is recorded here |
| 3.2 | **At least two people** can produce any critical original — **and they know they are named** |
| 3.3 | **No original leaves custody without a signed receipt** and a recorded return |
| 3.4 | **Copies are marked** as copies; a copy is never presented as an original |
| 3.5 | **Digital copies** are in the governed repository with access control; **not in email, chat or personal storage** |
| 3.6 | **If a document is lost**, it is recorded as `LOST` **with a remediation plan** (a certified copy, an affidavit, a reconstruction, a registry search) — **never silently** |
| 3.7 | **Privileged documents** are kept in a separate, restricted location and are **not** circulated (`D36` §4 S-05) |
| 3.8 | **Legal holds** (`D60` §3.9) override any deletion or movement |

## 4. Controls

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| C-01 | **No entry without inspecting the document** — the register is not built from memory or a list |
| C-02 | **A defective execution is recorded as a defect** and escalated for remedy (a confirmatory instrument, a re-execution, a corrected filing) |
| C-03 | **Filing/recordal is tracked to the acknowledgement** — a submission is not a registration |
| C-04 | **The register is reconciled** with `D68` (deadlines), `D73` (decisions) and `D67` (assets) at least quarterly |
| C-05 | **Access is least-privilege**; the register reveals the family's legal architecture |
| C-06 | **The register does not contain** privileged advice, full instrument texts, or personal data beyond the identifying minimum — **it is an index** |
| C-07 | **An AI summary of a document is not the document** and is never the evidence of its contents |
| C-08 | **Bulk import of fixture documents is prohibited** — the fixture "signed IP assignment" and the trademark record are fiction (`D39` §1) |
