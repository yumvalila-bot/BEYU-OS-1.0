Classification: `CONFIDENTIAL`. Related: `D04` §7, `D10`, `D25` §4.7, `D26` (typing rule), `D27`, `D28`, `D29`, `D51`, `D62`, `D66`, `D74`. Depends on: the decisions themselves; `Q-R-02` (execution formalities).

> **⚠ WHY THIS REGISTER EXISTS.**
> **Most governance failures are not decisions that were wrong — they are decisions that cannot be proved.**
> The register makes a decision **findable, attributable and effective-dated**: who decided, under what power,
> when, with what conflicts, and with what evidence.
> **And the rule that keeps it clean: a decision is recorded ONCE, at the level that took it, with a
> reference to any related decision at another level — never merged, never "summarised together".**

## 4. Controls

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| C-01 | **Every material decision is registered within `[N]` days** of being taken |
| C-02 | **The power is cited.** A decision without a power is flagged as a **governance defect** and reviewed |
| C-03 | Where a matter appears on both the Trust and the company side (`D27` §8.3), **both decisions are registered and cross-referenced**, and a single-sided entry is flagged |
| C-04 | **Emergency decisions** (`D62`, `D28` E-01) are registered with their ratification status |
| C-05 | **A committee `RECOMMEND` is registered as a recommendation** and never as a decision |
| C-06 | **Unimplemented decisions are followed up** — a decision with no implementation date is reported to the board |
| C-07 | **No deletion**: superseded, revoked and unwound decisions remain, with links |
| C-08 | **Access** is limited; the register reveals strategy and conflicts |
| C-09 | **An AI-generated minute or summary is not the decision record** until a human adopts it (`D02` cl. 7.4) |
| C-10 | **The register is the index, not the instrument**: the minute and the signed document are the evidence (`D74`) |
