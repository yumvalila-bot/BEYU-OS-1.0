## 3. Controls and prohibitions

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| C-01 | **The record of processing exists before a new tool or process goes live** — not after |
| C-02 | **No personal data in a personal email, personal device, personal cloud or unapproved AI service** |
| C-03 | **Least privilege, need-to-know**, reviewed at least annually and on every role change |
| C-04 | **Every processor is under a written agreement** with the security, breach and audit terms |
| C-05 | **Every cross-border flow is mapped and lawfully routed** — no "it's just an email abroad" |
| C-06 | **Every breach is assessed and, where required, notified** — the decision and the legal basis recorded |
| C-07 | **Deletion is scheduled and evidenced**; the exception is a **legal hold**, which is documented |
| C-08 | **No AI on privileged material** (`D36` §4 S-05), and **no AI as an authority** on a data question |
| C-09 | **The DPO reports to the board and the Audit and Risk Committee** — not only to management |
