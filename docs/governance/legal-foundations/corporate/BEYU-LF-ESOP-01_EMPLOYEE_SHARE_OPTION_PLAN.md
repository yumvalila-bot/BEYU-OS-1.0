> **⚠ THE FIVE THINGS THAT GO WRONG WITH AN ESOP.**
> **1. The pool was never validly created** (no shareholder resolution, no constitutional authority) — so the
> promises are unenforceable and the grants invalid.
> **2. The plan promises something the law does not permit** (options over unissued shares without authority;
> a company acquiring its own shares; a foreign plan in a jurisdiction that requires a filing).
> **3. The tax was never modelled** — the employee receives a surprise tax bill at vesting or exercise, or the
> company discovers a withholding obligation it did not collect.
> **4. The leaver rules are unfair or unclear** — so the best people leave with nothing and litigate, and the
> company's retention tool becomes its biggest liability.
> **5. The equity was granted before the company's own records were in order** (`CAP-01`) — so nobody can say
> what anybody actually holds.
> **Not one of these is cured by drafting. Each is a decision, an approval and a piece of evidence.**

**4.1 The prohibition.** **No leaver category may be defined by reference to a protected characteristic, to
the exercise of a legal right, to a report to an authority, or to a good-faith complaint** (`D02` §10.11
applied by analogy). A "bad leaver" definition that catches a protected act is void or voidable and dangerous.

## 5. Pool, dilution and cap-table integration

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | The pool is a **reservation of authorised but unissued shares** (or of shares to be transferred). It is **not** an issue. It appears in `CAP-01` §2.6 as **figure B**, never as issued shares |
| 5.2 | **An increase in the pool dilutes existing holders** — the founders' decision, taken on purpose, with the effect on each holder recorded (in **numbers**, not percentages alone) |
| 5.3 | Each grant is recorded against the pool; the unallocated pool is the residue. **An over-allocated pool is a defect** — the grants are unenforceable to the extent they exceed it |
| 5.4 | **Options are not shares**: no dividend, no vote, no capital return unless the instrument expressly provides |
| 5.5 | On exercise: the share is **issued** (or transferred) and **only then** enters `CAP-01` §2.1, the register (`D64`) and the resolutions record (`D73`) |
| 5.6 | Control plane: `esop_plans` (`pool_shares_authorized`, `pool_shares_issued`, `default_vesting`, `exercise_window_days`, `jurisdiction_code`, `legal_review_status`), `esop_grants` (`option_shares`, `exercise_price_per_share`, `grant_date`, `vesting_schedule_id`, `status`, `exercised_shares`, `tax_metadata`), `esop_grant_events`, and `vesting_*`. **Rows resolve to signed instruments or they are `UNSUPPORTED`** (`CAP-01` §5) |
| 5.7 | `legal_review_status` on every plan and grant, and `approved_by_resolution_id` on the plan: **a plan row without a resolution reference is not an adopted plan** |
