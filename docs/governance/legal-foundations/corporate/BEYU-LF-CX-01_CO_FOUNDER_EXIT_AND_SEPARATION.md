> **⚠ THREE RULES THAT DECIDE WHETHER A SEPARATION GOES WELL OR BADLY.**
> **1. The definition comes first.** A "bad leaver" definition that is vague, subjective, or drafted after the
> event is the single most litigated founder issue. **It must be objective, written before it is needed, and
> applied mechanically.**
> **2. Money follows solvency.** A repurchase obligation the company cannot lawfully satisfy is not an
> obligation — it is a dispute (`D24` §6).
> **3. Nothing here may be used to punish a person for asserting a legal right** — including a claim for
> unfair prejudice, a statutory remedy, a whistleblowing report, or a lawful complaint. **An exit agreement
> that requires a release of unknown claims or of protected rights is void or voidable in whole or in part**
> `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`.

## 2. The exit clause (for insertion in `FA-01` §3.5–3.6 or the Articles/SHA, per counsel)

> **Clause X — Cessation, classification and consequence. `[CONDITIONAL_DRAFT — COUNSEL TO SETTLE]`**
>
> **X.1 Cessation events.** A Founder's "Cessation" occurs on: (a) resignation from their office with the
> company; (b) termination of employment or engagement; (c) the founder ceasing to hold the office the
> agreement specifies, however caused; (d) the founder's death or incapacity; (e) the founder becoming a
> **"Bad Leaver"** under X.2; or (f) the board, in accordance with the Articles, requiring the founder to
> resign.
>
> **X.2 Classification. [The definitions must be objective.]**
> **(a) "Good Leaver"** means a Cessation arising from: death; incapacity determined under `D03` §6.4;
> retirement at or after `[AGE OR PERIOD]`; or termination by the company **without cause** (as defined).
> **(b) "Neutral Leaver"** means resignation by the founder **after `[PERIOD]` from the vesting commencement
> date** with `[NOTICE]` given and the handover in X.5 completed.
> **(c) "Bad Leaver"** means a Cessation arising from: (i) termination **for cause**, being dishonesty, fraud,
> wilful misconduct, a **material breach** of this agreement, the IP assignment, `NDA-01` or `RC-01`, or the
> commission of a criminal offence; (ii) **resignation within `[PERIOD]`** of the vesting commencement date;
> (iii) **breach of the restrictive covenants**; or (iv) the founder's **unremedied failure** to perform the
> agreed contribution after written notice and `[NUMBER]` days to remedy.
> **(d) Notice and remedy.** No Cessation is classified as a Bad Leaver under (c)(i) or (c)(iv) unless the
> founder has been **given written notice of the alleged ground, the evidence, and an opportunity to be heard**
> (`D02` §16.6 sets the standard the pack applies to fairness) — **unless the conduct makes prior notice
> impracticable, in which case the notice and the hearing follow immediately.**
> **(e) Dispute.** A classification may be disputed under §6.
>
> **X.3 Share treatment.**
> | Class | Vested shares | Unvested shares |
> |---|---|---|
> | Good Leaver | retained (or, at the founder's election, offered for sale at `[PRICE BASIS]`) | **vest in full** `[OPTION]` / lapse `[OPTION]` |
> | Neutral Leaver | retained | lapse and are cancelled, or are subject to the company's **call option** at `[PRICE BASIS]` |
> | Bad Leaver | subject to the company's call option at `[NOMINAL / LOWER OF COST AND FAIR VALUE — COUNSEL TO CONFIRM THAT THIS IS NOT A PENALTY]` | lapse and are cancelled **without payment** |
>
> **X.4 Solvency constraint. [MANDATORY.]** No repurchase, redemption or payment under this clause may be
> made unless the company can lawfully do so and the directors have made the solvency determination required
> by law (`D24` §6). **Where payment cannot lawfully be made, the obligation is suspended, and the parties
> shall agree an alternative (a deferred payment, a transfer to another buyer, or a variation) — the shares
> are not simply taken.**
>
> **X.5 Handover obligations of every leaver.** Return of property, credentials, documents and data; a
> **certificate of no retention** (`D43` §C5); completion of a written knowledge transfer; cooperation with
> reasonable transition requests; and continued compliance with `NDA-01` and the IP assignment.
>
> **X.6 No disparagement / no coercion. [BALANCED DRAFTING.]** Neither party shall make false or misleading
> statements about the other. **Nothing in this clause prevents any person from making a truthful statement
> to a regulator, a court, a law-enforcement authority, a tax authority, or in the course of a protected
> disclosure, or from reporting unlawful conduct.**
>
> **X.7 Release. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM — this clause is usually the most contested.]** **[Proposed:]** on
> completion of the separation, each party releases the other from claims arising from the founder's
> engagement, **other than**: (a) claims the law does not permit to be released; (b) claims for
> **indemnity**, contribution, or under an insurance policy; (c) claims arising from **fraud, dishonesty or a
> criminal act**; (d) statutory employment or worker rights that cannot be waived; (e) any right to
> whistleblow, report or cooperate with an authority; and (f) rights expressly preserved by the separation
> agreement.
>
> **X.8 Continuity of the founder's obligations.** The IP assignment, confidentiality, restrictive covenants
> (to the extent enforceable), and the founder's obligations in respect of **indemnities they have given**
> continue.

## 6. Disputes about a separation

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 6.1 | **First step:** the dispute ladder in `D29` §4 — written notice, a defined response period, mediation, then arbitration or court as counsel advises. **No self-help, no unilateral register changes, no withholding of undisputed pay, no lock-out from documents the person is entitled to** |
| 6.2 | **Preserve the position meanwhile**: the company may apply the restrictions that are **clearly** valid (confidentiality, IP, system access revocation) and **must not** apply a disputed call option unilaterally |
| 6.3 | **Freeze and preserve evidence** — emails, messages, the register, the decision record (`D60` §3.9 legal hold) |
| 6.4 | **Escalate** to the board and the Audit and Risk Committee; register the matter in `D71` |
| 6.5 | **Independent advice**: where the founder is a family member or a connected person, the `D72` process applies and the company takes its own advice |

## 7. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

2. The **price basis** for each class of leaver, and whether it is lawful.
3. Whether unvested shares **vest in full** for a Good Leaver or lapse.
4. Whether the company has a **call option**, and whether it is a company or a third-party option.
5. Whether **acceleration** applies on death or incapacity.
6. Whether the **release** extends to known claims only, or is broader — and counsel's view on what is
   releasable.
7. Who **decides a classification**, and the conflict rules.
