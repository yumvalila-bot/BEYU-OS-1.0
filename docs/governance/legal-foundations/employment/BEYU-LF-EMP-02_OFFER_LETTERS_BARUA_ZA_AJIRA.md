> **⚠ THE FOUR PROHIBITIONS THAT APPLY TO EVERY PAGE OF THIS DOCUMENT.**
> **1. Do not invent a person.** No name, no candidate, no position, no salary, no date, no reference number
> and no signature may appear in an offer letter until a real person and a real approved post exist. There is
> **no staff list** in this pack, and the software's employee/payroll rows are **synthetic demonstration data**.
> **2. Do not invent a term.** Salary, allowances, leave, probation and notice are `[ ]` blanks.
> **Employment law has not been researched**, so no figure may be asserted — not even "the statutory minimum".
> **3. Do not sign, and do not let anyone sign, anything the group cannot honour.** An offer letter is a
> document with legal effect once accepted.
> **4. Do not create an offer by editing this template.** The employer must be determined (`EMP-01` §2), the
> post must be approved and funded, and the authority limit must be confirmed (`D27`) **before** a letter is
> issued. **Nothing in this pack is an offer to anyone.**

## 4. Conditional-offer conditions: what may and may not be a condition

| # | May be a condition (if lawful in the jurisdiction) | May NEVER be a condition |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Verification of identity and the **right to work** | A waiver of a statutory right |
| 4.2 | Satisfactory **references** from named referees | Consent to an unlawful check, or a check the law forbids |
| 4.3 | Verification of **qualifications** required for the role | A **medical/health condition** other than fitness for the intrinsic requirements of the role, and only as the law permits and after an offer, with the DPO involved |
| 4.4 | A **sector-specific clearance** where the law requires it (e.g. a health-sector or financial-sector check) | A **bank guarantee, a deposit, a "bond" payment or a fee** from the candidate — **never** |
| 4.5 | Signature of the confidentiality, IP and policy acknowledgments | Agreement to a covenant the group knows is unenforceable, presented as valid |
| 4.6 | Availability to start on the stated date | A promise not to marry/not to have children, a pregnancy test, or any protected-characteristic condition — **never** |
| 4.7 | The post remaining approved and funded (an **internal** condition, not imposed on the candidate) | **Equity, a bonus or a promotion promised in place of pay** — never a substitute for remuneration |

| # | Step | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | The **post** is approved and funded (headcount, budget) | The approval (`D73`) |

| 5.3 | The **authority** of the signatory is confirmed within the `D27` limit | The `D73` entry |

| 5.5 | The letter is issued with the **contract and annexes** attached | The issued pack |

| 5.7 | The pre-employment conditions are **completed and evidenced before the start date** | The evidence file |
| 5.8 | The **personnel file** is opened; the policies acknowledgment, the IP assignment and the NDA are signed **on or before day one** | The file index (`D74`) |
| 5.9 | Payroll set-up is established with statutory registrations | `D69` |
| 5.10 | **No letter is back-dated**; where paperwork is late, the actual dates are recorded and the delay is noted | The file |
