### Q-T-02 — Is the Trustees' Incorporation Act (Cap. 318) route appropriate for a *private* family trust, or should a different structure be used?

- **Why it matters:** you have specifically been asked to investigate this. Cap. 318 is the route
  most visibly associated with "trusts" in Tanzania, but the text we read is framed around trustees
  appointed by a body/association of persons bound together by **custom, religion, kinship or
  nationality**, or established for **religious, educational, literary, scientific, social or
  charitable** purposes — and it contains a **compulsory incorporation** rule for trustees holding
  property for religious/educational/literary/scientific/social or charitable purposes.
- **Sub-questions:**
  - **Q-T-02(a)** Does a private family trust whose purpose is family asset-holding and
    multigenerational benefit fall within the Act's scope at all?
  - **Q-T-02(b)** Does `s.4` (compulsory incorporation for charitable etc. purposes) apply to such a
    trust? We record this as **undetermined** (`DAA-TRUST-009`).
  - **Q-T-02(c)** What is the **current consolidated revision** (R.E. 2002? R.E. 2019? later?) and the
    current **section numbering**? We found the numbering shift between retrieved revisions
    (e.g. application/incorporation sections appearing as 2/3 in one revision and 3/4 in another).
  - **Q-T-02(d)** Confirm `s.1A`'s exclusion list (NGOs under Cap. 56; companies under Cap. 212;
    societies under Cap. 337) and its consequences for a group that also intends to register
    companies and a nonprofit.
  - **Q-T-02(e)** Confirm the **name requirement** (`s.6(2)` — the words "Registered Trustees") and
    the **residency requirement** (`s.15/16` — a sole trustee, or not fewer than two of several
    trustees, ordinarily resident in Tanzania).
  - **Q-T-02(f)** Confirm the **liability position** (`ss.13/14` as read — trustees remain chargeable
    notwithstanding incorporation) and therefore the outer limit of any indemnity clause we draft.
  - **Q-T-02(g)** Confirm the **notification/returns regime** and periods (change of trustees within
    one month; returns of trustees; notification of change of trust) and the **current fee schedule**

  - **Q-T-02(h)** Confirm the **consequences of revocation** (as read: property vests in the
