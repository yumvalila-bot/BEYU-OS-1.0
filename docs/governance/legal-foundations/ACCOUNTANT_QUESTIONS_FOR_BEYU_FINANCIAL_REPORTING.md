## 9. The rule for recording answers

**When an answer is received, it is recorded with: the question, the answer, the person who gave it, the date,
the jurisdiction and entity it applies to, and the document(s) it changes.** **An oral answer is recorded in
writing and confirmed.** **Answers are not assumed to apply to a different entity, a different jurisdiction or a
later period** — and where the law changes, the question is re-asked.
**And: an answer to a tax question is not legal advice on the structure, and an accountant's answer does not
validate the legal existence of the entity it is about. The two sets of questions are asked separately
(`LEGAL_QUESTIONS_FOR_TANZANIAN_COUNSEL.md`) and answered separately.**
