> **⚠ THE NOTES ARE NOT OPTIONAL, AND THEY ARE NOT DECORATION.**
> **The notes carry the disclosures that make the numbers understandable and honest**: the framework applied,
> the judgements made, the related-party transactions, the commitments, the contingencies, the going-concern
> basis, and the events after the reporting date. **A set of statements without adequate notes is misleading
> even when every figure is correct.**
> **And for this group the notes are where the structural facts must appear**: that ownership is unverified,
> that no share issue has been evidenced, that the group's related-party and family transactions exist, and
> that the accounting policy is not ratified. **These are not embarrassments to be omitted — they are the
> disclosures that keep the statements true.**

## 1. Note structure (each note blank until the statements exist)

| Note | Content | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **N1** | **Basis of preparation** — entity, period, currency, framework and its version, going-concern basis, comparatives, rounding | **The framework is not ratified (`C-1`) — so this note states that, or the statements are not issued** |
| **N2** | **Significant accounting policies** — revenue, costs, depreciation, leases, inventory, receivables, FX, tax, provisions, equity | With the **policy source** (`D69`; `docs/finance` was **not** ratified) |
| **N3** | **Judgements, estimates and assumptions** — the areas with the most judgement (receivables provisioning, depreciation lives, provisions, going concern, valuation) | Each stated, with the sensitivity where material |
| **N4** | **Revenue** — disaggregated by stream, customer category and geography; the recognition basis; **grants shown separately, with their restrictions** | A "revenue" total with no breakdown conceals concentration risk |
| **N5** | **Segments** — if the entity operates distinct activities (and if the framework requires it) | Consistency with how the board actually reviews the business |
| **N6** | **Staff costs** — total, and the categories; **the statutory contributions**; the average headcount | **Employment law is unresearched — so the on-costs are `ACCOUNTANT QUESTION`; the figures follow once confirmed** |
| **N7** | **Depreciation and amortisation** — by class, with the policy | Ties to the fixed-asset register |
| **N8** | **Fixed assets** — opening cost, additions, disposals, depreciation, closing; and any **encumbrance** | Including assets held under a lease, and any asset subject to security |
| **N9** | **Intangible assets** — software, capitalised development, acquired IP; with the **basis on which the group claims to own it** | **Cross-reference `D40`/`IP-TM-01`: zero rights verified. An intangible asset recognised without a chain of title is a finding** |
| **N10** | **Inventory** — categories, valuation, and any writedown | With the count basis |
| **N11** | **Receivables** — ageing, concentration, and the provision; **related-party receivables shown separately** | A receivable from a family member or a related entity is disclosed, not netted |
| **N12** | **Payables and accruals** — including statutory amounts and staff costs |
| **N13** | **Loans and borrowings** — lender, terms, interest, maturity, covenants, **security given**, and **related-party loans separately** | **And whether any loan was made without documentation — which would be a finding** |
| **N14** | **Leases** — commitments, terms, and the framework's treatment | |
| **N15** | **Tax** — the current and deferred position, the **reconciliation** between the accounting result and the tax charge, and the **unrecognised items** | **Only with the accountant. No rate, threshold or exemption may be assumed** |
| **N16** | **Equity** — share capital (classes and numbers **per the register**), reserves, dividends | **State plainly that no share issue is evidenced (`CAP-01`) — do not present zero as understood capital** |
| **N17** | **Share-based payments** — the arrangements, the numbers, the assumptions and the expense | **No plan is adopted (`ESOP-01`) — so this note says so** |
| **N18** | **Related-party transactions** — the parties, the relationships, the transactions, the balances, the terms and the **approvals**; **remuneration of key management** | `D72`; `D14`; `D25` §6. **A related-party transaction without a recorded approval is disclosed as a finding** |
| **N19** | **Commitments and contingencies** — capital commitments, purchase obligations, guarantees given, claims and litigation (`D71`), contingent liabilities | **A known claim omitted from this note is a serious defect** |
| **N20** | **Going concern** — the assessment, the period, the assumptions, the mitigating factors and the conclusion | **The first question any accountant or lender asks** |
| **N21** | **Events after the reporting date** — everything material that occurred between the period end and the approval of the statements | **Including a share issue, a loss of a key customer, a funding decision, a legal claim, or the discovery of a defect in the records** |
| **N22** | **Group structure and consolidation** — the entities, the ownership percentages (**only if verified**), the control basis, and what is included or excluded | **`CAP-01`: ownership is unverified — so this note must say that rather than imply a group structure that has not been established** |
| **N23** | **Financial instruments and risk** — currency, credit, liquidity, interest-rate exposure; and the **concentration** of customers, suppliers and funding | Where the framework requires it |
| **N24** | **Compliance and regulatory** — licences held, obligations outstanding, filings made, and **any known non-compliance** | `CMP-01`. **A material non-compliance omitted from this note is a defect; and no compliance may be claimed without evidence** |
| **N25** | **Data protection** — where material (a data breach with a financial consequence; a processor arrangement; a fine) | `PRIV-02` §8, `D71` §3. **And no personal data beyond what is necessary** |
| **N26** | **Approval** — who approved the statements, and when | **An unapproved statement is a draft** |

## 2. The disclosure rules (the ones that matter most for this group)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Disclose, do not imply.** Where a fact that a reader would assume is **not true** (e.g. "the group owns its subsidiaries", "the shares are issued", "the IP is registered"), **the note must say so expressly** |
| 2.2 | **The registers are the source.** Entity and corporate notes come from `D63`/`D64`/`D65`/`D66`; related parties from `D72`; claims from `D71`; obligations from `CMP-01` |
| 2.3 | **Nothing may be disclosed that is not evidenced.** A note may not assert that a filing was made, a licence held or a registration obtained unless the evidence exists — **and where the group claims none, the note says so** |
| 2.4 | **Personal data in the notes is minimised.** Related-party disclosure names the **relationship**, not the private financial affairs of a family member (`PRIV-02` §2K). **Where the framework requires naming a person, the note does so — and the note is `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED`** |
| 2.5 | **A change in a note from a prior period is explained.** Notes are not silently edited |
| 2.6 | **Third-party and family arrangements are not omitted for delicacy.** A loan from a family member, a property used rent-free, a service provided by a family entity — **each is a disclosure, and each is a finding if it was never documented** |
| 2.7 | **No assurance language.** No note may say "in our opinion the accounts are correct" unless that is the accountant's signed statement — and an accountant's opinion exists only when it has been issued |

## 3. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Which entity's notes** these are, and whether consolidated notes are required.
2. Whether the family will accept **full related-party disclosure** — and it must, where the framework requires
   it; the alternative is not "omit", it is "organise the affairs differently so the transaction does not
   exist".
3. Who **prepares** the corporate and related-party notes (the Secretary), and who reviews them.
4. The **accounting policy** decisions the notes must describe (with the accountant).
