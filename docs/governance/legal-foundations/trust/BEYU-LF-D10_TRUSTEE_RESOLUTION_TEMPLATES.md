## 2. Universal drafting rules for every completed form

1. **Named humans only.** Every signature is by an identified natural person (or a body corporate through
   an identified authorised signatory). **No AI, system, bot, workflow or "system account" may sign,
   approve or attest anything.**
2. **Cite the power.** Every decision states the clause/instrument that gives the power. A decision
   without a cited power is **invalid as a record** and must be re-taken.
3. **Reasons are mandatory.** State why, what was considered, what advice was obtained, and what was
   rejected. "Noted and approved" is a defective record.
4. **Declare conflicts.** Every participant's conflicts are stated, even if "none".
5. **Evidence attached.** Attach the document relied on. Where a document does not exist, say so — do not
   invent a reference number.
6. **One date.** Use the actual date of signature. **Back-dating is prohibited**; if a document must record
   an earlier decision, use a "confirmatory" heading and state the true history.
7. **Numbers in words and figures** for amounts; state the currency.
8. **No placeholders in an executed document.** Every `[…]` must be completed before signature.
9. **Retention.** The executed original goes to `D74`; the copy and metadata go to the relevant register.
10. **Register update is part of the act.** A decision is not complete until the registers it affects are
    updated (the form names them).
