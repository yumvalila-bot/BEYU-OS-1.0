## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** To define: (a) how the Trust instrument may be amended; (b) how interests and class rights may be
varied; (c) how the Trust terminates and how the Trust Fund is distributed; (d) what happens on
deadlock, dispute or challenge; (e) what may **never** be done by any of those routes.

**1.2** Applies to Trustees, the Protector, Beneficiaries, the Family Council (in its consultation role),
the Company Secretary function and advisers.

## 2. Amendment of the Trust instrument

### 2.1 Classification of amendments

| Type | Definition | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Administrative** | Correcting a typographical error, an address, an adviser's name, a schedule reference; no change to rights, powers, classes or assets | Trustees by written decision; Protector notified |
| **B — Operational** | Changing a procedure (reporting frequency, meeting mechanics, delegation parameters) within existing powers; no change to the Reserved Matters list or to rights | Trustees + Protector consent (RM-01 as drafted) |
| **C — Substantive** | Any change to powers, duties, Trustee composition rules, the Reserved Matters list, distribution parameters, thresholds, or the Protector's role | Trustees + Protector + `[SUPERMAJORITY]` Beneficiary approval as `D04` §7 requires; counsel must review the draft |
| **D — Fundamental** | Changing the Beneficiary Classes; changing the governing law or jurisdiction; removing an entrenched clause (`D02` cl. 20.2); changing the Trust's purpose; changing the termination provisions | **Highest approval available under the instrument**, plus counsel, plus (where minors, unborn or incapacitated Beneficiaries are affected) **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` whether court sanction is required** |

### 2.2 Mandatory procedure for any amendment

1. **Identify the type** and therefore the required consents.
2. **Obtain advice** from counsel on legality, effect on class rights, tax consequences, and the effect on
   any related instrument (articles, shareholders' agreement, will, policy).
3. **Prepare a deed of amendment** (never a policy document, never a software edit).
4. **Test protectively**: does the amendment (a) breach `D02` cl. 8? (b) defeat creditors? (c) remove an
   entrenched protection? (d) undermine a Beneficiary's accrued right? (e) conflict with a mandatory rule
   of law? If yes to any — **stop**.
5. **Obtain consents** in writing, each signed by a competent person; record any person who could not
   consent (minor/unborn/incapacitated) and how their interest was protected.
6. **Execute** with the formalities the law requires (`Q-R-02`); hold the original in `D74`.
7. **Notify** whomever the route requires (regulator, if applicable) within the statutory period.
8. **Record** in `D73` and update every affected register, document and software reference.
9. **Communicate** to affected persons, and to the family in the manner the Family Constitution's
   governance provides (`D15` communication standards).

### 2.3 Prohibited amendments (absolute)

No amendment may: (a) authorise a breach of the prohibitions in `D02` cl. 8; (b) remove the AI
prohibition in `D02` cl. 7.4 without the **fundamental** approval and counsel's advice; (c) make Trust
assets available to non-Beneficiaries other than per `D02` cl. 10.6; (d) retrospectively validate an act
that was a breach of trust; (e) defeat a creditor or court order; (f) remove the requirement of a
**written, executed** instrument for any amendment; (g) be effected by a software change, a policy change,
a board resolution of any company, or a family resolution.

### 2.4 Amendment versus "interpretation"

If the Trustees believe the instrument's meaning is doubtful, that is **not** an amendment. The correct
route is: obtain counsel's advice; if the doubt persists and the matter is material, apply to the court for
directions (`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` the procedure). **A convenient reinterpretation recorded in a policy
document is a breach.**

## 3. Variation of class rights and beneficial interests

> **CROSS-REFERENCE (revision 1.1).** Dealings with a Beneficiary's interest, and the Trustees' response to
> them, are governed by **`D02` §11A (spendthrift / alienation and creditors)**, which distinguishes
> discretionary interests from vested and already-distributed entitlements and expressly preserves creditors'
> rights, court orders and mandatory family provision. See `BEYU-LF-TC-01` §2.

**3.1 Context.** Where the Trust holds shares in a company, a variation of class rights in that company is
a **company-law** matter with its own procedure, including (as read in the Companies Act) a right for a
significant minority of the affected class to petition the court within a short period, with the variation
taking effect only if confirmed. **The pack asserts no period or percentage as verified (`Q-H-01(f)`).**

**3.2 Matrix.**

| What is varied | Which regime | Approval | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company class rights (e.g. the 1,000-vote class) | Companies Act + articles | Per the Act + the articles' variation clause; counsel to draft the article | Do **not** use this document alone |
| Company articles generally | Companies Act | Special resolution (per the Act) | — |
| Shareholders' agreement | Contract law | Per the agreement's own variation clause | — |
| Trust beneficial interests (classes, entitlements) | Trust law + instrument | `D04` §7 RM-02 | — |
| Family Constitution | Family governance | Per `D11` — **does not touch legal instruments** | — |
| This document | Trustees + Protector | `D04` §7 RM-01 | — |

**3.3 Rule.** A variation must be effected **in the correct regime**. Changing a company's class rights by
amending the trust instrument (or vice versa) is ineffective. **This is a common and expensive error.**

**3.4 Protection of non-consenting interests.** No variation may extinguish an accrued beneficial
entitlement, or alter a class's rights, without the approvals that instrument requires — and, where a
Beneficiary is a minor, unborn or lacking capacity, without the protection counsel requires.

## 4. Deadlock: within the Trustees, and between the Trustees and the Protector

**4.1 Trustee deadlock procedure (escalating, time-boxed).**

| Step | Action | Time-box | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record the disagreement in writing: the matter, each position, the power in issue, and why resolution matters | Immediately | Minute + memo in `D73` |
| 2 | Refer to the Protector, who may require independent advice at the Trust's expense (within the cap) | `[NUMBER]` days | Protector's direction; adviser's written opinion |
| 3 | Structured mediation (see §6) with a mediator **experienced in trusts and family enterprises** | `[NUMBER]` days | Mediation agreement and record |
| 4 | If unresolved: apply to the court for directions, or (if the instrument permits) refer to arbitration | Immediate | Court/arbitration filing |
| 5 | **Interim protection**: the Trustees may take only the acts necessary to preserve the Trust Fund, comply with law, and avoid loss; each is recorded and reported | Continuous | Interim decision log |

**4.2 Non-escalation rule.** A deadlock must **never** be resolved by: (a) one party acting unilaterally
without power; (b) a software tie-breaker, automation, or AI decision; (c) simply doing nothing until an
emergency forces a decision (**the single most damaging failure mode in family trusts**); or (d) resigning
in a way that leaves the Trust below its minimum trustee number (`D03` §7).

**4.3 Casting vote.** If the instrument gives the Protector a casting or decisive power, that is the
instrument's choice; but the **legal validity** of such a power is a `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` item, and any
exercise must be in writing with reasons.

**4.4 Deadlock between Trustees and the Protector.** Where the Protector blocks a Reserved Matter the
Trustees consider essential: (a) the Trustees must record the reasons for the block in whatever form the
Protector provides, or, if the Protector gives none, record that no reasons were given; (b) the matter
escalates to §4.1 step 3 (mediation), and if unresolved to court; (c) the Trust must not act in breach of
the Reserved Matter. **If the Protector is unreachable for `[PERIOD]`, the instrument's fallback
(`D02` cl. 17.1) applies — and if there is no fallback, the matter goes to court.**

**4.5 Deadlock in the family (Family Council, Family Assembly).** Governed by `D13`/`D17`. **A family
deadlock does not block the Trustees' legal duties**; the Trustees must continue to administer the Trust
and may need to inform the family that a family-level disagreement has no legal effect on the Trust.

## 5. Termination of the Trust

### 5.1 Grounds

| Ground | Trigger | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed date / perpetuity limit** | The date or period in `D02` cl. 21.1 | Preparation of the final distribution plan; `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` any Tanzanian limit |
| **Trustee decision** | The Trustees, with the `D04` §7 approvals | Deed of termination; final accounts; distribution plan; notices |
| **Court order** | An order of a competent court | Compliance; counsel to advise |
| **Impossibility / failure of purpose** | The purposes can no longer be carried out | Counsel + court directions likely required; **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` whether any statutory cy-près-type rule applies** |
| **Regulator action (incorporated route)** | If the incorporation is revoked/suspended as read | Immediate counsel; the consequences as read are severe (property vesting in the Administrator-General as Public Trustee) — **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** |

### 5.2 Mandatory procedure

1. **Counsel instructed** — termination is a legal transaction, not a bookkeeping event.
2. **Final accounts** prepared, and audited if the route or the Protector requires.
3. **Distribution plan** prepared with tax and execution mechanics per asset class (`D06`).
4. **Approvals** obtained per `D04` §7 RM-03.
5. **Protective test**: does the plan prejudice a minor/unborn/incapacitated Beneficiary; defeat a
   creditor; breach a court order; or misapply a charitable asset (if any)? If yes — reconsider, or apply
   for court directions.
6. **Deed of termination** executed; original held in `D74`.
7. **Assets transferred** per `D06` (each asset class, each formality).
8. **Regulator/bank/counterparty notifications** completed; accounts closed.
9. **Final report** to the Protector and Beneficiaries.
10. **Records retained** after termination for the required period; **the beneficiary and distribution
    records are not destroyed on termination** (a former Beneficiary may need them; a tax authority may
    ask).

### 5.3 Prohibited on termination

(a) Distributing to a person who is not a Beneficiary (except per an approved final plan and applicable
law); (b) distributing before the accounts are done; (c) treating "the trust is over" as a reason to skip
tax or filing obligations; (d) distributing assets whose title was never properly vested in the Trust
(those assets belong to their actual owner and must be dealt with accordingly); (e) any distribution on
the basis of an unreconciled balance.

## 6. Dispute resolution

> **CROSS-REFERENCE (revision 1.1).** **`D02` §20A** now contains the "challenges and disputes" clause,
> including the **mandatory carve-outs** (`20A.1`), the **preservation of every person's right to approach a
> court, a regulator or an authority** (`20A.2`), and a **family-procedure-first request** (`20A.4`) that is
> **not a condition of any legal right**. The consequences sub-clause (`20A.5`) is expressly
> `[COUNSEL TO SETTLE]` and is severable (`D02` §23.6). **No procedure in this section may be operated so as
> to delay, condition or penalise the exercise of a legal right.** See `BEYU-LF-TC-01` §3.

### 6.1 Escalation ladder (default, to be reflected in the instrument)

| Stage | Forum | Scope | Binding? | Time-box |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Direct discussion** between the parties, with a written note of the issue | Any dispute | No | `[NUMBER]` days |
| 2 | **Trustee/Protector review** — the matter is put to the Trustees, and to the Protector if a Reserved Matter or a governance matter is involved | Trust administration issues | No | `[NUMBER]` days |
| 3 | **Family mediation** per `D17` (an accredited/experienced mediator; for family-relationship disputes) | Family and trust disputes | No, unless the parties sign a settlement | `[NUMBER]` days |
| 4 | **Expert determination** on a technical matter (valuation, accounting, actuarial) by an agreed expert | Valuation/accounting disputes | Yes, if the instrument so provides — **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** | `[NUMBER]` days |
| 5 | **Arbitration** — only if the instrument provides for it and only for arbitrable matters | Contractual/commercial disputes | Yes | Per rules |
| 6 | **Court** — the High Court or other competent court | All matters, and **always** available for the matters in §6.2 | Yes | — |

### 6.2 Matters that should **not** be sent to arbitration (and where court is the right forum)

Trust administration issues involving minors, unborn persons, incapacity, the construction of the
instrument, the removal of a Trustee for breach of fiduciary duty, variation of beneficial interests, and
any matter where a person who cannot consent needs protection. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` exactly which
matters are arbitrable in Tanzania.**

### 6.3 Rules for any dispute

1. **The Trust continues to be administered** during a dispute. Trustees must not use the dispute as a
   reason to stop keeping accounts, filing obligations, or paying debts (**except** where a court order or
   a genuine deadlock on a Reserved Matter requires otherwise, in which case the Trustees must record the
   reason).
2. **Legal costs** are paid from the Trust Fund only where the Trustee acted properly and the expense was
   properly incurred; a Trustee's personal defence costs must be **segregated and tracked**, and repaid if
   the Trustee is found in breach.
3. **Confidentiality** applies to the dispute and its documents; publication is prohibited unless the law or
   a court requires it (and then only after counsel's advice).
4. **Records are preserved** under legal hold (`D08` §8.1).
5. **No adverse treatment of a Beneficiary for raising a dispute in good faith.** Any consequence for
   challenging a Trustee act must be justified, recorded, and (per `D03` §5.5) subject to counsel's
   confirmation that it is lawful.
6. **Reporting**: every dispute is recorded in `D71` (litigation/claims) and reported in the annual
   trustee report.

### 6.4 Disputes involving the family (as distinct from the Trust)

Family-constitution disputes (allocation of roles, consultation, family-business participation, family
business conduct) are governed by `D13` and `D17`. **A family dispute does not give any family body power
over the Trust.** Where a dispute involves a Beneficiary's status or distribution expectations, the
subject matter is Trust administration and this document governs.

### 6.5 Disputes with third parties

Litigation/arbitration above `[LITIGATION THRESHOLD]` is a Reserved Matter (`D04` RM-10). Settlement above
`[CLAIM THRESHOLD]` is RM-17. **No settlement may be agreed that would require the Trust to do something
prohibited by `D02` cl. 8**, and no settlement may be funded from the Foundation's assets (that is a
separate body with its own purpose, `D51`).

## 7. Emergency interface

Acts taken under emergency authority (`D56`, `D62`) are **not** amendments, variations or terminations.
They are time-boxed, reportable, and must be reviewed at the next competent meeting, where they are either
ratified or corrected. **Emergency authority can never be used to amend the instrument, change class
rights, terminate the Trust, remove a Trustee, or approve a Reserved Matter.**
