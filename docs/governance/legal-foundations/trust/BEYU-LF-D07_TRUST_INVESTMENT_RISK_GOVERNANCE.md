## 1. Purpose, principles and limits of this policy

**1.1 Purpose.** To define what the Trust may hold, how risk is governed, who decides, how custody is
handled, how values are recorded, and what must never be presented as an asset.

**1.2 Principles.**

1. **The Trustees decide.** The Family Office, advisers and software **prepare and recommend**; they do
   not decide (see `D04` §2.3).
2. **Stewardship over speculation.** The purpose of the Trust is multigenerational benefit; the policy
   is written for durability, not for maximum short-term return.
3. **Documented mandate.** No discretionary management without a written mandate with limits, reporting
   and revocation (`D36`).
4. **Separation of duties.** Decision, execution, custody and accounting are performed by different
   people.
5. **Asset class discipline.** An asset may be acquired **only** if its class is permitted below and
   the acquisition has passed §5.
6. **No hidden leverage.** Leverage is a Reserved Matter (`D04` RM-05, RM-06).
7. **Concentration is a governance issue, not merely a market issue.**

**1.3 What this policy does not do.** It does not state any lawful investment standard, and it does not
assert that any Tanzanian statutory investment duty applies to the Trustees. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`**
whether a prudent-investment or trustee-investment standard exists in the applicable law and, if so,
whether this policy must be conformed to it.

## 5. Acquisition, disposal and valuation discipline

**5.1 Before acquisition, the following must exist:** (a) the class is permitted (§2); (b) the limits are
met on a **post-trade** basis; (c) due diligence is on file; (d) a conflict check is done; (e) tax advice
where material; (f) for any related-party acquisition, the `D04` RM-09 process; (g) for any cross-border
acquisition, the §2 A12 preconditions.

**5.2 Before disposal:** (a) authority confirmed; (b) for a **controlling interest** in the Holding Body,
RM-08; (c) tax consequences obtained; (d) proceeds routing confirmed through the accounting authority;
(e) counterparty checks.

**5.3 Valuation rules.**

| Class | Valuation basis | Frequency | Source required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash/deposits | Statement balance | Monthly | Bank statement |
| Listed securities | Market price on a stated date | Monthly | Exchange/ broker statement |
| Unlisted equity (A1, A7) | `[VALUATION METHOD — USER TO SUPPLY]` with the basis recorded | At least annually | Independent valuation or a documented internal basis with assumptions |
| Real property | `[VALUATION METHOD]` | At least every `[PERIOD]` or on a material event | Valuer's report |
| Funds | Published NAV | Per reporting cycle | Fund statement |
| Insurance | **Not valued** (contingent) | — | Policy schedule |

**5.4 No inflated or invented values.** A valuation must never be recorded unless the source document is
held. **Placeholder or demonstration values must be labelled as such and excluded from every total.**
Where a value is uncertain, record a **range** and mark it `UNVERIFIED`.

**5.5 Impairment.** Where an asset's value has fallen materially or its recoverability is in doubt, the
Trustees must decide (and record) whether to impair it. **A trust must not carry a dead asset at face
value to avoid an awkward decision.**

## 6. Custody, title and separation

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| C-01 | Trust assets must be registered/held in the **Trustees'** name (or the incorporated body's name), not in a Family Office, family-member, employee or adviser name. |
| C-02 | Where a nominee or custodian is used, it must be documented, the Trust must retain all beneficial rights, and the arrangement must be recorded in `D67` with the nominee's identity and the reason. |
| C-03 | The Family Office **may** hold custody records, but **never** assets, and never in a way that suggests ownership. |
| C-04 | Documents of title (certificates, title deeds, policy documents) are held in a **documented physical/digital vault** with a register of what is held where (`D74`), and never only in a cloud folder with no access control. |
| C-05 | Bank/custody mandates require **two** authorised signatories and are revised on every trustee change (`D03` §9). |
| C-06 | No commingling with personal funds, with the Foundation's funds, with a company's funds, or with the Family Office's operating funds. |
| C-07 | Access to custody accounts and vaults is logged; every movement is reconciled at least monthly by a person who does not execute the movement. |

## 7. Performance reporting (to the Trustees, and by them to the family)

**7.1** Reports must state: holdings by class with valuation basis and date; movements with reasons;
policy compliance (each limit L-01…L-12); breaches and remediation; fees paid to each adviser; conflicts
disclosed; and known uncertainties. **Reports must be reconcilable to the accounting records.**

**7.2** A report must never mix: (a) Trust assets; (b) Family Office operating costs; (c) Foundation
assets; (d) personal family assets; (e) contingent benefits. **A combined "family wealth" figure that
mixes these is prohibited** — it would misstate all of them (`D67` §Boundaries).

**7.3** Any figure that has not been reconciled is marked `UNRECONCILED` and excluded from decision
totals.
