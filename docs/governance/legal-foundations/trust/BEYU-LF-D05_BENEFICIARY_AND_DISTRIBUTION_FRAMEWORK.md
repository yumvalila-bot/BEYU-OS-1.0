## 1. Purpose, scope and the separation of concepts

**1.1 Purpose.** To define the categories, rights and processes by which a person may be considered for
and receive benefit from the Trust — and to keep four distinct concepts in strictly separate boxes.

**1.2 The four boxes (never to be conflated):**

| Box | What it is | Who decides | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Family membership** | Whether a person is within the family for family-governance purposes | Family per `D11`–`D13` (unratified decision — `FIR-003`) | Family record + family decision |
| **2. Beneficiary class membership** | Whether a person falls within a Beneficiary Class in the Trust instrument | **Trustees**, on the instrument's definition + legal advice | Trustee determination, written and recorded (`D73`) |
| **3. Entitlement / expectation** | Whether the person may expect or request a distribution | **Trustees' discretion** (subject to Reserved Matters) | Trustee decision |
| **4. Payment** | The actual transfer of value | Trustees decide; accounting records it | Trust decision + accounting record in the sole accounting source of truth |

**1.3 The rule.** Being in box 1 does not put you in box 2. Being in box 2 does not give you a right to
box 3. Being in box 3 does not generate box 4 automatically. **A person may be in box 1 and never
receive anything from the Trust; a person outside box 1 may be a beneficiary of the Trust if the
instrument's class definition includes them. The two records are separate and must be separately
governed.**

**1.4 Why this matters.** The repository contains (a) a beneficiary-registry model with demonstration
data, and (b) family-governance machinery. If either is presented as if it conferred an entitlement,
the family will be misled about their legal position and the Trustees will be distracted from their
duty. **No software record, registry entry, family resolution or AI output creates a beneficial
entitlement.** (`FIR-009`, `FIR-010`.)

| **G — Entities** | `[e.g. a family trust or foundation]` | Whether a body corporate may be a beneficiary | ☐ |

### 2.2 Definition discipline

Every class must satisfy four drafting tests, each to be confirmed by counsel:

1. **Certainty of objects** — can the Trustees determine, from the definition, whether a given person is
   in or out?
2. **Administrability** — can the class be closed or managed as generations pass?
3. **Non-arbitrariness** — the definition may not make the trust illusory or a mere power.
4. **Lawful scope** — no class may include an unlawful purpose or one that breaches a mandatory rule.

### 2.3 The family-definition dependency (unratified)

The family-definition questions are **open** in the BEYU programme and are **not** answered here:

| **Assisted reproduction / surrogacy** | Determines Class A | Not yet decided |

## 3. Rights, expectations and non-rights

### 3.1 The rights table

| # | What a Beneficiary **may** have | Extent | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | To be considered for a distribution | A Beneficiary within a class may be considered | The instrument (cl. 10.1) |
| R-02 | To receive a distribution properly made | Once made, the recipient holds it free of the Trust (subject to clawback if unlawfully made) | The instrument |
| R-03 | To request a distribution | By the process in `D34`/Schedule 5(5) of `D02` | The instrument / practice |
| R-04 | To receive information | As the Trustees decide within the disclosure policy in `D08`, subject to privacy law and safety | The instrument; law |
| R-05 | To be heard before exclusion | Procedural protection | `D02` cl. 9.4(b) |
| R-06 | To complain / refer a dispute | Through `D17` and, if the law allows, to court | The instrument; law |
| R-07 | To seek legal protection of their interests | Cannot be excluded by the instrument | Law (`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`) |

### 3.2 The **non-rights** table — drafting discipline

| # | A Beneficiary has **no** right to | Because |
|---|---|---|
| N-01 | Any specific asset of the Trust | `D02` cl. 5.3 |
| N-02 | A distribution at any particular time, or of any particular amount | Discretionary trust |
| N-03 | Attend or vote at trustee meetings | Not a trustee |
| N-04 | Instruct the Trustees, the Family Council or the Family Office | No authority |
| N-05 | Examine the Trust's books on demand | Subject to the disclosure policy and law |
| N-06 | Control, direct or veto an investment | Not a trustee |
| N-07 | Access to another Beneficiary's personal data | Privacy law; `D70` |
| N-08 | A share of capital on termination before the Trustees (and any court) decide | `D02` cl. 21 |
| N-09 | Succession to their own position as a Beneficiary | `D02` cl. 11 |
| N-10 | Protection from their own creditors by the Trust | The Trust is not a shield against lawful claims |

### 3.3 Asset-protection honesty statement (required reading)

> The Trust is **not** an asset-protection device. A settlement made to defeat creditors, a
> distribution made under a court order, a beneficiary's insolvency, matrimonial proceedings, tax
> liabilities and statutory claims can all reach assets or value. **No clause in this pack should ever
> be described to a family member as making assets "untouchable".** Where a Beneficiary has creditors or
> is in insolvency, the Trustees must take legal advice before making or refusing a distribution.

## 4. Distribution framework

**4.1 Decision chain (no step may be skipped):**

1. **Eligibility** — is the person within a Beneficiary Class (Trustee determination, recorded)?
2. **Power** — is there a power to make this distribution (`D02` cl. 10)?
3. **Purpose** — does it fall within Schedule 3 of `D02`?
4. **Limits** — does it breach a cap, the capital floor, or RM-11?
5. **Reserved Matters** — is Protector/Beneficiary consent required (§7 of `D04`)?
6. **Conflicts** — is any Trustee conflicted; is the matter a related-party transaction?
7. **Affordability** — can the Trust pay its debts as they fall due afterwards?
8. **Tax** — what is the tax treatment for the Trust and the recipient? `[COUNSEL/TAX TO CONFIRM]`
9. **Decision** — a written trustee decision (`D10` Form 1) with reasons.
10. **Payment** — executed through the accounting authority; recorded in the sole accounting source of
    truth; never by a software-only workflow.
11. **Record** — distribution recorded in the Trust's distribution register and in `D73`.

**4.2 Criteria for discretionary distributions `[PROPOSED]` — and the honesty caveat.** The Trustees may
consider: need; purpose (education, health, housing, enterprise); the Beneficiary's other resources;
the effect on the Beneficiary's incentive and independence; the effect on the Trust Fund; prior
distributions to that Beneficiary; and the interests of other Beneficiaries. **These criteria are aids
to judgement, not a formula, and they create no entitlement.**

**4.3 Consistency and non-discrimination.** The Trustees shall record their reasons whenever they treat
Beneficiaries differently, and shall review distribution patterns annually for unexplained
inconsistency. **A pattern of unexplained favouritism is a governance risk to be reported to the
Protector.**

**4.4 Caps and floors `[USER TO SUPPLY]`.**

| Parameter | Value | Consequence if blank |
|---|---|---|
| Annual income-distribution cap as % of net income | `[USER TO SUPPLY]` | **Reserved Matter for every amount** (fail-closed) |
| Capital floor (minimum Trust Fund value preserved) | `[USER TO SUPPLY]` | Same |
| Large-distribution threshold (RM-11) | `[USER TO SUPPLY]` | Same |
| Education cap per Beneficiary per year | `[USER TO SUPPLY]` | Same |
| Health/emergency cap | `[USER TO SUPPLY]` | Same |

**4.5 Emergency distributions.** A Trustee may make an urgent distribution under `D62` only to relieve
**immediate** risk to a Beneficiary's health, safety or shelter, within `[EMERGENCY CAP]`, with two
Trustees agreeing (one of whom must not be related to the Beneficiary) and a written record within
**[NUMBER]** hours and full reporting at the next meeting. **Urgency never removes the prohibitions in
`D02` cl. 8.**

**4.6 Loans, guarantees and facilities to Beneficiaries.** Any loan, advance, guarantee or business
facility to or for a Beneficiary must be: (a) documented (`D34` template); (b) approved as a Reserved
Matter above `[BENEFICIARY FACILITY THRESHOLD]`; (c) on terms the Trustees record as fair; and
(d) recorded in `D67` (asset/liability) and `D72` (related party). **The Trustees must consider
whether an unpaid facility should be treated as an early distribution — `[COUNSEL/TAX TO CONFIRM]`.**

**4.7 Clawback `[PROPOSED]` `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`.** If a distribution was made: in breach of the
instrument; without a required consent; on the basis of materially false information; or when the
Trustee was conflicted without disclosure — the Trustees shall seek recovery, and shall record the
steps taken. Whether recovery is available in law is for counsel.

## 5. Beneficiary Register rules (`D65` operational detail)

**5.1 Purpose.** To allow the Trustees to administer the classes correctly, **not** to create
entitlement.

**5.2 Contents (minimum).** Party reference (GlobalUserID); class; basis of inclusion; date determined;
determining body; meeting/decision reference; evidence reference; status (member / excluded /
conditional); exclusion ground if any; capacity flag (minor / lacks capacity); date of review.

**5.3 Access.** `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` — Trustees, Protector, Company Secretary function, auditor on
scope. **Never** in a public or open repository. **Never** in a location where a beneficiary can see
another beneficiary's data.

**5.4 Minors.** A minor's record is limited to what is necessary; no unnecessary personal data (no
school records, medical details, or financial account numbers beyond what the Trustees need).
`D70` §Minors applies.

**5.5 Disputes.** A contested class-membership determination is recorded as contested; no entry may be
deleted. The dispute follows `D17`.

**5.6 Review.** Reviewed at least annually before the distribution cycle, and immediately on: a birth
or adoption; a marriage or divorce; a death; a court order; a migration change affecting a person's
status; or an application to be considered.

**5.7 Certification.** The Trustees shall, at least annually, certify the register's completeness based on
the information reasonably available to them, and record **what they do not know** (e.g. births not
reported to them). **An uncertified register is not relied on for a distribution decision.**

## 6. Interaction with family governance (`D11`–`D19`) and the Family Office

**6.1 Family Council may recommend, not decide.** The Family Council may recommend a distribution to a
Beneficiary **in general terms**; it may not approve one, condition one, or direct its amount.

**6.2 Family support framework.** Family-level support (education funds, hardship support, enterprise
support, emergency relief) is documented in `D16` (family support and education framework). Where a
family-support payment is intended to come from the Trust, it must run the §4.1 chain. Where it is
funded by a company (e.g. a group company's employee-family policy) it is not a trust distribution and
must be documented as what it is.

**6.3 Family Office role.** The Family Office may: prepare applications; verify documents; maintain
records under instruction; prepare analysis; and coordinate advisers. It may **not**: determine class
membership; approve a distribution; process a payment outside the accounting authority; or hold
Beneficiary data outside the governed registers.

**6.4 Reporting to the family.** The Trustees shall report annually on: total distributions; number of
Beneficiaries considered; distribution policy compliance; any refusal and its reasons (in
non-identifying aggregate where privacy requires); and any change in the classes. `D08` governs the
format.

## 7. Interaction with the control plane

**7.1** The repository's `beneficiaries` / family registry models and the `trust_distributions` model
are **records with `authoritative_owner = FINANCE_OS`** and, as delivered, have
`legal_review_status = REQUIRES_LEGAL_REVIEW`. **`FIR-010` remains open.** Nothing here changes that.

**7.2 Mapping (record-keeping only).**

| This document | Control-plane record | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Class membership determination | A beneficiary-registry entry with `decision_ref` | The entry is a **record** of the Trustees' determination |
| Distribution decision | A `trust_distributions` record | The record follows the executed decision; it does not authorise it |
| Entitlement | **No field** | Never encode an entitlement flag; entitlement is a trustee determination, not a data value |
| Exclusion | A status change with `ground`, `evidence_ref`, `notice_ref` | Supports the procedural protection in `D02` cl. 9.4 |
| Emergency distribution | A record with `emergency_ref` and a mandatory post-hoc review link | Must not be usable to bypass RM thresholds repeatedly |

**7.3 Forbidden usages.** (a) Any "auto-distribution" or rule-engine payment; (b) any AI-generated
eligibility decision; (c) any ledger outside the accounting authority; (d) exposing the beneficiary
register through a URL, share link or export that has no access control; (e) treating a demonstration
seed row as a real beneficiary.

## 8. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Define the Beneficiary Classes** (or instruct counsel) — the family must decide the substance and
   counsel must confirm the drafting (`FIR-003`).
2. Decide whether **unborn and after-born** persons are included.
3. Decide the treatment of **divorce, remarriage, step-relationships and dependants**.
4. Decide whether **any class of charitable purpose beneficiaries** is intended (this has structural
   consequences — see `D01` §3).
5. Supply all `[USER TO SUPPLY]` caps, floors and thresholds (§4.4).
6. Decide the **information-sharing policy** with adult Beneficiaries (`D08`).
7. Decide whether the **Family Council** may recommend distributions (optional; subject to §6.1).
8. Confirm the **F** processes: who receives an application, within what time, and who communicates the
   decision.

## 9. Counsel review note — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

1. **Certainty of objects** for each class as finally drafted; whether any class must be closed; the
   position on **unborn persons**.
2. The validity of the **"no specific asset"** clause and of any **"spendthrift"**-type clause.
3. The lawfulness of each **ground of exclusion** and the adequacy of the hearing procedure.
4. The interaction of the distribution framework with **dependants' or family-provision rights** and any
   mandatory succession rule (`Q-S-02`).
5. The **tax** treatment of distributions, of income accumulated in the Trust, of a distribution to a
   non-resident, and of a loan/facility that is later forgiven (`Q-TAX-01`).
6. Whether **clawback** is available and how to draft the recovery right.
7. Whether a Beneficiary's **request** procedure creates any enforceable right, and how to avoid
   inadvertently creating one.
8. Whether the **procedural protection before exclusion** is required, advisable, or potentially
   harmful (e.g. a hearing that delays protection of other Beneficiaries).
9. **Privacy**: whether disclosing one Beneficiary's distributions to another is lawful under the PDPA
   or requires consent (`Q-DP-04`).
10. The treatment of distributions to a **minor** or to an adult who **lacks capacity**, including
    guardianship and receipt formalities.

**This framework identifies no beneficiary and creates no entitlement or legal effect.**
