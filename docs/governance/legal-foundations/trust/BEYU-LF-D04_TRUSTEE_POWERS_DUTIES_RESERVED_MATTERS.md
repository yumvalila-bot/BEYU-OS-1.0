### 3.2 Duties owed to other parties

| Beneficiary of the duty | Duty |
|---|---|
| The Beneficiaries | The fiduciary duties above |
| The Protector | Reporting, disclosure, and compliance with the Reserved Matters process |
| Creditors and counterparties | Honesty in dealings; no misrepresentation of authority |
| Regulators (Route 2) | Filing, notification, truthful returns, compliance with directions |
| The public revenue | Correct filing and payment of taxes due |
| The court | Compliance with orders; truthful disclosure on any application |

### 3.3 What a Trustee may **not** rely on as a discharge of duty

1. A **unanimous vote of the other Trustees** (a wrong decision is wrong however many agreed).
2. **A policy document**, a board pack, a committee recommendation or a family resolution.
3. **A software record** — an approval state, an audit entry, an RBAC grant or a dashboard.
4. An **AI output**, however detailed.
5. **Advice** — advice supports a decision; it does not transfer the duty to the adviser.
6. **The absence of a complaint** from a Beneficiary.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

### 4.1 Three categories

| Category | Meaning | Regime |
|---|---|---|
| **Declared interest** | A known, disclosed and recorded interest | May participate after disclosure where immaterial, with reasons recorded |
| **Material conflict** | An interest capable of influencing the decision | Disclosure + **recusal**; cannot count towards quorum; decision recorded with the conflict noted |
| **Prohibited conflict** | An interest that cannot be managed: an undisclosed interest; a personal benefit from a related-party transaction; a transaction with an entity the Trustee controls which is not on arm's-length terms | The transaction must not proceed |

### 4.2 Procedures

| Step | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Annual disclosure** of all interests, offices and material relationships | Signed declaration (`D14` template); Register of Declared Interests |
| 2 | **Ad hoc disclosure** as soon as a matter arises | Written disclosure to the other Trustees and Protector |
| 3 | **Determination** by the non-conflicted Trustees (or the Protector if all are conflicted) whether the conflict is declared/material/prohibited | Written determination with reasons |
| 4 | **Recusal** for material and prohibited conflicts | Minutes record absence from deliberation and vote; the conflict is recorded in `D73` |
| 5 | **Independent-party test** for related-party transactions | Arm's-length written opinion (`D72`) |
| 6 | **Re-take** any decision made without required disclosure; the original is recorded as voidable, not deleted | `D73`; `D74` |

### 4.3 Standing conflicts to manage (identified; no individual is named)

| Situation | Risk | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| A Trustee is also a director of the Holding Body | The Trust's interest as shareholder may differ from the company's interest | Written disclosure; recusal on any matter where the Trust must decide how to vote as shareholder **and** the matter touches the Trustee's own performance or remuneration |
| A Trustee is also a Beneficiary | Bias toward own benefit | Independence test in `D02` §3.1; recusal from decisions materially benefiting them |
| A Trustee is also an employee/supplier of a group entity | Self-dealing risk | Related-party regime; independent approval; no participation in fee-setting |
| A Trustee is also a Foundation governor | Duty to the charitable purpose may conflict with the family's interest | Mandatory recusal on any Trust–Foundation transaction; written confirmation of which hat is being worn (`D51`) |
| A Trustee is also a Family Council member | Family pressure | `D02` cl. 18 (Family Council has no authority over the Trust) |
| A Trustee received the office from a particular family branch | Branch capture | Independence and balance requirements in `D03` |

## 5. Indemnity, exculpation and insurance

**5.1 Trustee indemnity.** Subject to §2.2 X-04, the Trustees are indemnified out of the Trust Fund
against liabilities, costs and expenses **properly incurred** in administering the Trust (§`D02`
cl. 13.4).

**5.2 No exculpation for core breaches.** Any clause purporting to excuse a Trustee from liability for
**fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence or reckless disregard of duty** is void to that extent.
Additionally, and importantly:

> **Incorporation does not shield a Trustee.** Under the Trustees' Incorporation Act route as read,
> trustees incorporated remain **chargeable and accountable for their own acts, receipts, neglects and
> defaults** in the same manner and to the same extent as if incorporation had not occurred
> (`s.13/14` as retrieved). **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** the current provision.

**5.3 Reliance defence `[PROPOSED]` `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`.** A Trustee who acts in good faith in
reliance on: (a) a written opinion of a qualified professional obtained after full disclosure of the
facts; (b) a signed certificate of a person authorised to give it; or (c) an audited account, is not
liable for anything done or omitted in that reliance **unless** the Trustee knew or ought to have known
the facts relied on were false — **but this clause does not protect a Trustee from liability for a
breach of the core duties in §3.1 that applicable law does not permit to be excluded.**

**5.4 Insurance.** The Trustees shall consider, and where the Trust assets justify it shall maintain:
(a) trustees' liability/professional-indemnity cover; (b) trust-asset insurance (property, liability);
(c) key-person cover where a key person's death or incapacity would impair the enterprise. Each policy
is recorded in `D67`/`D71`. **Insurance proceeds are contingent; cover is never reported as an asset.**

**5.5 Cost allocation.** Premiums are a Trust expense. Where a premium protects a group entity, the
allocation must follow `D53` (cost allocation) and be recorded as a related-party arrangement if the
entity is a related party.

## 6. Remuneration and expenses

**6.1 Default.** A Trustee is **not** remunerated unless the Trust instrument (Schedule 7 of `D02`)
provides for it. Schedule 7 is **empty**; therefore, as drafted, **no remuneration is payable**.

**6.2 Corporate/professional trustee.** If remunerated, the basis and rate must be in writing, approved
by the Protector, disclosed in the Related-Party Transaction Register (`D72`), and reviewed annually
against market rates.

**6.3 Lay Trustee.** No fee; reimbursement of properly incurred expenses on production of receipts,
within `[CAP]` per item unless pre-approved.

**6.4 Extraordinary services.** Reasonable extra remuneration may be paid for extraordinary services
only with the Protector's written consent, after disclosure, and recorded.

**6.5 Prohibited remuneration.** No Trustee may accept: a commission or rebate from any adviser, broker,
bank or counterparty; a fee from a person with whom the Trust transacts; or any benefit for procuring a
transaction. Any such payment received must be accounted for to the Trust Fund.

**6.6 Protector remuneration.** Only as in Schedule 7 of `D02`, which is empty.

## 8. Relationship to the BEYU control plane

**8.1** The control plane contains a `governance` model of bodies, charters, meetings, resolutions, votes
and appointments, and an `assurance` model for risk and controls. **Their role here is
record-keeping and evidence traceability.**

**8.2 Mapping (record-keeping only):**

| This schedule | Suggested control-plane record | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Trustee decision | A governance record referencing the executed decision document | A recorded resolution is **not** the decision; the executed document is |
| Reserved Matter consent | A record of the consent with `document_ref` | A workflow "APPROVED" state is not a consent |
| Conflict declaration | A conflict-declaration record attached to the decision | Recording a conflict does not manage it — recusal does |
| Delegation to a manager | A delegation/authority record with scope, limits and expiry | A software delegation is not a legal delegation of trustee powers |
| Reserved matters matrix | A configuration of the same matter list | If the software list differs from §7, §7 governs and the difference is a defect |

**8.3 Forbidden usages.** (a) Using a software approval to satisfy a Reserved Matter requirement;
(b) recording an AI recommendation as a trustee decision; (c) allowing a manager's system access to
override a non-delegable decision; (d) treating `trust_provisions.legal_effect_status = INERT` rows as
if they were effective clauses.
