- **A.** The Settlor wishes to establish a trust for the benefit of the persons described in Part 4, to

(b) the Trustees may **exclude** a person from benefit only on a ground expressly set out in

**9.6 No automatic benefit on marriage or adoption.** Marriage into the family, adoption, or any other
status change confers no benefit automatically; a person becomes a Beneficiary only if within a
Beneficiary Class as defined in Schedule 2.

### 10. Income, capital and discretionary distributions

**10.1 Discretion.** Subject to clause 10.4 and Schedule 4, the Trustees may pay or apply the income
and/or capital of the Trust Fund to or for the benefit of any one or more Beneficiaries, in such
amounts and at such times as they think fit, and may accumulate income.

**10.2 Distribution purposes.** Distributions may be made for the purposes in Schedule 3, including
maintenance, education, health, housing, a business or professional start, and hardship relief.

**10.3 Trustee duty to record reasons.** Every Distribution decision shall be recorded in a written
trustee decision under clause 15.3, stating: the Beneficiary, the amount or asset, the purpose, the
power relied on, the interests considered, any conflict declared, and the approval path. **Unrecorded
distributions are invalid as against the Trustees' duty to account.**

**10.4 Limits on distributions.** The Trustees shall not make a Distribution that would:
(a) render the Trustees unable to pay the Trust's debts as they fall due;
(b) breach a Reserved Matter (Schedule 4);
(c) benefit a person who is not a Beneficiary (other than a permitted benefit under clause 10.6);
(d) constitute a concealed return of a settlement to the Settlor or the Settlor's estate other than as
permitted by clause 9.4 or Schedule 4;
(e) breach any applicable law or court order.

**10.5 Finance authority.** Every Distribution requires: (a) a trustee decision under clause 15.3; and
(b) processing through the group's accounting authority, with the accounting record held in the
**sole accounting source of truth**. **The Trust's software records, if any, hold references only —
they neither authorise nor account for a payment.** **Distribution authorisation is a human decision;
the accounting system records it.**

**10.6 Incidental and emergency benefits.** The Trustees may apply Assets for the relief of a person
who is not a Beneficiary where that person's need arises from an emergency affecting a Beneficiary, up
to **`[INCIDENTAL LIMIT — USER TO SUPPLY]`**, with a written record and subsequent report to the
Protector. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** whether any such application is lawful and its tax treatment.

**10.7 No capital distribution on demand.** No Beneficiary may demand a capital distribution. Any
capital distribution is at the Trustees' discretion subject to Schedule 4.

### 11. Distributions on death of a Beneficiary `[PROPOSED]` `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

**11.1** On the death of a Beneficiary, that person's interest (if any) in the Trust Fund ceases unless
Schedule 2 or a separate written instrument provides otherwise. **No distribution is payable to the
deceased Beneficiary's estate or personal representatives** unless the Trustees, with the Protector's
consent, decide otherwise in a written decision.

**11.2** The Trustees shall not treat any person as a Beneficiary's successor automatically; a
successor must fall within a Beneficiary Class.

**11.3** Nothing in this clause affects **creditors' rights, court orders, or any statutory right of
dependants or family members** that is not capable of being excluded by agreement or instrument.
**`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** the interaction with Tanzanian succession and family-provision rules.

## PART 5 — DUTIES AND CONDUCT OF TRUSTEES

### 12. Trustee duties

**12.1** Each Trustee shall, in exercising the trusts of this deed:
(a) act honestly and in good faith in the interests of the Beneficiaries;
(b) exercise the care, diligence and skill that a reasonably prudent person would exercise in managing
the affairs of another;
(c) act in accordance with this deed and applicable law, and not for any collateral purpose;
(d) **act personally** — a Trustee shall not delegate fiduciary judgement except as clause 7.2 permits;
(e) keep proper accounts and records (Part 7);
(f) avoid and, where a conflict arises, disclose and manage it (Part 6);
(g) treat Beneficiaries impartially in substance, save where the purposes of Schedule 3 justify a
different treatment, and record the reason;
(h) not make any secret profit;
(i) obtain professional advice where a decision requires it, and record that they did; and
(j) not act in a manner that would improperly benefit a related party.

**12.2 Standard of liability.** A Trustee is liable for a breach of trust. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`**
the standard (which the pack does **not** characterise as "negligence", "gross negligence" or
"wilful default" in Tanzanian law). Clause 13.4 limits indemnity; it does not excuse an honest breach
that the law does not excuse.

**12.3 Trust property to be held separately.** Trust property shall be held separately from the
Trustees' own property and be identifiable as Trust property. Trustees shall not commingle the Trust
Fund with any other person's property.

**12.4 Joint action and signing authority.** Bank and custody accounts, and all payment instructions,
shall require **two authorised signatories**, being two Trustees (or one Trustee plus an authorised
officer appointed by a recorded trustee decision, acting within a written, capped mandate).
**`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** the form of mandate and whether any statutory requirement applies.

### 13. Remuneration, indemnity and insurance

**13.1 Professional trustees.** A Trustee who is a professional or corporate trustee may be
remunerated as set out in **Schedule 7**. If Schedule 7 is blank, no remuneration is payable.

**13.2 Lay trustees.** A family-member Trustee shall not be remunerated for acting as a Trustee, but
may be reimbursed properly incurred expenses on production of receipts.

**13.3 Extraordinary work.** A Trustee may be paid reasonable extra remuneration for extraordinary
services with the Protector's written consent and after disclosure.

**13.4 Indemnity.** Subject to clause 8.5, the Trustees shall be indemnified out of the Trust Fund
against liabilities, costs and expenses properly incurred in the administration of the Trust. **This
indemnity does not extend to liability arising from a Trustee's own fraud, wilful misconduct, gross
negligence or reckless disregard of duty.** `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` the maximum lawful breadth of this
indemnity — and note that incorporation under Cap. 318 does **not** relieve trustees of accountability
for their own acts and defaults.

**13.5 Trustee insurance.** The Trustees may, and shall **if Schedule 3 so requires**, effect
professional-indemnity and trust-asset insurance, and record each policy in the Asset and Liability
Register (`BEYU-LF-D67`) and the Litigation/Claims/Insurance Register (`BEYU-LF-D71`). Contingent
insurance cover shall **not** be reported as an asset of the Trust.

### 14. Conflicts of interest and related-party transactions

**14.1 Duty to disclose.** Every Trustee shall disclose to the other Trustees and (where appointed) the
Protector, promptly and in writing: (a) any direct or indirect interest in any transaction or decision
of the Trust; (b) any other fiduciary duty or office that conflicts; (c) any benefit the Trustee or a
connected person may receive; and (d) any interest of a Beneficiary in the same matter.

**14.2 Register of interests.** The Trustees shall maintain a **Register of Declared Interests** and
update it at least annually; it forms part of the records in Part 7.

**14.3 Recusal.** A conflicted Trustee shall not vote on, or use their position to influence, the
matter, and shall withdraw from the deliberation **unless** the other Trustees (excluding the
conflicted Trustee) resolve in writing that the conflict is immaterial or that the Trustee's
participation is in the best interests of the Trust and a record is made. **A conflicted Trustee must
never form part of the quorum for that matter.**

**14.4 Effect of undisclosed conflict.** A decision taken without the disclosure required by clause
14.1 is **voidable**, and the Trustees shall re-take it after disclosure. `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` whether
and how this clause is enforceable as drafted.

**14.5 Related-party transactions.** The Trust shall not enter into any transaction with a related
party listed in Schedule 6 unless: (a) the disclosure requirements of clauses 14.1–14.3 are met;
(b) an independent Trustee (or, if none, the Protector) provides a written opinion that the terms are
**at least as favourable as an arm's-length transaction**; (c) the transaction is recorded in the
**Related-Party Transaction Register** (`BEYU-LF-D72`); (d) it complies with `BEYU-LF-D51` where the
counterparty is the Foundation; and (e) any required Beneficiary approval in Schedule 4 is obtained.
