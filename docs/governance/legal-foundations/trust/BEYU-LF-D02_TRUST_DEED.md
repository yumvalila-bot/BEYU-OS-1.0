### 2. Recitals (draft — to be settled by the Settlor)

- **A.** The Settlor wishes to establish a trust for the benefit of the persons described in Part 4, to
  preserve and steward family assets across generations, to support the education and development of
  the next generation, and to provide a durable structure through which the family's enterprise may be
  held and governed.
- **B.** The Settlor wishes to record the family's guiding principles — **Trust, Care, Integrity,
  Innovation, Impact** — and the family motto *"Bridging Care. Building Trust."* as the spirit in which
  the Trust is to be administered, while recognising that these are **expressions of intention, not
  legally enforceable obligations**, except where this deed makes them so.
- **C.** The Settlor has settled or intends to settle on the Trustees the property described in
  Schedule 1 (Initial Trust Property).
- **D.** The Trustees have agreed to act as trustees on the terms of this deed.

## PART 2 — SETTLEMENT AND TRUST FUND

### 4. Settlement `[MANDATORY]` `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

**4.1** The Settlor settles on the Trustees the property described in **Schedule 1**, to hold on the
trusts of this deed.

**4.2** The Settlor, or any other person, may add property to the Trust Fund with the consent of the
Trustees, on the terms of this deed, by a written instrument in the form of **Schedule 5(1)**.

**4.3** Until transfer of any property listed in Schedule 1 has been completed, the Trustees hold no
title to that property and must record its status as **PENDING TRANSFER** in the Asset and Liability
Register (`BEYU-LF-D67`). **A listing in Schedule 1 is not a transfer.**

**4.4** The Settlor warrants that, at the date of this deed: (a) they are legally competent to make
this settlement; (b) they are the owner of, or lawfully able to transfer, each item of Initial Trust
Property; (c) no item is subject to any charge, lien, restriction or claim that would be defeated or
breached by the settlement; and (d) they are not insolvent and do not contemplate insolvency.
**`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** the adequacy of these warranties and whether any creditor-protection
consequence follows from a settlement on trust in Tanzanian law.

**4.5 Reverse-transaction / anti-flip warning `[PROPOSED]`.** The Settlor acknowledges that a
settlement made with intent to defraud creditors, or in anticipation of insolvency, may be set aside
under applicable law, and that nothing in this deed is intended to, or shall be construed to, defeat
creditors' rights or any court order.

### 5. The Trust Fund

**5.1** The Trustees shall hold the Trust Fund on the trusts declared in this deed **for the benefit of
the Beneficiaries** (Part 4) subject to the Powers, duties, restrictions and Reserved Matters in Parts
3, 5 and 6.

**5.2** The Trust Fund is **not** available to meet the personal debts or liabilities of the Settlor, of
any Beneficiary, of any Trustee (except as provided in clause 13.4 and to the extent applicable law
requires), or of the Family Council.

**5.3** **No Beneficiary has any right to, interest in, or claim over any specific Asset of the Trust
Fund, and no Beneficiary may call for a transfer of any specific Asset.** `[PROPOSED]` — counsel to
confirm enforceability, and to confirm that this clause does not inadvertently contradict any rule
protecting a person entitled to be maintained.

## PART 3 — POWERS OF THE TRUSTEES

### 6. General powers `[PROPOSED]` (all subject to Part 6 restrictions and applicable law)

The Trustees may, in each case acting unanimously or by majority as Part 6 requires, in the interests
of the Beneficiaries and in good faith, and subject to the prohibitions in clause 8:

**6.1** hold, retain, invest, reinvest, vary, sell, exchange, lease, license, develop, manage, insure,
repair, improve, mortgage, charge, pledge or otherwise deal with any Asset, on such terms as they
think fit;
**6.2** subscribe for, acquire, hold, vote, transfer and otherwise deal with shares, debentures,
partnership interests, units and other securities in any company or undertaking, including the
Holding Body and any Group company, and exercise all rights incidental to them, including appointing
nominees and proxies;
**6.3** borrow or raise money, and secure borrowings by mortgage, charge, pledge, lien or otherwise
over the whole or part of the Trust Fund, **subject to the limits in clause 6.10 and Schedule 3**;
**6.4** establish, maintain, fund and dissolve family entities, trusts, foundations, funds or other
vehicles for purposes consistent with clause 9;
**6.5** make payments, loans or guarantees to or for the benefit of a Beneficiary **only** by way of
Distribution under Part 4 or under a documented family facility created under `BEYU-LF-D05` and
`BEYU-LF-D34`;
**6.6** employ, remunerate and instruct professional advisers (legal, tax, accounting, investment,
custodial, secretarial), and pay their fees from the Trust Fund where properly incurred;
**6.7** open, operate and close bank and custody accounts **in the name of the Trustees** (or, if the
Trust is incorporated, in the corporate name), acting jointly as clause 12.4 requires;
**6.8** institute, defend, compromise, refer to mediation or arbitration, and settle any legal,
arbitration or regulatory proceeding, and comply with any lawful order;
**6.9** pay all taxes, duties, levies, fees, expenses and liabilities properly payable in respect of
the Trust Fund or the administration of the Trust;
**6.10** **borrowing, guarantee and encumbrance limits `[PROPOSED]`** — the Trustees shall not, without
the prior written consent of the Protector (or the Reserved Matter approval in Schedule 4): (a) incur
indebtedness exceeding **`[BORROWING LIMIT — USER TO SUPPLY]`**; (b) grant any guarantee; (c) create
any security over more than **`[SECURITY LIMIT]`** of the value of the Trust Fund; or (d) borrow for
the purpose of making a Distribution to a Beneficiary.
**6.11** do all such other acts as are necessary, incidental or conducive to the management of the
Trust Fund or the furtherance of the purposes of the Trust, save as restricted by this deed or
applicable law.

### 7. Powers of investment, delegation and ancillary powers

**7.1 Investment power.** The Trustees may invest the Trust Fund in any asset class permitted by
`BEYU-LF-D07` and the Investment Mandate (`BEYU-LF-D34`), including but not limited to deposits,
government and corporate securities, listed equities, real property, private undertakings, group
companies, funds and insurance products. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** whether any statutory
prudent-investment standard limits or informs this power in Tanzania — the pack **does not** assert one.

**7.2 Delegation to advisers and managers.** The Trustees may delegate **management**, but not their
**fiduciary discretion**, to a qualified investment manager, custodian or Family Office capability,
under a written agreement complying with `BEYU-LF-D36` and subject to: (a) documented mandate limits;
(b) written reporting at least **[MONTHLY/QUARTERLY]**; (c) express reservation to the Trustees of
every decision listed in clause 7.3; and (d) a right of immediate revocation.

**7.3 Non-delegable decisions.** The following may **not** be delegated to a manager, adviser, officer,
employee, the Family Office, any software system, or any artificial-intelligence system:

(a) the decision to make, or refuse, a Distribution;
(b) the determination of who is within a Beneficiary Class (subject to clause 16.4);
(c) the decision to amend this deed (Part 8);
(d) the decision to appoint or remove a Trustee or the Protector (Part 6);
(e) the decision to lend money to or guarantee the obligations of a Beneficiary or a related party;
(f) the decision to commence or settle litigation;
(g) the decision to approve any transaction with a related party listed in Schedule 6;
(h) the approval of the annual accounts and any response to an audit finding;
(i) the decision to wind up the Trust or distribute the Trust Fund on termination (Part 8).

**7.4 Artificial intelligence — express prohibition `[MANDATORY in the architectural sense]`.** No
artificial-intelligence or automated system, including **NOELIA_AI**, may hold any office under this
Trust, exercise any power under this deed, make any determination of Beneficiary status, make or
approve any Distribution, or be treated as a source of authority for any act. Such a system may be used
only to prepare **advisory analyses and draft documents for human review**, and every output must be
attributed to the human who adopts it. **This clause gives effect to the established BEYU boundary
(`FIR-017`); it is a governance term, not a claim about any statute.**

**7.5 Nominees and joint trustees.** The Trustees may hold any Asset through a nominee or custodian
under a written arrangement, provided the nominee holds only to the Trustees' order and the arrangement
is recorded in the Asset and Liability Register (`BEYU-LF-D67`). Nominees hold no discretion and are
not Trustees.

**8.10** make any Distribution for the purpose of procuring a Beneficiary's consent to any matter, or

**9.1** The Beneficiaries are the persons within the classes listed in **Schedule 2**.

**9.2** The definition of a Beneficiary Class is a matter of **family definition and of law**. Which

**9.3** The Trustees shall maintain a **Beneficiary Register** as an administrative record of the
persons they have determined fall within a Beneficiary Class. **A register entry is evidence of the
Trustees' determination; it is not itself a legal entitlement** (`FIR-009`), and it does not create
rights against the Trust Fund beyond those conferred by this deed and applicable law.

**9.4 Addition and exclusion.** Subject to Schedule 4:
(a) with the Protector's written consent, the Trustees may **add** a Beneficiary Class by deed
supplemental;
(b) the Trustees may **exclude** a person from benefit only on a ground expressly set out in
Schedule 2, only by written decision recording the ground and evidence, and only after giving that
person (if adult and competent) **[NOTICE PERIOD — USER TO SUPPLY]** written notice and an opportunity
to be heard.
**`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** whether any ground of exclusion (including divorce, insolvency or
conviction) is lawful in **[JURISDICTION]**, and whether a hearing requirement is advisable.

**9.5 Minors and persons lacking capacity.** The Trustees shall consider the interests of minors and
persons lacking capacity as a separate category in every material distribution decision. Whether a
guardian's or the court's involvement is required is a legal question for counsel (`Q-S-03`, `Q-S-04`).

**9.6 No automatic benefit on marriage or adoption.** Marriage into the family, adoption, or any other
status change confers no benefit automatically; a person becomes a Beneficiary only if within a
Beneficiary Class as defined in Schedule 2.

### 10. Income, capital and discretionary distributions

**10.1 Discretion.** Subject to clause 10.4 and Schedule 4, the Trustees may pay or apply the income
and/or capital of the Trust Fund to or for the benefit of any one or more Beneficiaries, in such
amounts and at such times as they think fit, and may accumulate income.

**10.2 Distribution purposes.** Distributions may be made for the purposes in Schedule 3, including
maintenance, education, health, housing, a business or professional start, and hardship relief.

**10.3 Trustee duty to record reasons.** Every Distribution decision shall be recorded in a written
trustee decision under clause 15.3, stating: the Beneficiary, the amount or asset, the purpose, the
power relied on, the interests considered, any conflict declared, and the approval path. **Unrecorded
distributions are invalid as against the Trustees' duty to account.**

**10.4 Limits on distributions.** The Trustees shall not make a Distribution that would:
(a) render the Trustees unable to pay the Trust's debts as they fall due;
(b) breach a Reserved Matter (Schedule 4);
(c) benefit a person who is not a Beneficiary (other than a permitted benefit under clause 10.6);
(d) constitute a concealed return of a settlement to the Settlor or the Settlor's estate other than as
permitted by clause 9.4 or Schedule 4;
(e) breach any applicable law or court order.

**10.5 Finance authority.** Every Distribution requires: (a) a trustee decision under clause 15.3; and
(b) processing through the group's accounting authority, with the accounting record held in the
**sole accounting source of truth**. **The Trust's software records, if any, hold references only —
they neither authorise nor account for a payment.** **Distribution authorisation is a human decision;
the accounting system records it.**

**10.6 Incidental and emergency benefits.** The Trustees may apply Assets for the relief of a person
who is not a Beneficiary where that person's need arises from an emergency affecting a Beneficiary, up
to **`[INCIDENTAL LIMIT — USER TO SUPPLY]`**, with a written record and subsequent report to the
Protector. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** whether any such application is lawful and its tax treatment.

**10.7 No capital distribution on demand.** No Beneficiary may demand a capital distribution. Any
capital distribution is at the Trustees' discretion subject to Schedule 4.

**10.8 Nature of the interest. [MANDATORY — definitional; see `TC-01` TC-3.]** No Beneficiary has any
interest in the Trust Fund other than the right to be **considered** by the Trustees within the Beneficiary
Classes and to have that consideration exercised in good faith. **A Beneficiary has no right to any amount,
and a decision to distribute creates an entitlement only to the amount and on the terms decided.** This
sub-clause prevails over any other provision of this deed and over any statement in a family document
(`D11`–`D19`), register or software record.

**10.9 Scope of discretion. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM the outer limits.]** Subject to this deed and the law, the
Trustees may at any time (a) add to capital or income for a Beneficiary; (b) pay in instalments or lump sums;
(c) apply funds **directly for** a Beneficiary's benefit rather than paying them to the Beneficiary;
(d) distribute **in specie**; (e) make **loans** on terms the Trustees decide (with a written record and a
repayment schedule); (f) make a distribution **subject to conditions**; and (g) decide **not** to distribute.

**10.10 Purposes and considerations.** Distributions are made only for purposes within Schedule 3. In
considering a request the Trustees may take into account: age and maturity; education; health and medical
need; disability; debt and financial difficulty; the effect on the Beneficiary's own initiative; the
Beneficiary's other resources as disclosed; the needs of **other** Beneficiaries; family relationships; tax
consequences to the Trust and the Beneficiary; and the Beneficiary's conduct **only** where relevant to a
purpose in Schedule 3.

**10.11 Considerations that must NOT be decisive. [MANDATORY.]** The Trustees shall not treat as decisive:
(a) the fact that a Beneficiary has made a **lawful claim** or brought a **good-faith application**
concerning the Trust; (b) the fact that a Beneficiary has sought information to which they are entitled;
(c) the fact that a Beneficiary has made a **report to a regulator, a tax authority or law enforcement**;
(d) a Beneficiary's refusal to accept an improper condition; or (e) a Beneficiary's decision to obtain
independent advice. **A Trustee who does so acts in breach of this deed.**

**10.12 Impartiality. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM the content of the duty in the chosen law.]** Where two or more
Beneficiaries are eligible, the Trustees shall act **impartially**, which requires (a) informing themselves of
each eligible Beneficiary's relevant circumstances; (b) applying consistent criteria; (c) recording the
reasons for material disparities; and (d) not preferring a Beneficiary because of a Trustee's own interest,
convenience or relationship.

**10.13 Conflicts and self-dealing.** A Trustee who is also a Beneficiary, or connected to one, shall comply
with clause 14; **shall not vote or be counted for the quorum** on a distribution to themselves or to a
connected person; and shall record the recusal.

**10.14 Requests, process and timescales.** (a) A request may be made in writing by an adult Beneficiary, a
guardian, or a person acting under an instrument recognised by law. (b) The Trustees shall respond in writing
within **[NUMBER]** days, stating the decision and the **reasons**, except where clause 19.3 permits
withholding or where the decision is incomplete. (c) Before deciding a distribution above
**[LARGE DISTRIBUTION THRESHOLD]**, the Trustees shall complete the checks in 10.15 and obtain any consent
Schedule 4 requires. (d) A refusal is recorded with reasons, and the Beneficiary is told what evidence would
help a future request where it is proper to do so.

**10.15 Mandatory checks before deciding.** The Trustees shall satisfy themselves, and record, that:
(a) the Beneficiary is within a class (Schedule 2); (b) the purpose is within Schedule 3; (c) applicable caps
are observed; (d) any **Reserved Matter** consent has been obtained; (e) conflicts are identified and managed;
(f) the **affordability and liquidity** tests in 10.16 are met; (g) any **tax** consequence to the Trust and
the Beneficiary has been considered with the Trust's adviser `[ACCOUNTANT TO CONFIRM]`; (h) the accounting and
distribution records (`D08`) can be completed; and (i) the **dual-control and evidence** rules in 10.17 are
met.

**10.16 Affordability, liquidity and investment coordination.** The Trustees shall not make a distribution
that would (a) impair the Trust's ability to meet its existing and reasonably foreseeable obligations;
(b) breach an investment mandate, a covenant or a legal restriction (`D07`); or (c) require a forced sale at
a loss where deferring would not — **unless they record the reasons for doing so notwithstanding**. Where the
Trust Fund is reported alongside other assets, the Trustees shall consider the liquidity discipline in
`D33` §5.

**10.17 Dual control and evidence. [MANDATORY — controls.]**
(a) A distribution requires a Trustee decision under clause 15.3.
(b) Above **[DUAL CONTROL THRESHOLD]**, a **second, independent** authorisation is required — the Protector's
consent, an independent Trustee's written approval, or (where Schedule 4 requires) the consent of a specified
Beneficiary class.
(c) **No distribution may be made, approved, recorded, authorised, executed or paid by any software system,
workflow or artificial-intelligence output.** `BEYU OS` and `NOELIA_AI` may prepare, calculate, record and
report; **they may not decide, approve, sign, execute or release a payment** (clauses 7.3–7.4; `D31` §6).
(d) Every distribution is evidenced by: the request or the Trustees' initiative; the checks in 10.15; the
decision and reasons; any consent; the payment or application record; and the accounting entry in the
**accounting authority of record** (`D08` §1.3).

**10.18 Emergencies.** In an emergency within `D62` E-01, the Trustees (or a Trustee acting alone where this
deed permits) may make a distribution **strictly necessary** for a Beneficiary's health, safety or essential
maintenance, subject to: (a) a cap of **[EMERGENCY DISTRIBUTION CAP]**; (b) immediate notice to the other
Trustees and the Protector (if any); and (c) **ratification at the next meeting, and reversal or correction
if ratification is refused.**

**10.19 Protector and adviser involvement — OPTION ONLY. [COUNSEL TO CONFIRM.]** The Settlor leaves open
(a) whether the Protector must consent to distributions above a threshold; (b) whether an independent
adviser's certificate is required for any class of distribution; and (c) whether any reserved power is
retained. **Until counsel advises, none of these is required except where Schedule 4 expressly says so.**

**10.20 Register discipline.** The distribution record (`D08`) shall show, for each distribution: the
Beneficiary; the amount or property; the purpose; the checks performed; the decision reference; any consent;
the payment evidence; and **whether the interest was discretionary at the time of the decision**.

### 11. Distributions on death of a Beneficiary `[PROPOSED]` `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

**11.1** On the death of a Beneficiary, that person's interest (if any) in the Trust Fund ceases unless
Schedule 2 or a separate written instrument provides otherwise. **No distribution is payable to the
deceased Beneficiary's estate or personal representatives** unless the Trustees, with the Protector's
consent, decide otherwise in a written decision.

**11.2** The Trustees shall not treat any person as a Beneficiary's successor automatically; a
successor must fall within a Beneficiary Class.

**11.3** Nothing in this clause affects **creditors' rights, court orders, or any statutory right of
dependants or family members** that is not capable of being excluded by agreement or instrument.
**`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`** the interaction with Tanzanian succession and family-provision rules.
