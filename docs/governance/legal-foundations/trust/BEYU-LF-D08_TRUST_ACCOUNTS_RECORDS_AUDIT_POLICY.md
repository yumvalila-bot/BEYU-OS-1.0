## 1. Purpose and standing

**1.1 Purpose.** To define the books and records the Trustees must keep, the accounting basis, the audit
and assurance requirements, the documents that must be produced annually, who receives them, what they
must contain, retention, and access.

**1.2 Standing.** A governance and accounting **framework** for adoption by the Trustees. It has no legal
effect unless incorporated into the Trust instrument and adopted by a trustee decision.

**1.3 The single-source rule.** **All money is accounted for in the group's sole accounting authority of
record.** The Trust's software records (including any trust-distribution model or dashboard) hold
**references only**. **No parallel ledger, no spreadsheet-of-record, no software balance may be
maintained as an alternative source of financial truth.**

## 4. Annual financial statements — required content

**4.1** The Trustees shall prepare (or have prepared) annual financial statements comprising:

1. **Statement of the Trustees' responsibilities** (a plain statement that the Trustees are responsible
   for the accounts and for the Trust's compliance).
2. **Statement of assets and liabilities** (or balance sheet) as at the year end.
3. **Statement of receipts and payments / income and expenditure** for the year.
4. **Statement of capital movements** (settlements, capital distributions).
5. **Notes**, including: accounting policies; asset schedule by class with valuation basis; liabilities
   and security; related-party transactions; distributions made (aggregated, and by class where privacy
   permits); tax status and any provision; contingent liabilities (including guarantees and insurance
   cover); material uncertainties; subsequent events; and **any policy breach**.
6. **Comparative prior year** figures, unless it is the first year.
7. **Trustee's declaration** that the accounts are prepared in accordance with §3 and that no material
   matter has been withheld.

**4.2 Prohibited content.** (a) Any figure not derived from the accounting records; (b) any "estimated
net worth" that includes contingent insurance or unrealised valuations without disclosing the basis;
(c) any statement that the Trust "owns" an asset whose title is unverified; (d) any performance claim.

## 5. Audit and assurance

**5.1 Levels of assurance (choose per trust size and legal requirement — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`):**

| Level | When | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal review** | Small trust, no assets with third-party reliance | A written review by a competent person independent of the preparer; recorded conclusions |
| **Independent audit** | Where required by law, by a regulator, by an instrument, by a lender, or where a Beneficiary or the Protector reasonably requires it | Audit opinion on the financial statements in accordance with applicable standards |
| **Regulator-required audit** | Where the incorporation route requires audited accounts on demand or periodically (as read, the regulator may call for audited accounts) | Audited accounts filed/available within the required period |

**5.2 Duty to cooperate.** The Trustees shall give the auditor full access to records and shall not
withhold a document, mislead the auditor, or press for a particular opinion. **Coercion of an auditor is
a breach of fiduciary duty.**

**5.3 Audit findings.** Every finding must be: recorded as an **open item**; assigned an owner and a due
date; remediated; and re-tested. **A finding must never be closed by changing the description of the
item.**

**5.4 Independence.** The auditor may not be a person who: prepared the accounts; is a Trustee, Protector,
Beneficiary, family member, or employee; holds any other engagement that would impair objectivity; or is
connected to a related party. **`[AUDITOR TO BE ENGAGED — NOT YET ENGAGED]`**

**5.5 Appointment.** The auditor is appointed by the Trustees by written decision; the appointment, the
engagement letter, the fee and the basis are recorded in `D72` (related party) where applicable and in
`D73`.

**6.1 Honesty rule.** A report must present adverse information as prominently as favourable information.
A report that omits an overdue filing, a breach, a loss, an unresolved conflict, or a legal uncertainty is
**defective**, and the person who prepared it must be identified.

**6.2 No forward-looking claim as fact.** Forward projections, if included, must be labelled
**`PROJECTION — NOT A FORECAST AND NOT A PROMISE`** and must state assumptions.

**6.3 Disclosure limits.** The Trustees may withhold: (a) another Beneficiary's personal or health data;
(b) information subject to legal privilege; (c) information whose disclosure would expose the Trust or a
person to a real risk (safety, kidnapping, extortion) — with the decision, the ground and the review
date recorded; (d) information restricted by law or a regulator. **A withholding decision requires a
documented reason and at least annual review.** `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` any beneficiary's statutory right
to information (`Q-T-04(e)`), and the data-protection position (`Q-DP-04`).

**6.4 Language and accessibility.** Reports should be written so a non-specialist family member can
understand them; a governance summary should accompany the technical accounts.

## 7. Compliance filings and the obligation calendar

**7.1** Every obligation (statutory, regulator, tax, licence, court) is recorded in `D68` with: the
obligation, the authority, the due date/period, the owner, the evidence of completion, the consequence of
default, and the reviewer. **Evidence of completion = the acknowledgement, receipt or filed copy — not a
calendar tick.**

**7.2** Obligations must be derived from the chosen route and confirmed by counsel. The pack lists **no**
deadline as verified. Where a route requires notification within a period (as read for the incorporation
route: a change of trustees within **one month**; periodic **returns of trustees**; notification of a
**change of trust**; submission of records of **non-resident beneficiaries and beneficial owners**), the
calendar must be populated from the official instrument, not from this pack.

**7.3** **Overdue obligations escalate** to the Trustees and the Protector immediately, and the escalation
is recorded. Non-compliance is never a "back-office matter".

## 8. Retention, access, legal hold and confidentiality

| Record | Retention | Access class |
|---|---|---|
| Executed instruments (originals) | Permanent | `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` |
| Financial statements and audit files | `[PERIOD]` (counsel) | `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` |
| Distribution and beneficiary records | Permanent | `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` |
| Trustee decisions and minutes | Permanent | `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` |
| Tax and filing records | `[PERIOD]` (counsel) | `RESTRICTED` |
| Vendor/adviser records | `[PERIOD]` | `INTERNAL` |

**8.1 Legal hold.** On any actual or reasonably anticipated dispute, investigation, audit or court
proceeding, **all** disposal is suspended and the suspension is recorded with the date and the
authorising decision. **Deleting a record under legal hold is a serious failure, and deliberate deletion
is worse.**

**8.2 Destruction.** Permitted only per a written schedule, by a recorded decision, with a destruction
certificate; never for an original instrument; never for anything under hold; never for a record
evidencing a right.

**8.3 Access.** Granted on a least-privilege basis; reviewed at least annually; revoked on role change or
departure; every access to financial or beneficiary records logged.

**8.4 Confidentiality.** All persons with access sign a confidentiality undertaking. **Trustee
information is not to be discussed with family members who are not Trustees**, and no personal or
financial information may be posted, shared or exported outside the governed registers (`D70`, `D75`).
