## 1. Purpose and scope

To set out, **without recommending an outcome**, the legally plausible routes by which a private
multigenerational family trust could be constituted for BEYU, the trade-offs of each, and the
questions that must be answered before any route is chosen. This memorandum exists so that counsel's
GATE 2 opinion can be a **review of options**, not a request to invent them.

**Out of scope:** choosing a route; drafting the deed (`D02` does that, conditionally); tax advice;
asset-specific transfer mechanics (`D06`).

## 3. Why this memorandum does not simply "use the Trustees' Incorporation Act"

The engagement brief specifically asks whether the **Trustees' Incorporation Act (Cap. 318)** route
is appropriate, and expressly warns against inferring that requirements published for one category of
trusteeship apply to BEYU. The text retrieved (see `LEGAL_SOURCES_REGISTER` §TRUST-1) shows why that
caution is correct:

| Observation from the retrieved Act text | Why it matters to BEYU |
|---|---|
| The Act is framed around trustees appointed by a body/association of persons bound together by **custom, religion, kinship or nationality**, or established for **religious, educational, literary, scientific, social or charitable** purposes, **and** persons holding property **on trust for those purposes**. | A **private family wealth** trust may or may not fall inside this framing. Its purpose is the benefit of a family, which is not obviously one of the listed purposes. **Unresolved.** |
| The Act imposes **compulsory incorporation** on trustees holding property for religious/educational/literary/scientific/social or **charitable** purposes who are not incorporated under any law. | If any element of BEYU's trust is characterised as charitable, the "voluntary" character of incorporation disappears. |
| `s.1A` (as added) places trusts registered under the Act **outside** the definitions of registered NGOs (Cap. 56), companies (Cap. 212) and societies (Cap. 337). | **The forms are mutually exclusive.** A single vehicle cannot be both a "Registered Trustees" body and an NGO or company. This drives the whole multi-entity design. |
| `s.6(2)` requires the name to include **"Registered Trustees"**. | Changes the public identity of the vehicle (bank, land, tax, and brand-facing documentation). |
| `s.15/16` require a sole trustee to be ordinarily resident in Tanzania, and **not fewer than two** of multiple trustees to be ordinarily resident. | Constrains international trustee planning. |
| `s.13/14` preserve trustee **personal accountability** for their own acts, receipts, neglects and defaults **as if incorporation had not occurred**. | Incorporation protects the *trust fund's* continuity, not the trustees from their own defaults. Any indemnity drafting must respect this limit. |
| `s.17` requires submission of records of **non-resident beneficiary and beneficial owner** to the Administrator-General; the application provisions contemplate **disclosure of settlors and beneficiaries** and a **PEP oath/affirmation**. | Directly interacts with confidentiality expectations, with the Companies Act beneficial-ownership regime and with the PDPA. |
| `s.19`–`s.20` (numbering as retrieved) require notification of any **change of trust** and permit the regulator to **call for audited accounts**; `ss.22`–`25` provide for **revocation/suspension**, on which property vests in the Administrator-General as Public Trustee. | Creates a recurring compliance burden and a genuine downside risk that must be weighed. |

**Conclusion drawn (limited):** Cap. 318 is a **serious candidate**, but the pack cannot state that it
applies to a private family trust, cannot state that it is appropriate, and cannot state its current
section numbering or fees. That is `Q-T-02`.

## 4. Candidate routes (presented without recommendation)

For each route: what it is; what it delivers; what it costs; what is unresolved; what it would change
in this pack.

### Route 1 — Unincorporated trust with individual trustees

- **What it is:** a trust constituted by a deed (or declaration of trust), trustees holding legal title
  as individuals.
- **Delivers:** flexibility; no regulator registration for the trust itself; a straightforward
  beneficial-ownership model (trustee is registered owner; beneficiaries are beneficial owners).
- **Costs:** no perpetual succession — every trustee change requires asset re-vesting; difficulty
  holding land and shares cleanly; trustees personally exposed to contract/asset risk; potential
  difficulty for banks and counterparties.
- **Unresolved:** whether Tanzanian law recognises and enforces such a private trust and on what
  basis (`Q-T-01`); whether a deed requires a witness (`Q-R-02`).
- **Effect on the pack:** `D02` as drafted is written to work for this route.

### Route 2 — Incorporated trustees under the Trustees' Incorporation Act (Cap. 318)

- **What it is:** the trustees apply to the Administrator-General/RITA for incorporation as a **body
  corporate** with perpetual succession and a common seal (`s.6(1)/s.8(1)` as retrieved).
- **Delivers:** perpetual succession (trustee changes do not re-vest assets); capacity to hold and
  transfer property and to sue and be sued in the corporate name; a recognised institutional vehicle.
- **Costs:** registration and recurring filings; name must contain "Registered Trustees"; residency
  requirements; facilitator disclosure (settlors, beneficiaries, non-resident beneficiaries and
  beneficial owners); regulator power to call for audited accounts and to revoke; the `s.1A`
  exclusivity (cannot also be an NGO or a company).
- **Unresolved:** applicability to a private family trust (`Q-T-02(a)`); compulsory incorporation
  (`Q-T-02(b)`); current revision/sections/fees (`Q-T-02(c)`, `(g)`); land-holding restrictions
  (`s.6(1)` as retrieved contemplates restrictions on land) (`Q-T-05(e)`).
- **Effect on the pack:** `D01` §5 and `D06` §Vesting are written to support either this route or
  Route 1, and to require the mechanic to be chosen before execution.

### Route 3 — A Tanzanian company as trustee (private trust company), with the trust constituted by a deed

- **What it is:** a Tanzanian company limited by shares or by guarantee acts as trustee; the family
  trust exists as a deed; the company provides perpetual succession and institutional capacity.
- **Delivers:** perpetual succession without the Cap. 318 registration regime; familiar corporate
  governance and filings; ability to appoint professional directors as trustee-directors; easier
  banking and land transactions.
- **Costs:** an additional corporate entity and its compliance; the company must be properly
  capitalised and governed; conflicts and director duties overlay the fiduciary duties; the "trust"
  itself is still an unincorporated arrangement, so the underlying trust-law question (`Q-T-01`)
  remains.
- **Unresolved:** whether a Tanzanian company may act as trustee; whether a trust corporation is
  regulated or must meet capital/other requirements (`Q-T-03(b)`); whether `s.1A` exclusivity creates
  any difficulty (a company acting *as trustee* is not itself "a trust", but the point should be
  checked).
- **Effect on the pack:** `D03` §Eligibility and `D04` §Delegation are written to accommodate a
  corporate trustee, but several clauses are placeheld `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`.

### Route 4 — Foreign-law trust with a foreign trustee (the "offshore tier" option)

- **What it is:** a trust governed by foreign law (the seeded data suggests Mauritius; a DIFC holding
  company is also seeded).
- **Delivers:** a mature trust jurisdiction's settled law and institutional trustees; possible
  tax-neutrality at the trust level in that jurisdiction; familiarity to international advisers.
- **Costs and hard questions:** whether Tanzanian courts/law give effect to it; whether the trustee's
  presence in Tanzania creates a Tanzanian taxable presence; whether the **controlled foreign trust**
  rules attribute income to Tanzanian-resident beneficiaries (`Q-TAX-02`); whether the **PDPC
  cross-border permit** regime applies to the family/beneficiary data the trustee will hold
  (`Q-DP-02`); the beneficial-ownership and possible **exchange-control** consequences of a foreign
  holding structure (`Q-H-02(b)`, `Q-H-02(d)`); reputational and substance considerations; the cost of
  a second jurisdiction's compliance.
- **Unresolved:** all of the above.
- **Effect on the pack:** `D20` §Jurisdiction options addresses this explicitly; nothing in the pack
  presumes it is available or better.

### Route 5 — Foundation-style incorporation for a charitable purpose only

- **What it is:** the charitable vehicle (`D48`/`D49`) may itself take a Cap. 318 route.
- **Why it matters here:** it is the route in which the Act's compulsory-incorporation and exclusivity
  provisions are most clearly engaged, and it demonstrates why the **family** trust and the
  **charitable** vehicle must be separate legal persons with separate assets.
- **Effect on the pack:** `D48` §Options cross-references this memorandum; `D51` keeps the boundary.

## 5. Cross-cutting questions that must be answered before any route is chosen

| # | Question | Why it blocks route selection |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What body of law governs a private trust in Mainland Tanzania? (`Q-T-01`) | Without this, no deed clause can be assessed. |
| 2 | Does Cap. 318 apply to a private family trust — and is incorporation compulsory? (`Q-T-02(a)(b)`) | Determines whether Route 2 is even available. |
| 3 | What is the current consolidated revision and section numbering of Cap. 318? (`Q-T-02(c)`) | Prevents drafting against a superseded text. |
| 4 | What is the current fee schedule? (`Q-T-02(g)`) | Cost basis; the retrieved figures conflict. |
| 5 | May a corporate trustee serve, and must it be Tanzanian? (`Q-T-03(b)`) | Determines Route 3 and 4 viability. |
| 6 | Does the trust hold **land**, and what restrictions apply? (`Q-T-05(e)`) | Some routes restrict land-holding. |
| 7 | What are the **tax** consequences of the chosen route (settlement, year-by-year, distributions, termination)? (`Q-TAX-01`, `Q-TAX-03`) | A trust route cannot be chosen before its tax cost is known. |
| 8 | What are the **cross-border** consequences if any part is offshore? (`Q-TAX-02`, `Q-DP-02`, `Q-LEG-03`) | Determines whether Route 4 is viable at all. |
| 9 | How does the chosen route interact with the **Companies Act beneficial-ownership** regime where the trust holds shares? (`Q-H-05(b)`) | Determines what the group must disclose, and to whom. |
| 10 | Which route best preserves **family confidentiality** within the law? | Affects the family's expectations and must be stated honestly. |
