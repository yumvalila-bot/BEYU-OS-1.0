# BEYU-LF-D58 — Trustee, Director, Key-Person and Executive Succession Procedures

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D58` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — **no successor is identified, briefed or appointed for any role; no succession plan is in force.**
Owner (proposed): Company Secretary function with the Nomination Committee. Approving authority (proposed): the board, the Trustees and the Family Council, each for its own part.
Classification: `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` (this document concerns people and incapacity).
Related: `D03`, `D25`, `D26` (Nomination), `D56`, `D57`, `D60`, `D61`, `D62`, `D66`, `D73`
Depends on: `Q-T-03`, `Q-H-03`; the jurisdiction and structure decisions

> **⚠ THE RULE. A ROLE WITH NO SUCCESSOR IS A SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE — AND IT IS THE CHEAPEST RISK IN THE
> PACK TO FIX.** The test is concrete: **if the person doing this job left today, what happens tomorrow
> morning, and who is authorised to do the things only they can do?**

## 2. The transition procedure (per role)

| Step | Action | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Activate**: confirm the vacancy/absence per the applicable instrument (`D56` for capacity) | Day 0 |
| 2.2 | **Authorise the interim**: the appointing body records a time-boxed interim arrangement | Day 0–`[N]` |
| 2.3 | **Access and mandates**: transfer, rotate and revoke credentials; update bank mandates; update the registers | Within `[N]` hours for access; `[N]` days for mandates |
| 2.4 | **Notify**: insurer, bank, auditors, key counterparties, and — where required — the registry/regulator | Per requirement |
| 2.5 | **Handover**: documented handover of files, matters, obligations and relationships; a handover minute signed by both | Within `[N]` days |
| 2.6 | **Appoint the successor** per the instrument and the matrix (`D27` H-01/H-02) | Within `[N]` days |
| 2.7 | **Induct**: duties statement, conflicts declaration, access, briefing on the obligations calendar | Within `[N]` days |
| 2.8 | **Review**: the Nomination Committee reviews what the transition exposed (`D26` N-05, N-08) | Within `[N]` days |

## 3. Knowledge and access continuity (the part that is never done)

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | **No credential is held by one person only** (systems, domains, registrars, app stores, payment, banking, encryption keys) |
| 3.2 | **Every critical process has a written procedure** (not in someone's head); `D60` defines the critical set |
| 3.3 | **Every key relationship has a second contact** (bank, auditor, counsel, insurer, material customer/supplier) |
| 3.4 | **Documents and their location are registered** (`D74`); the successor can find them |
| 3.5 | **The founder's/mandatory's personal knowledge is recorded** where lawful and appropriate (commercial, not private) |
| 3.6 | **Access reviews** happen at least annually — because a successor is worthless if the systems were never actually handed over |

## 4. The succession checklist for a departure (any reason)

| # | Item | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Resignation/departure/incapacity documented, with the effective date | Secretary |
| 4.2 | The **register** updated (`D66`); the **registry filing** made where required (`D68`) | Secretary |
| 4.3 | **Access revoked** (all systems) and confirmed in writing | CTO |
| 4.4 | **Bank mandates** updated; signatories re-papered | CFO |
| 4.5 | **Confidentiality and IP** obligations reaffirmed; return of material confirmed (`D43`) | Secretary |
| 4.6 | **Knowledge transfer** completed and evidenced | Successor |
| 4.7 | **Insurance** notified where required | Secretary |
| 4.8 | **Conflicts**: post-departure restrictions, if any, noted and diarised | Secretary |
| 4.9 | **The gap analysis**: what was dependent on this person, and what must now change | Nomination Committee |

## 5. Decisions required

1. **Who the successors are** — for every role, named (and asked).
2. The **transition timings** in §2.
3. Whether a **deputy** arrangement is used for the founder's roles (`D56` §5.6).
4. Whether **key-person insurance** is taken, and for whom.
5. Who **owns the succession plan**, and its review cadence.

## 6. Counsel review note — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

1. The **appointment and removal formalities** for each role (`Q-T-03`, `Q-H-03`).
2. Whether an **interim appointment** is valid, and for how long.
3. The **registry/regulator notifications** on each change, and their deadlines (`Q-R-01`).
4. Whether an **acting** appointee has the same authority as the appointee (and any restriction).
5. The **restrictions** on a departing person (confidentiality, IP, non-solicitation).
6. Whether **access revocation** has data-protection implications (personal data held in a personal account).
7. Whether the **trustee** replacement requires a deed, a court step, or a notice.
8. Whether any **successor** requires an eligibility check (disqualification, residency).
