# BEYU-LF-D56 — Founder Incapacity and Emergency Continuity Policy

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D56` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — **no incapacity event has occurred or been assessed; no emergency authority exists; nothing here is in force.**
Owner (proposed): Chief Governance Officer with counsel. Approving authority (proposed): the board, the Trustees and the Family Council, each for its own part.
Classification: `HIGHLY_RESTRICTED` (this document concerns people and incapacity).
Related: `D03` §6 (trustee incapacity), `D08`, `D25`, `D27` (E-01/E-02), `D57`, `D58`, `D61`, `D62`, `D66`, `D73`
Depends on: `Q-S-03`, `Q-S-05`; `DAA-SUC-001`; employment-law input (**not researched**)

> **⚠ THE ONE DISTINCTION THIS POLICY TURNS ON.**
> **INTERNAL interim authority ≠ LEGAL authority.** The family and the group can organise, internally, who
> keeps the office running and who signs the routine things for a limited period. **That organisation cannot
> give anyone the founder's legal powers** — over a bank mandate, a shareholding, a directorship, a trust or a
> signature on a regulated filing. **Legal authority comes from an instrument (a power of attorney, a
> co-signatory, a delegation, a court appointment, a trustee provision) — and instruments must be signed and
> valid *while the person still has capacity*.**
> **Consequence: this is the one policy in the pack whose value is entirely in advance of the event. Nothing
> here can be created after capacity is lost.**

## 1. Scope and definitions

| # | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Capacity event** | Death; illness; injury; cognitive impairment; absence; detention; or any circumstance where the founder cannot exercise their offices or powers |
| 1.2 | **Temporary event** | Expected recovery within `[N]` days/weeks |
| 1.3 | **Permanent/indefinite event** | No expected recovery — triggers the succession framework (`D57`, `D58`) |
| 1.4 | **Determination** | Who decides which it is, on what evidence, and how that is reviewed |
| 1.5 | **Interim authority** | The internal, documented, time-boxed arrangement under §4 |

**1.6 Who may determine a capacity event — and who may not.** Two **medical practitioners** (or one, with a
second opinion where requested) provide the clinical position; the **administering body** (the board, the
Trustees, the Family Council as applicable) makes the **governance determination** on that basis, with
counsel; and the determination is **reviewed at `[N]`-day intervals**. **A determination is never made by
software, by an AI output, by a family majority alone, or by a person who stands to gain from it.**

## 3. Immediate actions on a capacity event (the first `[72]` hours)

| # | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Confirm the facts and the clinical position**; obtain the medical opinion | Family contact + CGO |
| 3.2 | **Secure access and continuity**: ensure operations, credentials and payments continue lawfully; **no unilateral mandate changes** | CGO + CFO |
| 3.3 | **Bank, mandates and access**: if the founder was a required signatory, execute the **pre-existing** co-signature arrangement; if there is none, take advice immediately | CFO + counsel |
| 3.4 | **Notify**: the board; the Trustees; the Protector; the insurer; the auditors (where material); and — only as required — the bank and regulators | CGO |
| 3.5 | **Preserve and protect**: confidential records; the founder's personal documents; the founder's own legal instruments (will, powers) | Company Secretary |
| 3.6 | **Do not**: change the ownership of anything; sign anything in the founder's name; access the founder's personal accounts; make distributions; or alter the trust | Everyone |
| 3.7 | **Open the incident record** in `D71`/`D73`, with the actions, the authority for each, and the decisions deferred | CGO |

## 4. The interim arrangement (internal, time-boxed, fail-closed)

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Interim authority is **granted by the body that has the power** (the board for company matters; the Trustees for trust matters) — **not by the family as such** |
| 4.2 | It is **written, limited by matter and by value, and time-boxed** to `[EMERGENCY WINDOW]`, renewable only by a fresh recorded decision (`D27` E-02) |
| 4.3 | It **cannot** exercise a reserved matter, amend an instrument, issue or transfer shares, make a distribution, or change a mandate beyond the express terms |
| 4.4 | **Two signatures** continue to apply; the emergency route does not suspend controls |
| 4.5 | **Every action is recorded** with the authority relied on; the record is reviewed by the Audit and Risk Committee within `[N]` days |
| 4.6 | **Fail-closed:** where the interim authority is exceeded or ambiguous, the act does not proceed |
| 4.7 | Where the event is **permanent or indefinite**, the interim arrangement ends and `D57`/`D58` operate |

## 5. Instruments that must exist **before** the event (the actionable list)

| # | Instrument | Why | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | **A second authorised signatory** on every account and mandate | Without it, the group's money may effectively freeze | Board |
| 5.2 | **Written delegations** for the executive roles (`D27`, `D73`) | Keeps operations lawful during absence | Board |
| 5.3 | **A power of attorney** (if lawful and appropriate, with formalities) | `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` the form and whether it survives incapacity (`Q-S-03`) | Founder + counsel |
| 5.4 | **A will** (and the succession plan) | `D57` | Founder + counsel |
| 5.5 | **Trust instrument provisions** on trustee incapacity and replacement | `D03` §6 | Counsel |
| 5.6 | **A documented deputy** for the founder's business roles, with access and knowledge | Continuity | Founder |
| 5.7 | **Key-person insurance** and the beneficiary/nominee position | Financial continuity | Board |
| 5.8 | **Recorded passwords/credentials in the approved store** (not in a personal vault only the founder can open) | Access continuity | CTO |
| 5.9 | **A crisis contact list** (counsel, auditor, bank, insurer, key executives) | `D62` | CGO |

**5.10 The test.** **If the founder became unreachable tomorrow morning, could the group pay its staff, keep
its systems running, and meet its filings — without breaking a law or a mandate?** Until the answer is yes,
this document is not a policy; it is a warning.

## 6. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

2. The **emergency window** and the persons with interim authority.
3. Whether a **power of attorney** is to be executed, and in what form.
4. The **second signatories** on every account and mandate (a specific, nameable list).
5. The **deputy** for the founder's business roles.
6. Who **notifies the family**, and how.
7. The **key-person insurance** position and the beneficiary.
