## 1. Purpose and method

| # | Provision |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | The register records: the risk, the cause, the consequence, the likelihood, the impact, the mitigation, the owner, the review date and the status |
| 1.2 | **The register is honest.** A risk that is "accepted" by the family is recorded as **accepted**, with the acceptance and the reason — not hidden |
| 1.3 | **Scoring is simple and consistent**: likelihood (rare/likely/almost certain) × impact (low/moderate/major/severe). A scoring matrix that nobody uses is decoration |
| 1.4 | **Every critical risk has a named owner and a dated action** — not a committee |
| 1.5 | **Regulatory and legal risks are not softened.** A missed filing or an unregistered controller is a fact with a penalty, not a "watch item" |
| 1.6 | The register contains **no privileged content**; legal advice is referenced, not reproduced (`D36` §4 S-05) |

## 3. Escalation framework

| Trigger | Escalation | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| A new **critical/severe** risk | Board + Audit and Risk Committee; and the Trustees where Trust assets are exposed | Immediately |
| A **regulatory default** or a missed filing | Board + committee; regulator engagement as advised | Immediately |
| A **fraud or misappropriation** suspicion | Board, committee, auditors, counsel (`D35` §4.3) | Immediately |
| A risk whose mitigation has **lapsed** | Board, with the reason and a dated action | Next meeting (or sooner for severe) |
| A risk the family has **accepted** | Recorded as accepted, with the acceptance, the reason and the date | On acceptance |
| A risk where the **owner is unavailable** | Reassigned with a recorded decision — never left unowned | Immediately |
| Any matter a director or officer believes is being **suppressed** | Direct to the Audit and Risk Committee, and to the Trustees where trust assets are exposed | Immediately |

**3.1 The upward-reporting rule.** **A risk report is never filtered by the person who causes the risk.**
The Audit and Risk Committee must be able to receive a report that contradicts management — and **a person who
raises a genuine risk in good faith must not suffer for it.**

## 5. Decisions required

1. The **risk appetite** statement — what the family will accept, and what it will not.
2. The **owners** for each critical risk.
3. The **review cadence** and who challenges the register.
4. Whether the group engages **external** assurance on any risk area.
5. The **acceptance** process: who may accept a severe risk (the pack's position: **only the board, with the
   Trustees where trust assets are exposed, and with the reason recorded**).

## 6. Counsel review note — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

1. The **penalties and cure possibilities** for each regulatory exposure (PDPA, filings, NGO compliance).
2. Whether any current exposure requires **voluntary disclosure**.
3. Whether the group's **insurance** program responds to the register's principal risks.
4. Whether **directors' personal liability** attaches to any unmitigated item.
5. Whether any risk requires **notification** to a counterparty, bank or regulator.

7. Whether the register itself could be **discoverable** in a dispute, and how to record advice safely.
8. Whether the family's **acceptance** of a severe risk should be documented in a particular form.
