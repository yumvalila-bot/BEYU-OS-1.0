## 0. Verification discipline and its limits

1. **Verification status** (`README.md` §3.3):
   - `VERIFIED_PRIMARY` — the official statute/regulator text was retrieved and read in this session.
   - `VERIFIED_SECONDARY` — only professional commentary or an aggregator could be retrieved.
   - `REQUIRES VERIFICATION` — the source was not retrieved; **no content is asserted**.
2. **No provision number, fee, deadline, rate or term is stated as settled law** unless it appears
   below with a retrieved primary source. Where sources conflict, **both are shown and the conflict is
   left open**.
3. **Environment limitation (material to the reader's confidence).** The drafting sandbox had outbound
   network access only to a small allowlist of package/git hosts, so Tanzanian official sites could
   not be fetched directly from the shell. Research was performed with the agent's web
   search/retrieval tooling, which did return official PDF text served from `rita.go.tz`,
   `brela.go.tz`, `tra.go.tz`, `mawasiliano.go.tz` and `nps.go.tz`, and statute texts served by
   `tanzlii.org`. **A retrieval-tool result is not the same as an advocate's check of the current
   consolidated revision.** Every primary entry below therefore also carries
   `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM: revision + amendment status]`.
4. **Revised Edition years matter.** Tanzanian statute citations frequently change section numbering
   between the R.E. 2002, R.E. 2019 and R.E. 2023 revisions (this register records at least two such
   shifts for the Companies Act and the Trustees' Incorporation Act). A clause drafted against the
   wrong revision is a real risk and is called out in `CROSS_DOCUMENT_CONSISTENCY_REPORT.md`.
5. **Zanzibar is out of scope of this register.** The United Republic comprises Tanzania Mainland and
   Zanzibar with separate legal/registry systems for several of these subjects. All entries below
   concern **Mainland Tanzania** unless stated. If BEYU intends to operate in Zanzibar, separate
   advice is required. `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`
