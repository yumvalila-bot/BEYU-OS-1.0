> **⚠ THE GATE.** **No template here may be executed until `D31` §2 is decided.** If the Family Office is
> not a legal entity, there is **nobody to contract with** — the service must be contracted as part of an
> existing company's functions, or with the individuals concerned (employment or consultancy), or a service
> entity must first be incorporated. **A service agreement with an unincorporated team is enforceable only
> against the individuals who signed it, and that is almost never what the family intends.**

## 2. Common clauses required in every template (`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` each)

| # | Clause | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parties and capacity** | Correct legal names; for a Trust, the Trustees "in their capacity as trustees" |

| 3 | **Authority limits** | The mandate limits (`D34` §3 clause 2); the suspension-on-breach rule (`D34` §7.3) |
| 4 | **Exclusions (mandatory)** | **No authority to decide; no authority to bind the client to a transaction; no custody of assets; no related-party approval; no payment without a recorded decision** |

## 3. Template A-1 — Family Office Services Agreement (skeleton)

```
FAMILY OFFICE SERVICES AGREEMENT | Ref: [REF] | Date: [DATE]
BETWEEN: [SERVICE ENTITY NAME], of [ADDRESS] (the "Service Provider")
AND:     [CLIENT BODY] — being [the Trustees of the Trust / the board of [COMPANY] on behalf of [COMPANY] /
          the Foundation's governing body] (the "Client")
RECITALS: A. The Client wishes to engage the Service Provider to provide the services in Schedule 1.
          B. The Client's governing body has approved this engagement by a decision dated [DATE] (ref [ ]),
             and (where applicable) the related-party process under D72 has been completed (ref [ ]).

1. SCOPE: Schedule 1 sets out the services, each classified PREPARE / ADMINISTER / EXECUTE, with its limits.
2. AUTHORITY: The Service Provider acts only within Schedule 1 and the limits stated. It has NO authority to:
   (a) make any decision for the Client; (b) bind the Client to any transaction; (c) hold custody of any asset
   of the Client; (d) approve a related-party transaction; (e) make any payment unless the requirement in
   clause 3 is met.
3. PAYMENTS: The Service Provider may execute a payment only where (a) the Client's recorded decision exists,
   (b) the payment is within the limit in Schedule 1, (c) two authorised signatories approve, and (d) the
   payment is recorded in the Client's accounting authority. Any breach suspends the Service Provider's
   authority immediately (clause 9.3).
4. STANDARD: The Service Provider will perform with the skill and care reasonably expected of a professional
   office, will reconcile every financial figure it reports to the Client's accounting authority or label it
   UNRECONCILED, and will escalate every breach, overdue obligation and exception immediately in writing.
5. FEES: [basis and amount] payable [terms]; no commission, rebate or benefit from any third party; fees
   reviewable annually.
6. CONFLICTS: The Service Provider will disclose any interest (including family relationships) and will not
   act where conflicted.
7. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION: [per Schedule 2, the D37 §2 clauses 8–9]; no client data in any AI
   service without the Client's prior written authority.
8. RECORDS: The Client owns the records; the Service Provider holds them as administrator, gives access on
   request, and returns them on termination within [NUMBER] days with a reconciliation.
9. TERM/TERMINATION: [term]; terminable on [notice]; immediately terminable on a breach of clause 2 or 3.
   On termination the Service Provider stops acting, hands over, and takes only steps necessary to preserve
   the Client's assets.
10. LIABILITY: [per advice]; nothing excludes liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.
11. AI: The Service Provider will not permit any AI system to act for the Client, to make or be recorded as
    making a decision, or to be identified as the Service Provider.
12. GOVERNING LAW/FORUM: [ ]
SCHEDULES: 1 Services and limits | 2 Data protection terms | 3 Fee schedule | 4 Authorised signatories
SIGNED: [Service Provider] ______  [Client] ______  [WITNESS/SEAL per Q-R-02 — NOT COMPLETED]
```

## 5. Template A-6 — Foundation services agreement (higher requirements)

**Additional, mandatory clauses (beyond §2).** (a) A statement that the services serve the **Foundation's
charitable purposes** and are **necessary** for them; (b) an express confirmation that the charge is **no less
favourable to the Foundation than an arm's-length charge** (with the comparison recorded); (c) the
Foundation's **independent governing-body decision** reference (`D49`/`D51`); (d) a **purpose-lock** clause:
the agreement must not facilitate a private benefit to a family member or to a group company; (e) a
provision that the agreement terminates if the Foundation's governing body determines it no longer serves
its purposes; and (f) a statement that this is **not** a grant and that any grant is a separate decision
under `D52`. **`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` — but note the pack's position: the family must not use a services
agreement to move money out of the Foundation's charitable funds for the family's or the group's benefit.**

## 6. Execution and registration

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | Counsel settles the form and confirms the formalities (`Q-R-02`) |
| 2 | The engaging body approves (with the power cited), and the `D72` process is completed where applicable |
| 3 | **Correct signatories** — the Trustees for a Trust matter; the authorised directors for a company; the Foundation's authorised governor for the Foundation |
| 4 | The agreement is executed (with a witness/seal if required) and the **original** is filed in `D74` |
| 5 | Registered in the adviser/service register and in `D72` if a related party |
| 6 | The **fee schedule** and the **authorised signatories** schedule completed and kept current |
| 7 | Any **data-processing** term implemented in practice (access, security, breach route) |

## 7. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **The legal form** of the Family Office (`D31` §2) — **the gate for every template here**.
2. The **client bodies** that will engage it, and the services each will take.
3. The **fees** and the allocation basis.
4. The **liability** position and whether a cap is sought.
5. Whether a **cap** on the Family Office's authority to execute payments is set, and at what value.
6. Who signs for each party.
