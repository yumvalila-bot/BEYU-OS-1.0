# BEYU-LF-D36 — Adviser Selection, Fees, Conflicts, Confidentiality and Oversight Policy

Document ID: `BEYU-LF-D36` · Version 1.0 (draft) · Date 2026-10-15
Status: **`COMPLETE_DRAFT`** — **no adviser selection process is in force, no fee mandate exists, no conflict has been assessed.**
Owner (proposed): Chief Governance Officer with the Family Office Principal. Approving authority: the board for company advisers; the Trustees for Trust advisers.
Classification: `CONFIDENTIAL`. Related: `D27` (F-15, L-07), `D31`, `D32`, `D34`, `D35`, `D51`, `D71`, `D72`, `D74`. Depends on: `Q-TAX-01`, `Q-G-03`, `Q-IP-04`.

> **⚠ THE ADVISER RULE.** **An adviser advises; the family decides.** And: **a second opinion is not an
> insult, it is a control.** The pack's stance is that on any matter where the family is relying on one
> person's judgement about its own money, its own structure or its own exposure, **the cost of a second view
> is almost always lower than the cost of being wrong once.**

---

## 1. Selection

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Defined scope first** — no engagement without a written scope, a deliverable, a fee basis and a budget |
| 1.2 | **Competence evidence**: relevant experience, references, and (for regulated services) current registration/licence `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` |
| 1.3 | **At least `[N]` proposals** for any engagement above `[ADVISER LIMIT]` (`D27` F-15) — compared on scope and quality, not price alone |
| 1.4 | **No adviser is engaged by a person who will benefit personally** from the engagement without disclosure |
| 1.5 | **Engagement letter** in every case (template `D37` A-4), filed in `D74` |
| 1.6 | **Registration** of the adviser and the engagement in the register of advisers |

## 2. Fees

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| 2.1 | Fee basis, budget, invoicing and the review date stated in the engagement letter |
| 2.2 | **No success fee, no commission, no undisclosed referral payment** — an adviser must not be paid twice for the same work, or paid by a counterparty |
| 2.3 | **No "estimate" may become an open account**: material overruns require prior approval |
| 2.4 | **A fee increase requires the engaging body's approval**, not an invoice |
| 2.5 | **Payment** only per `D35` §4, against the engagement letter and a compliant invoice |
| 2.6 | **Fees are reported** in the periodic report (`D33`, `D38`), per body, by adviser |
| 2.7 | **The family is told**, in plain numbers, what the structure costs to run — advisership is a cost of the structure |

## 3. Conflicts

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Disclosure before engagement** and **at the start of every matter**: any interest in a transaction, any relationship with a family member or a connected party, any other client with an opposing interest |
| 3.2 | **The register of advisers and interests** is maintained and reviewed at least annually |
| 3.3 | **An adviser must not act for two sides of the same transaction** where the interests conflict; where a firm does, **the consent must be recorded and the separate teams evidenced** |
| 3.4 | **Where the adviser is a family member, a connected person, or an entity a family member controls**, the `D72` process applies, and — for the Foundation — the `D51` process applies **before** the engagement |
| 3.5 | **The adviser's own commercial interest** (e.g. selling a product) is disclosed and recorded; a recommendation to buy from the adviser is treated as a related-party transaction |
| 3.6 | **Conflicts of the family**: a family member's interest in an adviser (a friend, a relative's firm) is declared and entered in `D66`/`D73` |

## 4. Confidentiality and information handling

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Confidentiality undertaking** signed before any sensitive information is shared; it survives termination |
| 4.2 | **Privileged material** is identified and circulated only to those entitled to it; **circulating privileged advice to a family member, an adviser's competitor or an unapproved AI service may destroy the privilege** (`[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`) |
| 4.3 | **No client data into an AI service** without an approved service, a lawful basis and a recorded decision (`D70` §4). **This applies to the group's own advisers** |
| 4.4 | **Cross-border transfer** of personal data by an adviser requires the analysis in `Q-DP-02` |
| 4.5 | **Document return/protection** on termination; the group's records remain the group's (`D74`) |

## 5. Oversight

| # | Control |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | Every engagement has a **named internal owner** who monitors performance and receives the deliverable |
| 5.2 | **Deliverables are reviewed before they are acted upon** — an adviser's draft is not an instruction, and a recommendation is not a decision |
| 5.3 | **A second opinion is mandatory** for: a related-party transaction; a valuation; a tax position with a material exposure; a counterparty's structure; and any matter where the first adviser has a conflict |
| 5.4 | **Adviser performance** is reviewed at least annually: responsiveness, accuracy, cost, and whether the advice was usable |
| 5.5 | **Termination** is a recorded decision; access is revoked; files are obtained (`D08` §8.3, `D74`) |
| 5.6 | **A pattern of errors or a missed deadline** is documented and acted upon — including replacing the adviser |

## 6. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. The **adviser list** and the internal owner for each relationship.
2. The **number of proposals** required at each value level.
3. Whether **success fees** are ever permitted (the pack's default: **no**).
4. Who may **instruct** a second opinion, and the budget for it.
5. Whether the family will accept **disclosure of adviser relationships** — including friendships.
6. Who receives **privileged advice**.

## 7. Counsel review note — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

1. How **privilege** is protected in the relevant jurisdictions, and what destroys it.
2. Whether an adviser's **conflict** requires a formal consent or a waiver, and its form.
3. The **regulatory status** required for each service (legal, tax, audit, investment, valuation) (`Q-LEG-04`).
4. Whether a **success fee** is lawful for each engagement type.
5. Whether the **Foundation** may engage an adviser connected to the family, and the process required (`Q-G-03`).
6. The **data-protection** obligations of an adviser processing family data (`Q-DP-02`).
7. Whether an adviser may be **paid from Trust assets**, and on what authority.
8. The **liability and insurance** expectations for a material engagement.
