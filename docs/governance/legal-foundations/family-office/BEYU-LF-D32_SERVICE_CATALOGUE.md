> **⚠ THE TWO RULES OF THIS CATALOGUE.**
> **1. A service in this catalogue is delivered only under a written mandate from a competent body**
> (`D31` §3.1, `D35` §Mandates). **A catalogue entry is not an appointment, not a mandate and not a fee
> approval.**
> **2. Every service is classified by what the Family Office is allowed to *do*:** **PREPARE** (analysis and
> drafts), **ADMINISTER** (records and processes), or **EXECUTE** (act, within a written mandate). **No
> service confers a decision right.** Where a service touches a distribution, a company act, a grant or a
> related-party transaction, the decision belongs to the competent body and is not part of the service.

## 1. Service taxonomy

| Class | Meaning | Default authority of the Family Office |
|---|---|---|
| **S-1 Records and registers** | Maintain the governed registers from source documents | ADMINISTER under mandate |
| **S-2 Reporting and analysis** | Prepare reports, models, packs, valuations requests | PREPARE only |
| **S-3 Governance support** | Prepare agendas, minutes drafts, decision templates; maintain the decision register | ADMINISTER under mandate (minutes are **approved by the body**) |
| **S-4 Transaction support** | Prepare files, due diligence, instructions to advisers, and execute within a written execution mandate | PREPARE, then EXECUTE only within stated limits |
| **S-5 Treasury support** | Bank mandate administration, payment preparation, reconciliation | EXECUTE only under `D35`'s two-signature rule |
| **S-6 Family programme** | Administer the `D16` programme and family-activity budget | ADMINISTER under the Assembly's budget |
| **S-7 Compliance support** | Maintain the obligation calendar; prepare filings; track deadlines | PREPARE + ADMINISTER; **the filing is made by the accountable body/officer** |
| **S-8 Adviser coordination** | Engage, monitor, and manage advisers within `D36` | PREPARE + ADMINISTER + EXECUTE within mandate |
| **S-9 Risk and control** | Run reconciliations, access reviews, exception checks; escalate | PREPARE + report |

## 3. Service levels (SLF) — framework, not commitments

**3.1 No service level in this catalogue is in force.** The parameters are `[USER TO SUPPLY]`. **Until a
mandate sets them, the Family Office may not represent to anyone that a service level exists.**

**3.2 What every service level must specify.** (a) the deliverable; (b) the trigger; (c) the turnaround;
(d) the quality requirement (reconciliation, evidence, source); (e) the escalation route; (f) the exception
process; and (g) **what the Family Office does when it cannot meet the level** (escalate — never conceal).

**3.3 Mandatory non-negotiables (apply to every service, no matter what the SLA says).**

| # | Non-negotiable |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reconciliation: any financial figure must reconcile to the accounting authority or be labelled `UNRECONCILED` |
| 2 | Source: any register entry must cite the signed/registered source document |
| 3 | Separation: no single person initiates, approves and reconciles |
| 4 | Two signatures for any payment or bank movement (`D35`) |
| 5 | No decision taken by the Family Office or by software in place of the competent body |
| 6 | Escalation of every breach, overdue obligation, limit breach and exception — immediately, in writing |
| 7 | Personal data handled only on a lawful basis and in a compliant environment (`D70`) |
| 8 | No asset held in the Family Office's name (`D31` §3.2 L2) |

## 4. Fees, cost allocation and related-party consequences

**4.1 Fee models (choose per service; `[USER TO SUPPLY]`):** (a) an **annual charge** by the Family Office
entity to each mandating body; (b) a **cost-plus/recovery** allocation; (c) an **internal reallocation**
where the Family Office is a function inside one company; (d) **nil** where the family absorbs the cost
personally.

**4.2 Requirements for every model.** A written basis; a written annual amount or rate; approval by the
mandating body; **`D72` registration** where the counterparty is a related party; **transfer-pricing
advice** where the charge is cross-border or between related entities (`Q-TAX-04`); and a review at least
annually against scope.

**4.3 Prohibited fee practices.** (a) A charge with no written basis; (b) a commission or rebate from an
adviser; (c) charging the **Foundation** for services that do not benefit its charitable purpose, or at a
non-arm's-length rate (`D51`, `D53`); (d) charging the Trust without a trustee decision; (e) recovering a
cost from a body that did not incur it; (f) treating the family's personal expenses as a group cost.

**4.4 The Foundation line.** Where the Family Office provides services to the Foundation: the service must be
genuinely needed for the Foundation's purposes; the rate must be **at least as favourable as an arm's-length
rate**; the Foundation's governors must decide (independently); and the arrangement is recorded in `D72`.
**A group service charge must never be a mechanism for extracting value from charitable funds.**

## 5. Records, confidentiality and the control plane

**5.1 Service records.** Each service has a file: the mandate; the deliverables; the evidence; the
escalations; and the fee charged. **The file belongs to the mandating body**; the Family Office holds it as
administrator and returns it on termination (`D31` §7).

**5.2 Confidentiality.** The Family Office is bound by `D14` §4 and `D31` §5.4. **A service to one body does
not entitle the Family Office to disclose that body's information to another** — including to a family
member, to another group company, or to another beneficiary. **Sharing data between services requires either
a lawful basis or the owner's authority** (`D70`).

**5.3 Control-plane position.** The Family Office's permissions, dashboards and workflow approvals convey
**no authority** (`D31` §6.4). The service catalogue must not be implemented in software as a set of
authority grants: **a service being "on" is not a mandate being in force.**

**5.4 AI.** AI may prepare and analyse within a service. AI may not deliver a service that involves a
decision, a payment authorisation, a legal act, or advice to a person who may rely on it. **An AI-generated
deliverable must be reviewed and adopted by a named human before it leaves the Family Office** (`FIR-017`).

## 6. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Which services** the Family Office will provide (a subset of §2), and to whom.
2. The **mandates** (per §1 authority class), with limits.
3. The **service levels** for each mandated service.
4. The **fee model** and the annual amount/rate.
5. Whether the Family Office may **execute** payments and transfers, and within what limits (`D35`).
6. Whether the Family Office **engages advisers directly** or only recommends (`D36`).
7. What the Family Office may tell a **family member who asks** about Trust/company money.
8. The **escalation route** and the escalation contacts.
