> **⚠⚠ THE CLAIM RULE — THE ENTIRE PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT.**
> **EVERY INVESTOR-FACING CLAIM MUST BE EVIDENCED, OR IT MUST NOT BE MADE.**
> There is **no traction, no customer, no revenue, no user, no partner, no contract, no licence, no registered
> IP, no patent, no valuation and no investor interest in this group.** The repository contains **fictional**
> pitch-deck text and **synthetic** metrics in fixtures and seed data — **they are demonstration data and may
> never be migrated into a deck** (`GATE-0_REPOSITORY_AND_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md` §6).
> **Why this matters more than it looks:** an investor deck is a **representation**. In many systems, a false
> statement to an investor can found a claim for **misrepresentation, negligent misstatement or fraud**, and it
> survives into the definitive documents as a **warranty the company must then stand behind**. **The family's
> name and the family's credibility are the assets at stake — and they are worth more than the round.**
> **A deck with an honest gap is investable. A deck with an invented number is not recoverable.**

## 2. Deck structure (the slide set — with the evidence requirement for each)

| # | Slide | Content | **Evidence required for every claim on it** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Cover** | What the company is, in one line; the entity; the date | **A verified entity name** |
| 2.2 | **The problem** | The problem, stated from **evidence** (research, interviews, published data — **cited**), not from assertion | Citations; interview notes (with consent, `PRIV-02`); published sources |
| 2.3 | **The solution / product** | What the product does **today**, and what it does not | A demo, screenshots of the real system, or a live environment. **No roadmap presented as product** |
| 2.4 | **Why now** | The change that makes this possible now | A cited source; **not a hunch** |
| 2.5 | **Market** | Size and structure — **with the method stated** | The source, the date, the calculation. **Never a top-down number with no method** |
| 2.6 | **Business model** | How money is made; pricing; unit economics | **The actual pricing decision**; the model (`FIN-02`); **no invented margin** |
| 2.7 | **Traction** | **Whatever is true — including "pre-revenue".** Signed contracts, pilots, users, LOIs — **each named and evidenced** | **The documents themselves.** A signed contract, a signed pilot agreement, a real user count from a real system. **Never an honorary "partner" or a "pipeline" presented as revenue** |
| 2.8 | **Competition** | The real alternatives, including "do nothing" and "spreadsheets" | Honest competitive research; **no disparagement; no invented competitor weakness** |
| 2.9 | **Team** | Who the people are, **and what they have actually done** | **Verified biographies.** No invented credentials, no unearned titles, no implied employer relationships |
| 2.10 | **Governance and structure** | The entity structure, the family's role, and the decision rights | The **structure decision** (`D20`); **`D21` cannot be presented as complete** |
| 2.11 | **IP and defensibility** | What is owned, what is licensed, what is open source | **`D40`/`IP-TM-01`: zero verified rights. State the position truthfully** |
| 2.12 | **Regulatory position** | What licences/registrations the group has, and which are required | **`CMP-01` §5: none held. Say so, and say what is planned** |
| 2.13 | **Data protection** | How personal data is handled | `PRIV-01`/`PRIV-02` — **an investor will ask, and "we comply" is not an answer** |
| 2.14 | **Financials** | Historical (if any) and forecast, **clearly distinguished** | **Historical = `FIN-04`–`FIN-09` or none. Forecast = `FIN-02`, with the assumptions shown** |
| 2.15 | **The ask** | The amount, the use of funds, the milestones it buys, and the runway | `FIN-02` (the runway calculation and its inputs); **no invented runway** |
| 2.16 | **The risks** | **The real risks, stated by the company before the investor finds them** | An honest list, including the structure, the stage and the compliance gaps. **This slide builds credibility more than any other** |
| 2.17 | **The close** | The next step | `FIN-03` (a term sheet is **not** implied); `NDA-01` |
| 2.18 | **Appendix** | Cap table (`CAP-01`), detailed model (`FIN-02`), the evidence index (§3) | Everything above |

## 3. The claim evidence checklist (the pack's operating rule — attach one per claim)

| # | Claim (as it will appear) | Slide | Class | Evidence held | Reviewer | Verified? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | *[example] "we have a signed pilot with X"* | 2.7 | **VERIFIED FACT** | The signed agreement | CFO/Secretary | ☐ |
| 3.2 | *[example] "the market is TZS N billion"* | 2.5 | **PUBLISHED SOURCE** | The source and the calculation | CFO | ☐ |
| 3.3 | *[example] "we will reach N users by 2027"* | 2.14 | **FORECAST / ASSUMPTION** | The model and the assumption register | CFO, board | ☐ |
| 3.4 | *[example] "our technology is patented"* | 2.11 | **FORBIDDEN CLAIM** | **None — must be deleted or restated as "no registration yet"** | Board | ☐ |

**3.5 The classification labels (use exactly one per claim):**
**`VERIFIED FACT`** — evidenced by a document in the group's custody · **`PUBLISHED SOURCE`** — cited, with the
date · **`FORECAST`** — clearly a projection, with the assumptions shown · **`ILLUSTRATIVE`** — a scenario, labelled
· **`ASSUMPTION`** — labelled and attributed · **`COUNSEL QUESTION` / `ACCOUNTANT QUESTION`** — not yet answered,
so not a claim · **`FORBIDDEN CLAIM`** — no evidence exists and none can be obtained; **it must be deleted.**

**3.6 The three-strike rule.** Any claim that cannot be classified into 3.5's permitted categories, any claim
whose evidence cannot be produced within one business day, and any claim that a reasonable person would read as
stronger than the evidence supports — **is deleted from the deck, without discussion.**

## 4. Forbidden claims (see one? delete it)

| # | Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Invented traction** — users, downloads, revenue, GMV, "growth", a chart with no data behind it | It is a false statement, and it is checked in diligence |
| 4.2 | **An invented or "exploratory" partner, customer or investor** — any name dropped without a signed instrument | The named party may discover it. **This is the single most damaging category** |
| 4.3 | **"In talks with"** / **"soft-circled"** / **"oversubscribed"** without documentation | Implies commitment that does not exist |
| 4.4 | **A valuation** that was not derived from a documented method or a real transaction | The valuation becomes the negotiation's anchor and then the warranty's subject |
| 4.5 | **Registered IP, patents, "proprietary technology" as a legal claim**, without a certificate | Zero rights are verified (`IP-TM-01`) |
| 4.6 | **Licences, certifications, approvals or "fully compliant"** | `CMP-01` §5: none held |
| 4.7 | **Audited, reviewed or "prepared by" a firm** where no engagement exists | Implies assurance that does not exist |
| 4.8 | **Team credentials that are not accurate** — an unearned title, an uncompleted degree, an employer who never employed the person | Misrepresentation, and it is verified in reference checks |
| 4.9 | **Clinical, diagnostic or medical claims** for a health-sector product | "Cure", "diagnose", "clinically proven" — none may be claimed without the evidence and the sector analysis |
| 4.10 | **Financial-service claims** — returns, yields, "risk-free", "guaranteed" | Regulated territory; `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]` |
| 4.11 | **A market size with no method**, or a top-down "1% of a huge market" | It is the first thing a serious investor discounts |
| 4.12 | **"There is no competition"** | Either untrue or evidence of no market |
| 4.13 | **A runway or use-of-funds figure that is not derived from the model** | `FIN-02` — and the derivation must be visible |
| 4.14 | **Anything about the family's wealth, the trust's assets or the family office** | Private information, irrelevant to the raise, and a privacy breach (`PRIV-02`) |
| 4.15 | **A claim that a named person or entity has approved, endorsed or adopted the group's work** without the instrument | Includes a family member, a regulator, an institution or a "partner ecosystem" |

## 5. Presentation rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | **The deck is version-controlled** with a date and a version number on the cover, and the board's approval recorded (`D73`) |
| 5.2 | **Every copy shared externally is a numbered copy** where the disclosures are sensitive, and any NDA position is recorded (`NDA-01` §4.6) |
| 5.3 | **No slide is added, edited or re-used for another entity or market** without re-approval — a repurposed deck becomes a false statement in the second context |
| 5.4 | **The presenter must have the evidence pack** available, including the appendix and the claim checklist |
| 5.5 | **Never present a forecast as history.** The deck must **state, on the page**, which is which |
| 5.6 | **Never present a family member as a "partner", "adviser" or "investor"** unless that is the verified position, and never as a source of funding |
| 5.7 | **Never present the group's software as a legal entity, a trustee, a director or an adviser** (`D31` §6; `DEFINED_TERMS` §2) |
| 5.8 | **Diligence questions are answered from the register** — "let me confirm and come back" is always the right answer to an uncertain question |

## 6. The honest version of the deck today

**What may truthfully be said today (subject to `§1`):**
> **"We are at the formation stage. There is no company, no product in market, no revenue and no customers. What
> exists is [the real assets: the software, the plan, the team's work]. We are deciding the structure, and we
> are not raising money yet. When we are, here is what we will be able to prove."**

**What may NOT be said today:** anything in §4. **A deck that cannot be produced honestly is not a deck — it is
the reason to wait.**

## 7. Decisions required from the user / family / governing bodies

1. **Whether the group intends to raise at all**, and if so, **what** (equity, debt, grant, revenue).
2. **The entity** that raises.
3. Whether the group is prepared to state the stage honestly, including **"pre-revenue"**.
4. The **use of funds** and the milestones the round buys.
5. **Who may present the deck**, and the claim discipline they follow.
6. Whether the family's involvement is presented, and how (or not).
7. The **evidence pack owner** — who can produce a document within a day for any claim.

## 8. Counsel review note — `[COUNSEL TO CONFIRM]`

1. Whether any **statement in the deck** requires a securities disclosure, a prospectus or an exemption
   (`Q-H-02(b)`; `FIN-03` §6).
2. Whether **projections** must carry a cautionary statement, and in what form.
3. Whether **"in talks"**, pipeline and LOI language needs a disclaimer.
4. Whether the deck's **market and technical claims** create any liability distinct from the contract.
5. Whether any **sector rule** restricts what may be said (health claims, financial promotions).
6. Whether **prior investor materials** must be reconciled when the facts change — **and they must**.

**There is no deck, no traction, no customer, no revenue, no partner, no licence, no registered IP, no valuation
and no investor interest. Nothing in this document may be presented to any person as a current pitch. Every
claim must carry evidence or be deleted.**
