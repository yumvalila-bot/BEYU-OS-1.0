> **⚠ THREE WARNINGS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE.**
> **1. There is no transaction.** No investor has been approached, no term has been offered, no negotiation has
> occurred, and no valuation exists. **Nothing in this document may be shown, summarised or described as an
> offer or as interest from any person.** Presenting it externally as a live deal would be a false statement.
> **2. A term sheet is a negotiation document, not a contract.** Save for the clauses expressly marked
> **BINDING**, it creates **no obligation**. The binding clauses are almost always confidentiality, exclusivity,
> costs and governing law.
> **3. Every number is `[BLANK]`.** Pre-money valuation, investment amount, price per share, post-money
> percentage, option pool, liquidation preference, board seats, protective provisions — **a term sheet with
> invented numbers is a fabricated document.** The only honest term sheet at this stage is a **blank,
> complete framework** — which is what this is.

## 3. Coordination with the corporate documents and the cap table (the consistency work)

| # | Term-sheet term | Must be coordinated with | The failure if it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Liquidation preference and dividends | `D22` class rights; the Articles | An investor holds a right the constitution does not create |
| 3.2 | Anti-dilution | `D24` §5; `D22` §6 | A future issue breaches a right the register cannot implement |
| 3.3 | Protective provisions / veto | `D23` §4; **`D27` §5 reserved matters** | Two inconsistent veto lists, one of which nobody notices |
| 3.4 | Board seats and composition | `D25`, `D27` H-01, `D23` §4 | An investor nominates a director the Articles cannot seat |
| 3.5 | Pre-emption | `D23` §5; `D22` §6; the Articles | A statutory pre-emption right is accidentally waived |
| 3.6 | Drag-along | `D22` §7; `D23` §5 | A drag that cannot reach a class that did not adopt it |
| 3.7 | Option pool | `ESOP-01` §5; `CAP-01` §2.6 | The price is computed on a different denominator than the pool requires |
| 3.8 | Founder vesting | `FA-01` Schedule D | An investor assumes vesting that was never adopted |
| 3.9 | IP representations | `D39`–`D47`; `IP-TM-01`; `IP-EV-01` | A warranty is given about IP the company does not own |
| 3.10 | Beneficial ownership | `D65`; `CMP-01` | A filing deadline is missed on closing |
| 3.11 | Governing law of the term sheet vs the constitutional documents | `Q-R-02` | An inconsistent dispute regime |
| 3.12 | Taxation of the instrument | `Q-TAX-01`/`Q-TAX-03` | An unexpected tax cost at closing or on conversion |

**3.13 The warranty rule.** **A term sheet's warranties become the definitive agreement's warranties.** Since
`D21` is `BLOCKED`, the cap table is empty and no IP is verified, **the company cannot truthfully warrant its
own capitalisation or its IP ownership today** — which is itself the reason this document stays unissued.
